# Source and novelty audit

Checked 28 November 2026. These are scoped comparisons with primary
literature, not a guarantee of priority or a prediction of journal acceptance.

## Existing results that must be credited

1. **Vacuum input.** The new quotient proof uses the repository's standing
   vacuum Theorem H, smooth-slice theorem, and exact arrangement graph
   construction at base revision `acda6f59a240537f521583630ff9e9fac71e145f`.
   Those inputs have not been independently re-proved in this addition.

2. **Time-dependent three-dimensional inequalities.** Czech and Dong,
   [arXiv:1905.03787](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.03787), prove the
   contraction-certified inequalities in their simply connected HRT
   phase framework. Grado-White, Grimaldi, Headrick, and Hubeny,
   [arXiv:2407.07165](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07165), identify the
   multiply connected issue and extend the argument for cyclic topology.
   A collision figure with passing five-party facets is not by itself a
   first covariant inequality theorem. The present all-party matching
   corollary uses a direct graph representation, and its singular-spacetime
   HRT identification is explicitly left separate.

3. **Two-particle formation and its entropy kernel.** Matschull,
   [gr-qc/9809087](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9809087), constructs collapse
   of two massless particles into a nonrotating BTZ hole. Aref'eva,
   Khramtsov, and Tikhanovskaya,
   [1706.07390](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.07390), already calculate the
   equal-time interval entropies and mutual information. This work uses
   their kernel, factors it, proves its global distributional positivity,
   and replaces a branch-comparison triangle-inequality step by an exact
   calculation from the developed geodesics.

4. **Particle-spacetime data.** Barbot, Bonsante, and Schlenker,
   [1202.5753](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.5753), describe collision data
   with a graph of chiral surface pairs and surgeries. Their ordinary
   spinless massive-particle model has equal chiral cone angles. A single
   fixed pair across arbitrary collisions is not a result of that paper.

5. **Negative-curvature analysis.** Lefeuvre,
   [1805.05155](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.05155), gives the smooth
   asymptotically hyperbolic scattering/Liouville facts used after funnel
   replacement. Bonsante--Schlenker,
   [math/0609116](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0609116), and
   Tan--Wong--Zhang,
   [math/0404226](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0404226), supply the relevant
   small-angle geodesic-realization background. The fixed-ideal-endpoint
   extension and the specific uniform collar estimate are explained in
   the new proof rather than attributed verbatim to a source with different
   boundary conditions.

6. **Extremal BTZ.** Caputa, Jejjala, and Soltanpanahi,
   [1309.7852](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.7852), give the exact extremal
   coordinate map and interval formula. The additions here are the
   quantitative finite-party limit estimate, the explicit peripheral
   invariant-plane obstruction, and the distinction between the limiting
   compact prescription and attained HRT on the unwrapped exterior.

7. **Graph cone and five-party data.** Bao et al.,
   [1505.07839](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.07839), give graph
   characterization and closedness. Hernandez-Cuenca,
   [1903.09148](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.09148), establishes the complete
   five-party cone. The supplied 372 rows come from expansion of the
   author-maintained catalogue, with URL and exact subset/orbit indices
   retained in `data/facets_n5_all372.json`.

## Results derived in this run

The new proof package contains finite-group HRT/graph descent without a new
maximin hypothesis; the all-angle relaxed cone-functional theorem by
funnel replacement; the exact small-angle collar and length preservation;
the positive-measure failure of filled-isotopy Crofton; the static tilted
cone counterexample to the proposed chiral HRT identity; the global
positive collision kernel and branch-existence calculation; and the
quantitative extremal limit.

Targeted searches combining entropy cones with particles, conical defects,
orbifolds, and bilocal quenches did not locate these exact assembled
statements. This is insufficient to label every constituent identity or
argument new to the literature. In particular, the finite-cover idea,
submodularity, Crofton, and temporal-function averaging are standard tools;
the claimed contribution is their stated deduction with the specific
geometric and regulator issues handled.

## Defensible paper scope

The physical matter result can be stated for compatible finite spinless
orbifold quotients. The unrestricted positive-angle cone result is a
mathematical length-functional theorem. The exact collision result is an
all-time interval-kernel and matching-functional theorem, with a concrete
remaining multicurve realization problem. The static tilted-cone example
must be acknowledged before claiming a general chiral-cone HRT formula.

The phrase "black-hole formation never leaves the static cone" would
require the remaining physical collision realization theorem. It is not
an established headline of this package.
