# Exact update to the matter and G5 status

28 November 2026. This is an additive research update, not a replacement of
the standing proof files or their assumptions.

## Matter / G2

**Closed in a new physical subclass:** compatible finite orbifold quotients
of the smooth vacuum theorem class, with ordinary positive-deficit spinless
particles, arbitrary finite boundary party collections, and allowed boundary
Cauchy profiles. The minimizing curves avoid the axes. Smooth uniformly
spacelike homology slices descend in orbifold charts. There is no new
maximin existence assumption.

**Not covered by that subclass:** generic cone angles, intrinsic spinning
helical quotients, and collision vertices. Ordinary spinless particles
have equal chiral cone angles. A time-shift identification has timelike
orbits near its proposed filled axis and requires a different causal setup.

**An explicit proof obstruction:** filling particle marks in the isotopy
classes invalidates the geodesic/minimal-intersection identity at every
angle in \((0,2\pi)\), on a positive-measure family of test geodesics. The
correct distinction is punctured isotopy and filled homology.

**A physical identification is explicitly false:** static conical AdS with
angle \(3\pi/2\), endpoint separations \(11\pi/16\) in space and
\(9\pi/16\) in time, has a regular acausal HRT curve with a Cauchy
homology slice. Its length differs from the relaxed common-class chiral
cone half-sum by \(-\log\sin(5\pi/8)\). Thus importing the vacuum
chiral-cone formula unchanged is not a valid all-angle Theorem H extension.
This is not an entropy-cone counterexample.

**Closed at the mathematical-functional level for arbitrary cone angles:**
the relaxed positive weighted common-class length infimum lies in the full
RT cone, by negatively curved funnel replacement and a limit with fixed
endpoint terms. For angles below pi, a uniform collar lemma preserves all
regular simple geodesic lengths exactly and gives an attained functional
theorem. This removes the Crofton/minimal-position obstruction using a
replacement construction. It does not supply physical spacetime HRT
identification or regular attainment in every large-angle class.

**Two-particle formation:** the exact published interval kernel has a
positive kinematic measure, with all switching contributions controlled.
Its noncrossing-matching functional is in the full RT cone at every time.
The remaining physical statement is simultaneous acausality and homology
admissibility of a minimizing matching in the actual collision geometry.
The numerical facet audit tests that functional and does not prove this
remaining condition.

## G5

- **Closed:** actual HRT for bounded unions of intervals on the unwrapped
  extremal BTZ exterior, using the explicit half-Poincare-patch map and the
  standing disk homology theorem.
- **Closed as a limit prescription:** arbitrary finite interval parties on
  a single compact BTZ exterior, with entropy defined by the nonextremal
  limit. The uniform error is
  \(k\alpha^2P^2/6+P\alpha\) in length units for \(2k\) endpoints.
- **Closed at the geometric-functional level:** common unmarked ordinary
  cusp ends in all chiral metrics, after removable peripheral zero-infimum
  cuts are deleted.
- **False as an attained-geodesic claim:** a genuine hyperbolic-parabolic
  extremal quotient has a positive peripheral translation-length sum but
  no invariant bulk geodesic realizing it. The whole-boundary core cost
  requires a limiting/generalized prescription.
- **Open here:** general multiboundary mixed-parabolic Theorem H and the
  full arbitrary-angle collision extension.

This work supports publishing the finite-orbifold theorem and the exact
collision-kernel result with their stated scopes. It does not support
marking all of G2 or G5 closed.
