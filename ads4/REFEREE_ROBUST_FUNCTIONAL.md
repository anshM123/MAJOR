## Issues

1. **(major, applicability and labels) The certificate does not back the headline AdS number.** At `C₁ = 1` the certified flat
   margin `9/50` gives positivity above `n = (50/9)^{1/2} = 2.357`. The conditional bound uses `n = 2.2`, taken from a [NE]
   leading-Regge scan that uses the kernel at `m ~ 2Λ_s`, where it is not valid (the document's own caveat, §6). With the
   certified-flat threshold the bound becomes `|ĝ₃|²Λ⁸ ≤ 1.06·10⁵` and `√(t₄²+α_T²) ≤ 8.3·10⁻⁴` (`Δ_g = 100`).
   - §0 item 5 and STATUS row 11 should state both numbers and say plainly that `6.4·10⁻⁴` rests on the [NE] threshold, not on Theorem R.
   - The headroom is thin: the last negative point is `2.16Λ` and `n = 2.2`.
2. **(minor–major, reproducibility) The certified object is not committed.**
   - `run_all.sh cert` reads `robust/cache/functionals.json`. That file is git-ignored and is regenerated by a floating-point
     LP (HiGHS), so a fresh full run need not reproduce the M02/M01 in the document.
   - The certificate logs do not record a fingerprint of the functional they certified. Here `functionals.json` (17:27) is
     newer than every certificate log (16:55–17:23); I checked that the current coefficients equal those in §3 and that the
     re-run C and M certificates pass.
   - Fix: commit the exact `x, y, a`, and have `rcert.py` print a hash of them.
3. **(minor) M01 dense-check row (§3 table) is wrong.** See the table above: the true minima are `0.09939`/`0.09972`, and the `+−`
   minimum is at `m ≈ 22`, `J ≈ 168`, not at `m = M`. So the margin is not flat at `0.1`, and the `+−` minimum sits in the
   old near-zero region of `F_{g3}`. "No isolated near-zero" holds for M02 only. The dense grid misses oscillatory minima.
4. **(minor) Mutation count.** The document says 6 failing polynomials; 2 fail.
5. **(minor, labels) Two different component norms.** Theorem R's `N` has 3 parts (`FW = B₄+B₅` merged, `−up²ψ₂` inside `B2`).
   The Regge envelope scans (`ads.py`, as in `ADS4_FUNCTIONAL` statement R) use 4 parts (`|B₂|+|B₃|+|B₄|+|B₅|`, which is
   `≥ N` by `≈ 2au³`). The phrase "the `N_comp` used in the AdS envelopes" is right only for the hybrid criterion (A). Say which norm is meant where.
6. **(minor) Region M table wording.** "`b̂⁴𝔉 ≥ 1.2·10⁴` vs `C_R ≤ 1.2·10⁴…2.5·10⁴`" is garbled. The log has
   `C_R(u≤u₁) ≤ 1.18–1.72·10⁴`, and net lower bounds of `1.34–2.02·10⁴` for `+−`.
7. **(minor, novelty) What the comparison with `F_{g3}` covers.** "At least as good as `F_{g3}` in both constants" (M01:
   `37.5457 < 37.8387`, `−46.9286 < −45.4452`) is arithmetically correct, but only for the `ε → 0` constants. The `Δ_IR` and
   eikonal (Theorem 3(ii)) analysis is not redone for M01/M02. Both are well above the paper's optimum (4.4) `24.9 log − 27.6`
   (SDPB, uncertified), and §0 should say so. The novelty is the certified uniform margin, not a better bound.
