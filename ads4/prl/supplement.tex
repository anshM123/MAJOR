\section{Grades, provenance, layers}

\textbf{Grades.} [P] proved; [certified] computer-assisted proof (every inequality checked in exact
rational or interval arithmetic, on top of written-out lemmas); [D] derived; [D given X] derived from the
inputs X (net grade = weakest of them); [D-sketch] derivation sketched (at least one step asserted from a
standard result without being written out or checked); [DV], [D\,+\,DV], [P\,+\,DV], [D; DV], [P; DV] derived or proved and
checked numerically; [Q] quoted; [NE$\pm$] numerical evidence with an explicit error budget (every model
term carries a $\times3$ safety factor, sign-free, and the conclusion survives it; this is an error
\emph{budget} on model terms, not a bound); [NE] numerical evidence; [NE\,+\,M]
computed inside the near-gap model of Sec.~\ref{sec:pos}; [M] model (an approximation whose accuracy is
itself a hypothesis); [A] assumption; [I] open. \emph{Composites.} ``[X\,+\,Y]'' (e.g.\ [D-sketch\,+\,M],
[Q\,+\,DV]) means X with one ingredient of grade Y, and has net grade the weaker of the two (for ``+\,DV'' the
check adds confidence but does not raise the grade); ``[X; DV]'' is X with a numerical check; ``[D given X]''
is derived from inputs X; ``[D, one step D-sketch]'' is net [D-sketch]. A chain carries the grade of its weakest link, ordered
[P]\,$=$\,[certified]\,$>$\,[DV]\,$>$\,[D]\,$>$\,[D-sketch]\,$>$\,[NE$\pm$]\,$>$\,[NE]\,$>$\,[NE\,+\,M]\,$>$\,[M]\,$>$\,[A]\,$>$\,[I].

\textbf{The Letter's wording (v7, v8).} The Letter's main text carries no grade tags. Its words map to the grades as
follows: ``we prove'' = [P]; ``certified'' = [certified]; ``we derive'' = [D] or [DV]; ``derived and checked numerically'' =
[DV]; ``sketched'', ``we sketch the argument'', ``we argue'' = [D-sketch]; ``with error control'' = [NE$\pm$]; ``computed'',
``measured'' (of a value) = [NE] or [DV]; ``computed inside models'', ``in a model'', ``interpolated'' = [NE\,+\,M] or [M];
``we assume'', ``assumption'' = [A]; ``open'' = [I]. Its assumptions (i) and (ii) are A1$'$ and A2 below; ``the two
conditional theorems'' are Theorems W and L$'$ of Sec.~\ref{sec:largespin}; ``one technical assumption'' (heavy-state
contributions split into two analytic waves decaying at a uniform rate beyond the Regge regime) is (AG$_U$) of
Sec.~\ref{sec:largespin}.D, [A] beyond all orders; ``one integral of the graviton's contribution near the light-cone edge'' is
(EDGE-R) of the same subsection, [M]; ``the other magnitudes \dots\ estimated in models'' are the spinning $O(1/m^4)$ window
coefficients (premise W), the $+-$ helicity transfer, Theorem L$'$'s edge amplitude (EDGE) and its $O(1/m^2)$ coefficients above

AdS mass shift; we sketch the argument'' is row LS4 ([D given (E$'$)], (E$'$) [D-sketch]).

\section{Definitions and published data}
\label{sec:defs}

\subsection{Normalisations and couplings}
$c_T=1$ for a free real scalar; $c_T=32L^2/(\pi G)$ in Einstein gravity, so $8\pi G=256/c_T$
($L=1$). The gap scale is $\Lambda=\sqrt{(\Delta_g-2)(\Delta_g-1)}$, i.e.\ $298.4996$ /
$998.4999$; under A1$'$ it is the spin-4 gap. Heavy states:
$m^2=(\Delta-J-2)(\Delta+J-1)$, $\cosh\beta=1+(2J+1)^2/(2m^2)$, $b=(2J+1)\Lambda/m$; the gap
state of spin $J$ has $m_{\rm gap}^2=(\Delta_g-2)(\Delta_g-1+2J)$. $t_4$ is the $d=3$
Hofman--Maldacena parameter, $\alpha_T$ the parity-odd coefficient; $\tau^2:=t_4^2+\alpha_T^2=57600|\hat
g_3|^2$ in AdS units; the $t_4$ half, $t_4=-240\,{\rm Re}\,\hat g_3$, is [DV] (two independent literature checks), the
$\alpha_T$ half, $\alpha_T=\mp240\,{\rm Im}\,\hat g_3$, is [D] with its sign convention open [I]; only the modulus
$\tau=240|\hat g_3|$ enters, at grade [D]. $\Lambda$ denotes the gap scale throughout, never a cosmological constant. The flat MHV function is
$f(s,u)=8\pi G/(stu)+\dots+g_4+g_5t+\dots$ in $M(1^+2^-3^-4^+)=\langle23\rangle^4[14]^4f$, with
type~II at $g_4M^6/8\pi G=2\zeta(3)$, $M^2=4/\alpha'$. Under A1$'$, $g_4$ is the $R^4$ coefficient with
light spin-0 and spin-2 fields kept in the amplitude, in the minimal light-exchange completion of
Ref.~\cite{CHLPS} (App.~B there); integrating out a light scalar would shift it by $|g_0|^2/m^2$. The
all-plus eight-derivative coefficient $\hat g_4$ (complex) is not bounded. Unit of beyond-Regge
corrections: $c_2:=m^2\times$(relative correction).

\subsection{Cutoff}
\label{sec:cutoff}
$\theta(\nu)=\tfrac12[{\rm erf}((\nu+\nu_c)/a)-{\rm erf}((\nu-\nu_c)/a)]$, even and entire,
bounded on horizontal strips, super-Gaussian decay. Every de-shift weight $\chi$ is replaced by
$\chi\theta$, with $(\nu_c,a)=(\Lambda+100,10)$ ($a$ is the cutoff width here only) for both $k=2$ pieces ($\Omega^{EE}$ and the smeared
$k=2$ piece of $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$, whose weight extends to $\nu\approx\Lambda$) and $(150,6)$ for the
$k=4,5$ pieces of $\Omega^Q$, the $k=2$ weights being the continuum weights (next subsection). On every
\emph{computed} number the cutoff's relative effect is $\lesssim e^{-180}$ ($k=4,5$) and $\lesssim10^{-41}$ ($k=2$) [D\,+\,NE]: these
are evaluated on $\nu\le\Lambda$ (or, for leading-Regge images, against kernels bounded in $\nu$), where $\theta=1$ to within its
leakage. Without the cutoff the interchange cannot be proved under $J_0\le1$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:HK}).
\emph{Beyond $\Lambda$} [DV, scalar proxy; M for $EE$]. Above a heavy state's mass $m\,(\ge\Lambda)$ its exact per-$\nu$ image grows
exponentially ($\ln\Gamma\approx m\,G(\nu/m)$, $G'\approx0.13$--$0.19$ at $\nu=m$, rising to $\pi/2$ only as $\nu/m\to\infty$; for the
$J=0$ gap state at $\Delta_g=1000$ it is $1.6\times10^{16}$ times its $\nu\to0$ value at $\nu=\Lambda+100$). The continuum $k=2$ weights
decay like $e^{-(\pi/2)(\nu-\Lambda)}$ beyond $\Lambda$ and super-Gaussianly beyond $\Lambda+30$, so the tail beyond a state's mass is
$\le2.3\times10^{-5}$ of $\int|\psi|\,d\nu$ on every state tested ($\le4.4\times10^{-7}$ ($\Omega^{EE}$) / $1.6\times10^{-4}$
($\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$) of the state's own proxy action). The published $\Delta_g=1000$ \emph{grid} files, by contrast, run to
$\Lambda+312$ and carry a non-decaying double-precision floor ($|X|\approx10^{-16}$--$10^{-14}$) beyond $\approx\Lambda+10$; read
literally with $\theta(\Lambda+100,10)$, that floor would add to near-gap states with $m-\Lambda\lesssim60$--$100$ a tail of the size
of their action or larger, with a sign set by round-off. This is why the grids are read as zero beyond $\Lambda+10$ (next
subsection). (The earlier statements ``every per-state value is unchanged to $\lesssim10^{-41}$'' and ``no growth issue for the
$k=2$ cutoff'' hold for the computed values and for the continuum-defined functional, not for the literal grid object; equivalently,
the cutoff $(\Lambda+60,6)$, with the same leakage $\tfrac12{\rm erfc}(10)$ at $\Lambda$, would remove the floor.)

\subsection{The functionals}
All pieces are built from CLS-D's per-$\nu$ functional $\Psi_{k,\nu,j'}$ on $\langle TTTT\rangle$
(the spinning functional of Ref.~\cite{CLSD}), in heavy normalisation
$\hat\Psi_\nu=\Psi_{k,\nu}/N_{\rm com}(\nu)$, $N_{\rm com}=2^{11}\pi^2|\Gamma(\tfrac{11}{4}+\tfrac{i\nu}{2})|^4$.

\emph{$\Omega^{EE}$ (item 1).}
\begin{equation}
\Omega^{EE}=\int_0^1\!dp\,\psi_2(p)\,\Lambda^4\hat C'_{EE,\Lambda p}+\int_0^1\!dp\,\psi_3(p)\,\tfrac12\Lambda^6\hat C_{3,\Lambda p}.
\end{equation}
Weights NF02: $\psi_k=(1-p)^5pX_k(p)/\mathcal N_D$, exact rationals, $\mathcal N_D=27.48509817$ (\texttt{k0} in the data
file), normalised by $K_0:=-2\!\int\!\psi_2p^6+2\!\int\!\psi_3p^4=1$ (the flat value of the light coefficient $K$). The $k=2$ part has $j'=0$, unsubtracted
$f=v'-u'$, and the dual (2,4) structure
$EE=[V_{0,42}^2(H_{24}-2V_{2,04}V_{4,02})/(X_{20}X_{40})]^2$. Its smeared form is
${\rm Re}\int d\nu\,X_\theta(\nu)\hat\Psi_{EE,\nu}$, $X_\theta=X\theta$, where $X=r_+Y$ and $Y$ is the
\emph{minimal-norm} solution ($Y-\psi_2$ of least $L^2(d\nu)$ norm; the null space is
two-dimensional) of the generalised Abel equation
\begin{equation}
\mathcal C[r_+Y](t)-i\tanh t\,\mathcal S[r_-Y](t)=\mathcal C[\psi_2](t)/\cosh t,\qquad t\ge0,
\label{eq:abel}
\end{equation}
$\mathcal C[f]=\int_0^\infty f\cos\nu t$, $\mathcal S[f]=\int_0^\infty f\sin\nu t$,
$r_\pm=(R_-\pm R_+)/n$, $n=(|R_-|+|R_+|)n_q/|n_q|$, with $R_\mp$ the EE leading-Regge
coefficients ($h_-=g\,\varrho(\nu+i)$, $h_+=\tilde g\,\varrho(\nu-i)$,
$\varrho(\nu)=32(4\nu^2+1)(4\nu^2+9)/((4\nu^2+25)(4\nu^2+81))$). \emph{Definition (v8).} The $k=2$ weight is the explicit
continuum weight
$\chi=Q(\nu)B(\nu)+\sum_{j\le2}y_j\nu^{2j}(\nu^2+1)\log(\nu^2+1)e^{-\nu^2/2}$, $Q=\nu^2/(\nu^2+\tfrac14)^2$,
$B$ a Gaussian basis ($X=\chi(R_-+R_+)$; file \texttt{k2/k2\_continuum\_D\_}$\Delta_g$\texttt{.json}), times $\theta$; it equals the
exact minimal-norm solution to $\le4\times10^{-7}$ of $\max\psi_2$. The published grid of that solution tabulates it on
$\nu\le\Lambda+10$ and is to be read as \emph{zero} for $\nu>\Lambda+10$, where the file holds only round-off
(Sec.~\ref{sec:cutoff}; truncated copies \texttt{*\_trunc.txt}, Table~\ref{tab:data}). Both give the same $|\hat g_3|^2$ light
coefficient and hence the same bound, and no computed number uses either beyond $\Lambda$. The $k=3$ part is
$\hat C_3={\rm Re}[\Psi_{3,\nu}[o_H\,q]/\hat N_3(\nu)]$, $j'=1$, unsubtracted, not de-shifted.

\emph{$\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$ (item 2, upper).} The flat target Q3$^*$ acts on a flat heavy state as
$\mathcal F=\int\psi_2(2-y)D_h+su\int\psi_3D_h+a(2J(J+1)-c_4+s)u^3-2c_Qu^2$ ($u=m^{-2}$, $y=up^2$,
$D_{++}=P_J(1-2y)$, $D_{+-}=P^{(0,8)}_{J-4}(1-2y)$, $(s,c_4)=(1,1)$, $(-1,41)$), with
$A=2\psi_2'(0)=17.240713$, $\kappa=0.109908$, $a=0.169405$, $c_Q=\tfrac12$ (\texttt{c} in the data file), nominal LP margin $\delta=0.2$.
Q3$^*$ is \texttt{Q3-full-delta0.2} with its weights stated in factored form, $\psi_2=p(1-p)^3X(p)$,
$\psi_3=(1-p)^3Y(p)$, $X,Y$ the exact quotients of the stored coefficients (coefficients change by
$\le1.7\times10^{-8}$; $A$, $\kappa$ unchanged to $10^{-10}$), published as \texttt{q3star\_factored.json} (exact rationals; script
\texttt{make\_q3star.py}, which reproduces the fingerprint $\texttt{80e0834a}\dots\texttt{33f2}$ used by the certificate). This is definitional: the stored binary weights carry the
factor $(1-p)^3$ only to rounding ($\psi_2(1)=1.8\times10^{-11}$), which the backward peak
$\tilde d^J_{44}(-1)=\binom{J+4}{8}$ amplifies until the flat action is negative at $+-$, $m=M$, $J\ge1990$
[NE]; Q3$^*$ has minimum $+0.2000$ there. The true flat minimum of Q3$^*$ is $0.1835$ ($++$, $J\approx3600$,
$m\approx100M$, $b\approx72$) [NE], so the nominal $\delta=0.2$ is not met (Sec.~\ref{sec:pos}). It is
realised by: $\hat C_4^{ds}$ and $\partial\hat C_4^{ds}$ (C4 value and derivative), weights $\chi=Q(\nu)B(\nu)$
with $Q=\nu^2(\nu^2+1)(\nu^2+4)/[(\nu^2+9)^p(4\nu^2+1)(4\nu^2+25)(4\nu^2+(3+2\sqrt2)^2)]$, $p=1$, $3$, and
$B$ a 401-term Gaussian basis (spacing $0.15$, width $0.45$; 60-digit coefficients); $\hat C_5^{ds}$
(C5), weight $c/\hat N_5=Q_5B_5$ with poles at the zeros of the crossed $R_{\mp2}$; and the smeared
$k=2,3$ parts $\int\psi_2^Q\hat C'_{EE}$, $\int\psi_3^Q\hat C_3$, the $k=2$ part de-shifted by the
minimal-norm solution $X^Q$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:abel} with $\psi_2\to\psi_2^Q$, defined, as for $\Omega^{EE}$, by its continuum
weight of the same form (\texttt{k2/k2\_continuum\_Q\_}$\Delta_g$\texttt{.json}) times $\theta(\Lambda+100,10)$; its grid is read as
zero beyond $\Lambda+10$.

\emph{$\Omega^Q_{\rm low}$ (item 2, lower).} $\hat C_4^{ds}$ with the C4-value weight alone:
leading-Regge heavy image $P_{-1/2}(\cosh\beta)>0$, flat image $2(|c^{++}|^2+|c^{+-}|^2)/m^8$, light
side $2\tilde g_4$. Its positivity evidence and the lower end it would give: Sec.~\ref{sec:lower}.

\emph{Positivity.} For a primary $\mathcal O=(\Delta,J)$ the action of $\Omega$ on the blocks of
$\mathcal O$ is a Hermitian matrix $M_\Omega(\mathcal O)$ in the basis of $\langle TT\mathcal O\rangle$
structures: $2\times2$ on $(++,+-)$ for even $J\ge4$, plus a $1\times1$ parity-odd block (OO) for
parity-odd even-$J$ exchange; $1\times1$ ($+-$, parity-odd) for odd $J\ge5$; $1\times1$ ($++$) and
$1\times1$ (OO) for $J=0,2$, where no $+-$ structure exists; spins $1,3$ do not occur. (Pos$_\Omega$):
$M_\Omega(\mathcal O)\succeq0$ for every heavy primary ($\Delta-J\ge\Delta_g$, single- or multi-trace; the statement
refers only to $(\Delta,J)$ and parity, not to $c_T$) and, under A1$'$, for every
light spin-0, 2 single trace at the $k$ where it enters (Sec.~\ref{sec:light}). Values are quoted as
$\lambda_{\min}/N_s$, in units of the state's flat normalisation $N_s$.
\emph{Parity-violating theories.} The block structure above has no entry mixing the parity-even and parity-odd
$\langle TT\mathcal O\rangle$ structures of an indefinite-parity operator. Flat side: every flat functional used
is built from the MHV function $f$ alone, whose heavy side is $\sum_X(|c^{+-}_X|^2F_{+-}+|c^{++}_X|^2F_{++})$ whatever
the parity of $X$, so the certificates cover such states [D]. AdS side: the dual structures ($EE$, $j'=1$ for
$\hat C_3$) are built from parity-even invariants and the integration region is parity-symmetric, so the action
is even in the parity-odd OPE coefficient and mixed even--odd waves integrate to zero; this was shown, and
checked end to end to $2\times10^{-12}$, for the earlier functionals [D; DV], and carries over to $EE$ and to the
de-shifted pieces (the de-shift weights depend on $\nu$ only) [D-sketch]. The overall normalisation of the
parity-odd coupling is [I]; it is not used, because positivity is quoted per block.

\subsection{The light side as one functional, and the collinear set}
\label{sec:arc}
\emph{Definition.} For celestial angles $a\neq b$, the single-ordering swapped form is
$E^+[G](a,b)=\oint_C\oint_C t_1^wt_3^wf_kG$, and the block action is
$\omega_k[\mathcal O](a,b)=E^+[G^s_{\mathcal O}](a,b)+(-1)^kE^+[G^s_{\mathcal O}](b,a)$. The
functional is $\Omega_k[G]=\sum_{\mathcal O\in(12)}p_{\mathcal O}\,\omega_k[\mathcal O]$, the
de-shifted piece $\hat C^{ds}=\int d\nu\,\chi_\theta\int d\varphi_1d\varphi_3\,C_\nu\Omega_k/N_{\rm com}$.
This is CLS-D's and CHMRS's sum-rule object~\cite{CLSD,CHMRS}; heavy blocks enter it one at a time, and the light
side is $\sum_{\rm light}p_{\mathcal O}\omega_k[\mathcal O]$, by linearity the sum over tree terms
$X$ of the term-wise $s$-channel value $\Omega_k[X]$.

\emph{Mellin form.} With
$\prod X_{ij}^{-\delta_{ij}}=(X_{14}X_{23})^{-2}(U/V)^{-\delta_{12}}V^{\delta_{13}}$ and every
$X_{ij}$ on its principal branch on $C$ (Lemma C$'$), the kernel
$\mathcal K(\delta_{12},\delta_{13})=\oint_C\oint_C H\,(U/V)^{-\delta_{12}}V^{\delta_{13}}$ is entire
in $\delta_{12}$ because $|U/V|\le1$ on $C$. Three evaluations of one object: the fixed-angle
integral $D_{\rm LR}$ ($t$-integrals outside), the straight Mellin line, and the term-wise
$s$-channel sum (close $\delta_{12}$ to the left: double poles at $\delta_{12}=-n$, single-trace
poles). Cauchy at fixed $\delta_{13}$:
\begin{equation}
\text{line}-\text{term-wise}=-\oint_{\rm arc}\Gamma(\delta_{12})^2\Gamma(\delta_{14})^2M\,\mathcal K .
\end{equation}
After $1\leftrightarrow3$ (anti)symmetrisation the $D_{\rm LR}$ value of any microcausal term
vanishes as an integrand identity, so $\Omega_k[X]=\mathcal A_k[X]$, the arc. Along the arc $M$
grows like the Regge growth of $X$ and $\mathcal K$ like the corner behaviour of $H$: for Regge
spin $n_X<k$ the integrand is $o(|\delta|^{-1})$ and $\mathcal A_k=0$; at $n_X=k$ it is
$\sim c/\delta_{12}$ with $c\propto$ the top Regge coefficient. This is the position-space form
of CHMRS's light-side formula. [D given C$'$ and Mellin convergence.]

\emph{Checks [DV]} on a spinning family $T'=\sigma\otimes T_{\rm free}$, $G_\sigma$ an exact sum of
AdS$_4$ Witten diagrams: term-wise $=$ whole-function values to $10^{-11}$--$3\times10^{-5}$; a
borderline contact gives a finite light value ($-8.7642\times10^{-3}$ at one configuration)
equal to its top-Regge term to $\le7\times10^{-7}$; a borderline exchange (the analogue of the
graviton pole of $\Omega^{EE}$) matches its top-Regge term to $8\times10^{-8}$. At the $k=4$ borderline
(the $R^4$ analogue) a contact, a crossed exchange and a direct exchange with its single-trace block match
their top-Regge terms to $5\times10^{-7}$, $8\times10^{-8}$ and $1.4\times10^{-6}$; below the borderline
the symmetrised sums vanish to $\le1.3\times10^{-5}$ of scale, as a cancellation between a single-trace
block and its own double traces. At the borderline the fixed-angle integral itself diverges
(joint-corner exponent $-3$), so an earlier reading that the $R^4$ value ``sits at the collinear set or
in the $\nu$ integral'' is withdrawn.

\emph{Collinear set (L6$'$).} $|C_\nu(\varphi_1,\varphi_3)|\le A_{13}^{5/4}\bar K(\Delta\varphi)$,
$\bar K=2\log(1/\Delta\varphi)+8.69+o(1)$, uniformly in $\nu$; a light function growing like
$\Delta\varphi^{-p}$ pairs absolutely iff $p<\tfrac72$ [D]. In the gauge of the functional
($x_2=-e$, $x_4=e$, light-ray points $x_3=t_3z(b)$, $x_1^+=-t_1z(a)$), the collinear variable
$y=2\sin^2(\Delta\varphi/2)$ enters only through $X_{13}=2yt_1t_3$ and the contractions linking legs 1 and 3
($Z_1\cdot Z_3=-y$, $Z_1\cdot P_3=-t_3y$, $Z_3\cdot P_1=t_1y$, $P_1\cdot P_3=-yt_1t_3$), and through an explicit
$y^{-1}$ in the $k=2,4$ subtractions $f_2$, $f_4$ [P]. A spinning contact diagram is a finite sum of
monomials in these invariants (at most one $\epsilon$) times scalar $D$-functions with shifted

\subsection{The de-shift identity in shifted form and the published weights}

\subsection{Light single traces under A1\texorpdfstring{$'$}{'}}
\label{sec:light}
\emph{Which occur.} For identical conserved $T$ in $d=3$ there is no $\langle TT\mathcal O_J\rangle$ structure at
$J=1,3$, so spin-1 and spin-3 single traces (for instance $R$- or flavour currents) never enter
$\langle TTTT\rangle$ at $O(1/c_T)$; this concerns the $\langle TTJ\rangle$ structure, not any current
two-point coefficient. At $J=0,2$ there is one parity-even ($++$) and one parity-odd structure; $+-$ exists only
from $J=4$~\cite{CHLPS}.

\emph{Regge spins.} The functionals see only the helicity-preserving function $f(s,u)$ at fixed $u$ ($EE$
annihilates flips; $\hat C_{4,5}$ are flip-free). With $n_X:=4+$(growth of $f_X$ in $s$): Einstein $2$; $W^3\otimes W^3$
$4$; a light scalar of either parity, coupled through $\phi W^2$ or $\phi W\tilde W$ (two-derivative couplings vanish
on shell), $3$; a massive spin 2, $4$ (its crossed-channel $n=2$ pole is double-flip, annihilated by $EE$ to
$\le5\times10^{-46}$ and absent from $\hat C_{4,5}$); $R^4$ $4$; $g_5$ $5$ [Q~\cite{CHLPS}\,+\,D]. The same count
holds for the top Mellin degree in AdS [D-sketch].

\emph{Drop-out.} By L4, a light exchange with $n<k$ has zero value together with its own double traces: light
$J=0$ at $k=4,5$ and light $J=2$ at $k=5$ drop out [D]; in the $\sigma\otimes T_{\rm free}$ family this is a
cancellation between the single-trace block and its double traces to $\le1.3\times10^{-5}$ of scale [DV]. Light
$J=2$ at $k=4$ is borderline: its arc is fixed by the top coefficient, flat value $12|g_2|^2p^2/m^4$, which is $0$
in the value piece and a favourable $+12a|g_2|^2/m^4$ in the derivative piece. At $k=2,3$ (unsubtracted) there is
no drop-out: a light state contributes its block action, like a heavy state [D-sketch; flat P].

\emph{Signs [P].} With the light-matter sum rules of Ref.~\cite{CHLPS} (code validated against their printed
$F_{g_3}$ exactly), per unit coupling and as exact polynomials in $m^2$ ($\Lambda=1$):
$F_D^{(0)}=11.317027+20.771893m^2$, $F_D^{(2)}=3-6.766265m^2-6.905463m^4+20.771893m^6$ (min $0.448$ at

\section{Per-state positivity: evidence}
\label{sec:pos}

\textbf{How it is computed.} The action of each functional on a block is its flat part plus the leading-Regge
AdS curvature plus beyond-leading-Regge corrections. The flat part is certified (below). The curvature is an
explicit special-function integral, bounded where needed by Lemma C [P]: for real $\nu$, $\beta$,
$|P_{-1/2+i\nu}(\cosh\beta)-J_0(\nu B)|\le(1-{\rm sech}(\beta/2))+\min(2,\nu(B-\beta))$, $B=2\sinh(\beta/2)$, from the
Mehler--Dirichlet formula (worst ratio $0.73$ on a 30-digit check [DV]); since $\nu B=bp$ exactly, it compares the
two functions of the hybrid curvature directly. The corrections are computed from direct numerical evaluations
of the light-ray integrals at their bulk-point saddle (a Laplace series, ``$K_2$ rows''), checked against direct
quasi-Monte-Carlo nodes, and at small $\nu$ from an analytic $1/\Delta$ expansion of the same integrals; per spin
they are assembled along the mass direction by a near-gap interpolation (the ``chain''), with leading Regge plus a
scalar skeleton elsewhere (the ``hybrid model''). For $J=12$, $16$, $21$, $29$ at $\Delta_g=1000$ full direct rows
replace the Laplace series on the state's own row, so no truncation term enters, and odd-$J$ light-cone edges are
integrated by Gauss--Kronrod quadrature of direct nodes, with no interpolation. Values so obtained are [NE\,+\,M]; where every model input carries
an explicit safety factor ($\times3$ on each model term, sign-free) and the conclusion survives, [NE$\pm$]. Such an
error budget is not a bound: only the flat floor and the Lemma-C curvature term are bounds; the beyond-Regge
part of the budget is an estimate with a safety factor.

\section{Large spin: two conditional theorems}
\label{sec:largespin}

This section states what is proved, and under which premises, about $\Omega^{EE}$ at $\Delta_g=1000$ on heavy
states beyond the computed sets. The Letter calls the two results ``the two conditional theorems'' (``theorem 1'' =
Theorem W, ``theorem 2'' = Theorem L$'$ in its Fig.~2). \textbf{Neither is unconditional.} Both rest on one structural
assumption, (AG$'$) of Sec.~\ref{sec:largespin}.D, beyond all orders [A], and on magnitude premises graded [M]; Theorem W
also on a leading-Regge floor that is [NE] at its lower spins. % AGPROOF: (AG') is [A] here; update grades if it is proved
Notation: $\beta$ is the transverse (H$_2$) impact parameter in AdS units, $\sinh(\beta/2)=(J+\tfrac12)/m$,
$b=2\Lambda\sinh(\beta/2)=(2J+1)\Lambda/m$, $\eta=\cosh\beta$, $u=\Lambda^2/m^2$, $x=\nu/m$, $P=P_{-1/2+i\nu}(\eta)$,
$\Omega_k(\beta)=\int_0^1\psi_k(p)P_{-1/2+i\Lambda p}(\cosh\beta)\,dp$, and
$F_{\rm LR}(\beta,u,\sigma)=2\Omega_2(\beta)+u\sigma\Omega_3(\beta)$ ($\sigma=\pm1$ for $++$/$+-$) is the leading-Regge
per-state action. Every analysis of this section is unreviewed; an independent check (Sec.~\ref{sec:repro}) reproduced the
identity of Sec.~\ref{sec:largespin}.A, the constant $D$, Lemma LR$_\infty$ and the algebra of Theorem W.
% v7 sources: PW_IMAGES; INDEP_CHECK2 Sec. 1-4; LRINF_TIGHT Sec. 0-6; P2_CONTRACT Sec. 0-4; P4_WINDOW Sec. 0-7.

\subsection{An exact de-shift identity for the $EE$ kernel}
The $EE$ kernel is an exact finite sum of shifted scalar continuous-spin blocks (Table~\ref{tab:inputs}, LRC$^*$),
$K^{EE}_\nu=N(\nu)r^2\sum_bC_b(x;{\rm pols})v^{b/2}\hat G^1_b(x;\nu)$, with $\nu$-independent $C_b$ that carry all the
polarisation dependence ($r$ the radial variable). \emph{Identity.} For every shift $b$ and all complex $\nu$,
\begin{equation}
2\eta(1+r^2)\,\hat G^1_b(\nu)=\Big(1+\frac{i}{2\nu}\Big)\hat G^0_b(\nu+i)+\Big(1-\frac{i}{2\nu}\Big)\hat G^0_b(\nu-i),
\label{eq:magic}
\end{equation}
with the \emph{same} coefficients for every $b$ (those of CHMRS's magic-block identity at $b=0$~\cite{CHMRS};
$\hat G^{0}_b$ are the corresponding blocks one unit lower in the exchanged dimension). \emph{Grade:} [P] to $O(r^6)$ with
$b$ and the spin both symbolic, so it is an identity in $b$ at each of these orders; [P] to $O(r^8)$ for every integer
$b=-4,\dots,4$ (every residual coefficient is the zero rational function); [DV] to $O(r^{40})$ in 50-digit arithmetic
(relative residual $\le3.7\times10^{-48}$ for $b=-4,\dots,4$, $0.37$, $7$, $10$ and complex $\nu$; the original computation
gives $\le7.9\times10^{-48}$ for $b=0,\dots,4$); [D] at all orders. \emph{Consequence} [P\,+\,DV, given the
representation above]: for every polarisation set and to all orders in $r$, the raw image of \emph{any} input (plane
wave in any gauge, any polynomial or polarisation insertion, any heavy block) is exactly a scalar de-shift,
${\rm raw}(\nu)=R^{\rm sc}_-(\nu)F(\nu+i)+R^{\rm sc}_+(\nu)F(\nu-i)=:\mathcal A_g[F](\nu)$, with $F$ even and analytic for
$|{\rm Im}\,\mu|\le3/2$; end to end against the direct light-ray quadrature: $2\times10^{-7}$--$1.1\times10^{-6}$
with refined quadrature (quadrature-limited; the default quadrature gives up to $7.6\times10^{-3}$ at $\nu=6$). Our weights are built for $\mathcal A=\mathcal A_g\circ c$ with
$c(\mu)=32(4\mu^2+1)(4\mu^2+9)/((4\mu^2+25)(4\mu^2+81))$, and $\mathcal A_g[F]=\mathcal A[F/c]$ is of that form iff
$F(\pm i/2)=0$, a Regge-spin-0 (``monopole'') condition. It holds at leading Regge order, but it is not protected
[P]: radial-weight shifts that $O(1/m^2)$ terms produce give $c_s(i/2)=0.914$ ($|y|^2$) and $0.500$ ($r=|x||y|$).
\emph{Exact cost.} The $k=2$ action of $\mathcal A_g[F]$ is $\int\psi_2F/c+D\,F(i/2)$, with
$D=-9.98332\times10^{-4}$ ($\Delta_g=1000$; $-1.09754\times10^{-2}$ at 300; $\approx-990/\Lambda^2$). The form and the
impact-parameter independence of the monopole term are derived [D]: $1/c=\tfrac1{32}+\tfrac{15/2}{4\mu^2+1}-\tfrac{9/2}{4\mu^2+9}$,
the first and last pieces and $(F-F(i/2))/(4\mu^2+1)$ are analytic in the de-shift strip, so the defect of $F/c$ is
$\tfrac{15}2F(i/2)\,\delta_1$ with $\delta_1$ the defect of $1/(4\nu^2+1)$; only the value of $D=\tfrac{15}{2}\delta_1$ is
numerical [DV] (reproduced to 8 digits independently). The equivalent cost kernel is bounded by $1.0\times10^{-3}$ for
$t\ge0.03$. So the only part of a correction not of the form the weights were built for is one monopole term per state,
with an exact, impact-parameter-independent cost. \emph{For $\langle TTTT\rangle$ at $O(1/m^2)$ the monopole cancels}
(Sec.~\ref{sec:largespin}.C).

\emph{Two lemmas used below.} \emph{Lemma I} [P]: if a de-shifted image is $\int P_{-1/2+i\nu}(\cosh t)\,\sigma(dt)$,
its $k=2$ action is $\int\Omega_2(t)\,\sigma(dt)$; every correction structure therefore has an exact cost. \emph{Congruence}
[P]: if $M(m)=\mathfrak n\,U^\dagger gU$ with $\mathfrak n>0$ and $U$ invertible and $\nu$-independent, then $M\succeq0\Leftrightarrow g\succeq0$:
$\nu$-independent normalisations and coupling-basis maps cannot change positivity, at any size. In the symmetric
frame of Ref.~\cite{CHMRS} the spinning integrand is a sum of $(\Delta,J)$-independent weight-shifting operators
acting on scalar seeds~\cite{CPPR} [Q\,+\,D]. A Weyl reflection,
$T(-x,-y;\Delta,J)=T(x,y;3-\Delta,-1-J)$, which is $m\to-m$ at fixed $\beta$ [D\,+\,DV, to $10^{-40}$], makes the
symmetric-frame integrand even in $1/m$, so $O(1/m)$ effects are of this congruence type.

\subsection{Leading-Regge floor at every impact parameter}
\emph{Lemma LR$_\infty$} [P\,+\,certified]. With $a_1=\psi_k'(0)$, $a_2=\psi_k''(0)$ ($\psi_2$: $188.896$, $-1023.546$; exact
rationals) and the function $M_1$ below, which is positive for all $\beta$ [certified],
\begin{equation}
M_1(\beta)=\int_0^\infty\frac{2\nu P_{-1/2+i\nu}(\cosh\beta)\,d\nu}{e^{2\pi\nu}+1},\qquad
\Omega_k(\beta)=\frac{a_1M_1(\beta)}{\Lambda^2}-\frac{a_2}{2b^3}+E_k(\beta),
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
F_{\rm LR}\ge\big(1-r(\beta)\big)\frac{A_1M_1(\beta)}{\Lambda^2}+\frac{2047.09}{2b^3}>0
\label{eq:lrinf}
\end{equation}
for all $\beta\ge0.37$ (i.e.\ $b\ge0.37211\Lambda=371.6$), all $m\ge\Lambda$ and both helicities, with $A_1=327.149$ and
the certified $r(\beta)$ of Table~\ref{tab:thmL}. The remainder $|E_k|$ is bounded by one-dimensional integrals of
elementary positive functions (Mehler--Dirichlet, an exact closed form of $\int_0^1\psi\cos(px)dp$, and a Hadamard finite
part whose identity is now proved). The small-$x$ constant in that bound is $\int_0^1|\psi_k|$, certified as
$10.386029907129$ ($k=2$) and $12.835622283960$ ($k=3$) by an exact Sturm count and interval arithmetic. (The v5--v6 table,
$r\le0.577$ at $\beta\ge1$, used the cruder rigorous constant $\sum_n|c_n|/(n+1)$ of the polynomial coefficients; with
$\int|\psi_k|$, $r\le0.0162$ at $\beta\ge1$.) Every piece of the bound is monotone in $\beta$, so it is evaluated in
interval arithmetic on cells covering $[0.1,64]$, with certified lower bounds on $M_1$ per cell; for $\beta\ge64$ an
explicit decreasing majorant gives $r\le2.03\times10^{-7}$ [P\,+\,certified]. The weaker statement $F_{\rm LR}>0$ is
certified for $\beta\ge0.245$ ($b\ge245.2$); there the certified floor is only $3$--$30\%$ of the true one, so we do not
use it as a threshold. At $\beta=0.37$ the certified floor is $0.57$ of the floating-point minimum, at $\beta\ge1$ above
$0.97$; the true $r$ is below $10^{-6}$ (independent check), so the bound, not $F_{\rm LR}$, sets the threshold.
\emph{Coverage of the floor} (flat part [certified] plus curvature): an extended Mehler--Dirichlet curvature bound gives a
positive hybrid floor for $b\le119$ at $m/\Lambda\in\{1.3,1.5,2,3,5,10,20\}$ (floor $\ge0.049N_s$ at $b\approx119$) [P\,+\,DV]; the

\subsection{Higher orders: a window lemma and the assumption (AG$'$)}
\emph{Why a window.} Costing the truncated fixed-$\beta$ $1/m$ series over the whole range $0\le\nu\le\Lambda\approx m$ fails
beyond $J\approx80$, but the failure is an artefact: the series is asymptotic and worsens past $x\approx0.3$--$0.4$. On exact
scalar heavy moments (Arb, $J=40,48,64,100$ at the gap) the true remainder beyond the hybrid and beyond $O(1/m^2)$ costs
$\le1.5\times10^{-3}$ of the budget (at $J=100$, $22$ times less than the series' estimate) [DV]. Inside the Regge window
$\nu<\nu_w=3/\beta$ the series converges (successive orders fall by $\le6.5\times10^{-3}$ at all 47 states) and matches the
exact cost to $0.3\%$ [DV].

\emph{Lemma P4W} [P] (Cauchy). Fix a state and an entry $e$; let $G$ be the beyond-hybrid correction (exact minus hybrid,
de-shifted, per unit leading image), $p_w=\nu_w/\Lambda$, and $F_2^{\rm bh}$ the computed $O(1/m^2)$ correction minus the
hybrid's own flat $O(y)$ tower. Then $|{\rm cost}_2(G)|$ is at most the window cost of $F_2^{\rm bh}/m^2+S_4/m^4$ plus
$2\varepsilon_w\int_0^{p_w}|\psi_2|$, plus $4[V(p_w)M_w+V(1)(M_1+A)+V'(1)A+{\rm Top}]$, plus the exact monopole term, where
$V(p_0)=\int_0^T|\psi_2(p_0+iy)|e^{-\kappa y}dy$ and $A$ is the known edge amplitude of the hybrid's curvature waves;
the $k=3$ piece is the same with $u\psi_3$. The $1/m$ series is never used outside the window, and no analyticity is used
inside it. Premises:
(W) on $\nu<\nu_w$, real axis only, $|G-F_2^{\rm bh}/m^2-S_4/m^4|\le\varepsilon_w$, where $S_4$ is the $O(1/m^4)$ beyond-hybrid term
(scalar seed computed; spinning part $=K_{4w}\times$ the window costs of $\{\eta^2\nu^4P,\ \eta\sinh\beta\,\nu^4\partial_\beta P\}$);
(AG$'$) below;
(EDGE) the amplitude of exact $-$ flat at $p=1$ ($\nu=\Lambda$, $x_1=\Lambda/m$) is at most $M_1$; on the top side at most
$M_{\rm top}$ (suppressed by $e^{-25}$).
The boundary amplitude $M_w$ is taken as twice the real-axis sup near $\nu_w$ [D-sketch]. On the exact scalar the lemma's
bound is $4.0$--$7.9\%$ of the budget against a true cost $\lesssim10^{-3}$ [DV].

\section{A conditional remark on \texorpdfstring{$\Delta_g\ge300$}{Delta g >= 300}}
\label{sec:cor}

This was a ``corollary'' in earlier drafts. It is not a result of this work: it is a conditional remark,
recorded to show how the method would extend, and its inputs include an open item and an assumption close to
its own conclusion (see \emph{What it assumes} below).

\textbf{Statement} [D-sketch\,+\,M]. Assume (i) A1$'$, A2 and the inputs of Table~\ref{tab:inputs} at
every $\Delta_g\ge300$; (ii) (Pos) at $\Delta_g=300$ for the functional in question, which at 300
includes the open window of $H_{\rm LJ}(300)$ [I]; (iii) $H_{\rm mono}$: at fixed $(J,m/\Lambda)$ the
beyond-leading-Regge correction (in $c_2$) grows at most like $m$, and in the Regge window it is
bounded uniformly in $\Delta_g$ [M] (at $J=0$: the growth is exactly a $\nu$-flat factor $21.0m$ plus an
$m$-independent remainder [D given RD]\,+\,[NE]); (iv) $H_{\rm cont}$ for states between the 300 and $\Delta_g$
twist edges [NE]; (v) $H_{\rm LJ}(\Delta_g)$ [D-sketch\,+\,M]. Then (Pos) holds at every
$\Delta_g\ge300$, and Table~\ref{tab:dg} gives bounds there.

\textbf{What it assumes.} (a) Input (ii) contains the Regge window of $H_{\rm LJ}(300)$, which is open [I]; so the
remark is at best [I] as it stands. (b) Input (v) assumes the near-gap large-spin hypothesis separately at
\emph{every} $\Delta_g$; that is most of what positivity at $\Delta_g$ requires beyond the monotonicity of (iii),
so the remark transfers little that is not assumed. (c) Its support is a proxy analysis (below), not a
computation at any $\Delta_g$ other than 300 and 1000. The $\Lambda$-forms of Sec.~\ref{sec:lambda} are values of the
implication, and nothing in this paper establishes them as bounds at other gaps.

\section{ABJM consistency check}
Dictionary: $M^2=4/\alpha'$, $L^2/\alpha'=\pi\sqrt{2\lambda}$, so
$\Lambda\leftrightarrow ML=2^{5/4}\pi^{1/2}\lambda^{1/4}\approx4.2156\,\lambda^{1/4}$, at leading order in $1/(ML)$.
\emph{Higher-spin gap.} In the type-IIA 't~Hooft regime ($\lambda\gg1$, $g_s\to0$) the single traces of spin
$J\ge4$ are string states; the lightest is at the first massive level, whose highest spin is 4, with twist
$\approx ML-4$; supergravity Kaluza--Klein modes on $\mathbb{CP}^3$ have spin $\le2$ [D-sketch]. So $\Lambda$ is the
spin-4 gap of Refs.~\cite{CHLPS,CHMRSswamp}, and A1$'$ holds on its own: it constrains only spins $\ge4$, and allows
light spin-0, 2 single traces. \emph{No light single traces in $T\times T$} (a separate statement, needed only
for the scalar condition of item (2a) and to make the rows LS of Table~\ref{tab:inputs} vacuous). At tree level only the
stress-tensor multiplet is exchanged in the stress-tensor-multiplet four-point function: ``the only bulk fields
that contribute a meromorphic piece in the exchange diagrams are those in the stress tensor multiplet''
(Ref.~\cite{CPY}, $\mathcal N=8$), and the IIA tree-level amplitude of Ref.~\cite{BCP} has poles only for that
multiplet, through the $R^4$ order [Q]. Kaluza--Klein modes couple to two zero-mode gravitons at two derivatives
only through $\int\sqrt g\,\chi(y)=0$~\cite{CHLPS}, and the only multiplet member in $T\times T$ is $T$ (the others are
$R$-charged or have spin 1) [D-sketch]. For $\mathcal N=6$ the four-point function of the multiplet's bottom component
fixes only two of five superconformal structures, so the $\langle TTTT\rangle$ statement rests on the bulk
three-point argument [D-sketch]. Hence, at leading order in $1/\lambda$, ABJM-IIA satisfies A1$'$ and A2 (by the spin count alone) and the
condition of item (2a) (by this exchange argument, [D-sketch]).
\emph{What is bounded.} At tree level no Kaluza--Klein state is exchanged between zero modes, so the flat limit of
$\langle TTTT\rangle$ is the ten-dimensional IIA amplitude at zero internal momentum and
$g_4M^6/8\pi G_4=2\zeta(3)=2.404$, $\lambda$-independent~\cite[Sec.~3.8, Eq.~(3.43)]{CHMRSswamp}; Binder, Chester and Pufu~\cite{BCP}
derive this coefficient within ABJ(M) from localisation and the stress-tensor-multiplet four-point function, and
Chester, Pufu and Yin~\cite{CPY} in the M-theory regime (not covered here: no string scale); for the IIA regime
Ref.~\cite{CPY} is cited only for the exchange content, alongside Ref.~\cite{BCP}. $\hat g_3=0$ by
ten-dimensional supersymmetry. \emph{Placement.} $2.404/72.04=3.34\%$, $2.404/92.81=2.59\%$ ($\lambda=2.51\times10^7$ / $3.15\times10^9$ for $\Lambda=ML$; earlier drafts quoted $2.6\times10^7$ / $3.2\times10^9$,
which correspond to $ML=\Delta_g$); with twist$(J=4)\approx ML-4$, $(\Lambda/ML)^6$ shifts these by $\approx-11\%$ / $-3\%$ relative, so they are
meaningful to one digit. Against the ten-dimensional window $[0,3.000]$ (Ref.~\cite{CHMRSswamp}, Sec.~3.8, Eq.~(3.42)), $2\zeta(3)$ is at $80\%$; the small
fraction reflects the four-dimensional infrared logarithm, which is real for this sum rule. \emph{Status.} A
consistency check of a theory inside the assumptions [D-sketch for ``inside''], not evidence for positivity: (Pos)
is not shown for string states in AdS, and at $3\%$ of the upper end the check could fail only grossly. Loops
(finite $g_s$) are outside A2.
