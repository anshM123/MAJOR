\section{Provenance and grades}

All statements are quoted from the project's consolidated documents: the unified statement
(which merges Result D, the cubic bound, and Result Q, the quartic bound), the two result
statements, and six later documents whose conclusions supersede the unified statement where
they conflict. Four of the six are \emph{unreviewed} analyses: the closure of $H_{\rm RS}$ by
a contiguity argument, the reduction of its remaining premise to a cycle-class premise
$P_{\rm cyc}$, an interacting check of the Lemma A mechanism, and the $\Delta_g$ scan. The
other two are \emph{independent reproductions} with fresh code (Sec.~\ref{sec:repro}). Items
resting on unreviewed documents are marked $^\dagger$ below.

\textbf{Grades.} [P] proved; [certified] computer-assisted proof; [D] derived; [D-sketch]
derivation sketched; [DV] derived and checked numerically; [Q] quoted from the literature;
[NE] numerical evidence; [NE + M] computed inside the near-gap chain / hybrid (HYB) model;
[M] model (an approximation whose accuracy is itself a hypothesis); [A] assumption; [I]
open. A chain carries the grade of its weakest link. \HLJstatus

\section{$\Lambda$-forms and error terms}
\label{sec:lambda}

\textbf{Closed forms [D]; constants [DV].} With $a=3/(2\Lambda)$ (the exact graviton pole
$16\pi G/(\nu^2+9/4)$, H7) and $L(\psi;a):=\int_0^1\psi(p)\,dp/(p^2+a^2)$, a polynomial weight
$\psi=\sum_n c_np^n$ ($c_0=0$) integrates term by term,
\begin{equation}
I_0=\frac{\arctan(1/a)}{a},\quad I_1=\tfrac12\ln(1+a^{-2}),\quad I_n=\frac{1}{n-1}-a^2I_{n-2},
\end{equation}
and for $\psi=c_1p+c_2p^2+c_3p^3+R$, $R=O(p^4)$,
\begin{equation}
L=c_1\ln\frac1a+\Big[c_2+\frac{c_3}{2}+\int\frac{R}{p^2}\Big]-\frac{\pi}{2}c_2a
+a^2\Big[\frac{c_1}{2}+c_2-c_3\ln\frac1a-\int\frac{R}{p^4}\Big]+O(a^3).
\end{equation}
Result D ($16L_A=16L(\psi_2^{\rm NF};a)$, $k(0)=1$):
\begin{equation}
16L_A=A\ln\frac{2\Lambda}{3}+B_\infty+\frac{b_1}{\Lambda}+\frac{b_{2L}\ln(2\Lambda/3)+b_2}{\Lambda^2}
+O\Big(\frac{\ln\Lambda}{\Lambda^3}\Big),
\end{equation}
$A=3022.3377$, $B_\infty=-5327.2096$ ($=B_{\rm AdS}+A\ln\tfrac32$, $B_{\rm AdS}=-6552.662$),
$b_1=19293.4$, $b_{2L}=-13549$, $b_2=-14705$; equivalently
$16L_A=A\ln(\Lambda/\Lambda_0^D)+b_1/\Lambda+\dots$ with $\Lambda_0^D=8.7415$. Result Q
($2L_{\rm AdS}=2L(\psi_2^Q;a)$): $A_Q=17.240713$, $B_\infty=-19.278176$, $b_1=10.3137$
($=-(3\pi/2)c_2$), $b_{2L}=860.35$, $b_2=-2508.6$, $\Lambda_0^Q=4.5889$. Hence
\begin{align}
\sqrt{t_4^2+\alpha_T^2}&\le\frac{240\sqrt{16L_A(\Lambda)}}{\Lambda^4}
=\frac{1.3194\times10^4}{\Lambda^4}\sqrt{\ln\frac{\Lambda}{8.7415}+\frac{6.384}{\Lambda}
+O\Big(\frac{\ln\Lambda}{\Lambda^2}\Big)},\\
2L_{\rm AdS}(\Lambda)&=17.2407\ln\frac{2\Lambda}{3}-19.2782+\frac{10.314}{\Lambda}
+\frac{860.3\ln(2\Lambda/3)-2508.6}{\Lambda^2}+O\Big(\frac{\ln\Lambda}{\Lambda^3}\Big).
\end{align}
The leading behaviour is $p=4$ with a $\sqrt{\ln}$ enhancement for the cubic bound, and
$17.24\ln\Lambda-26.27+10.3/\Lambda$ for the quartic upper end.

\textbf{The drift of $B_{\rm eff}$.} $B_{\rm eff}=-19.220$ ($300$) $\to-19.265$ ($1000$) is
the finite-$\Lambda$ tail of the exact AdS pole $1/(p^2+9/4\Lambda^2)$ and converges to the
flat constant $-19.2782$ (the stored $B$ of the flat functional). Of the difference to the
flat value at 300, $0.0450$: $0.0242$ from the $1/\Lambda$ term, $0.0199$ from the
$\Lambda^{-2}\ln\Lambda$ terms, $0.0009$ higher order.

\textbf{Error terms as functions of $\Lambda$.}
\begin{itemize}
\item $c_1$ (Result D): relative shift of the cubic bound per unit $c_1$,
  $\mp1920\int\psi_2/(\Lambda^2\sqrt{16L_A})=\mp19941/(\Lambda^2\sqrt{16L_A})$ [D, linearised].
\item $c_1$ (Result Q): $|\varepsilon_{c_1}|=2\int\psi_2^Q|c_1t_4|\Lambda^2=2.032|c_1t_4|\Lambda^2$;
  with Result D's $t_4$, $\le487.7\sqrt{16L_A}\,|c_1|/\Lambda^2$ [D].
\item H17: $|\delta k_{\rm H17}|=424.97/\Lambda^2$ (heuristic model), i.e.\ a band
  $\pm212.5/\Lambda^2$ on the bound [M].
\item EE curvature correction $\delta k(0)=-68.08/\Lambda^2\,(1+O(\Lambda^{-1}))$ [D] (first-order
  symbol), against the exact $-68.3$ / $-68.1$ at $300$ / $1000$ [DV]; bound factor
  $1+34.0/\Lambda^2$.
\item $\kappa=0.1099$ and $c_L$ are flat numbers plus $O(\Lambda^{-2})$ [D-sketch + M]; the
  lower end $-O(\Lambda^{-2})$ has no computed constant.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Accuracy of the truncated forms} (checked against the exact integrals, and
independently reproduced): the cubic form with the $1/\Lambda$ term has relative error
$\lesssim5\times10^{-5}$ at $\Delta_g=300$ and $1000$ (without it, $-0.30\%$ / $-0.067\%$); the
quartic form has absolute error $-0.058$ / $-0.013$ with the logarithm only, $-0.024$ /
$-0.003$ adding $10.314/\Lambda$, and $\le10^{-3}$ with all terms shown. $\Lambda^4$ must not be
replaced by $\Delta_g^4$: at $\Delta_g=300$ that alone moves the cubic bound by $-2.0\%$.

\textbf{$H_{\rm RS}$ for Q's binding state}$^\dagger$. (i) \emph{Contiguity [D].} To every order
in $a=1/\Delta$ the engine's integrand is $\Psi_\lambda\times$(Laurent polynomial), with
$\Psi_\lambda$ a product of one-variable twisted local systems (four radial, $s$, $\varphi$,
$\alpha$). Integration by parts reduces every monomial to a rational multiple of
$\int\Psi_\lambda$ with cycle-independent coefficients; $s$- and $\varphi$-odd forms are exact
and the $\alpha$-odd class is not populated. Hence $\int_\Gamma=c_\Gamma(\nu)\times$(engine)
order by order, and $c_\Gamma$ cancels in the state/reference ratio $\rho(\nu)$. The hypotheses
(same local system as the scalar, no interior poles, no integer exponents, no boundary layers)
are checked on the engine's pieces to $a^4$--$a^5$ [DV]. (ii) \emph{Far region.} The EE
integrand equals $a\,Q\times$(scalar integrand) exactly, with $Q$ single-valued rational and every
pole of $Q$ already singular for the scalar [P + DV]. On the real section cut at the zeros of
$B$ ($D'$) the far region contributes $O(\Delta^Ne^{-c\Delta})$, $c=2\,{\rm artanh}(r/2)$
($\ge0.336$ at the guaranteed split $r=1/3$) [D]; integrated checks give fitted exponents within
$3\%$ of the analytic value for EE and scalar [DV]. (iii) \emph{What remains}: $P_{\rm cyc}$, the
statement that the physical cycle lies in the twisted-homology class of $D'$. It is the same
premise for scalar and EE, and is [DV] for the scalar through its periods (engine = exact
Mellin skeleton to $\le6\times10^{-9}$ at $\Delta=300$, $\le1\times10^{-9}$ at 1000). Net:
$H_{\rm RS}$ [D given $P_{\rm cyc}$]. Error budget: truncation $|\Delta s_d|\le0.015$ /

which the EE integrand does not decay, which plausibly explains an earlier loss of signal in
direct crystal checks below $\nu\approx2$ [D-sketch]; the real-angle cycle $D'$ is admissible.

\section{The $\Delta_g\ge300$ corollary}
\label{sec:cor}

\textbf{Statement}$^\dagger$. Assume, in addition to the hypotheses of the theorem at
$\Delta_g=300$, $H_{\rm mono}$ (Table~\ref{tab:pos}) and $H_{\rm cont}$ for states between the
300 and $\Delta_g$ twist edges. Then positivity holds, and the bounds of
Sec.~\ref{sec:lambda} with their error terms apply, for every $\Delta_g\ge300$ [D-sketch given
$H_{\rm mono}$ + $H_{\rm cont}$ ([M]) + $H_{\rm LJ}(\Delta_g)$]. The corollary inherits
\HLJcorollary. It is not a proof: $\Gamma$ is measured only where $K_2$ rows or direct data
exist, and the $\Delta_g$-independence of the window correction is a structural argument.

\textbf{Why it is plausible} (proxy analysis reusing exact per-state weights and the
corrections measured at 300/1000; validated on the 300/1000 verdicts). At fixed $(J,m/\Lambda)$
a correction fixed in $x=\nu/m$ costs $\propto\Lambda^{-2}$ and one fixed in $\nu$ at small $\nu$
costs $\propto\Lambda^{-4}$ [D for the weights, DV]; the empirical exponent between 300 and 1000
is $p=2.83$ (median; range $1.4$--$3.2$) [NE]. The binding baseline decreases monotonically to the
certified flat $0.200$ (excess $0.0043$ / $0.0009$ / $0.00016$ at 300 / 1000 / 5000) [M + NE].
The large-spin window tolerance grows like $\Lambda^{1.0\text{--}1.2}$ [D, DV]. Q's binding state
is $\Delta_g$-stable: the measured values fit $s_d\approx0.5076+147/\Delta^2$ [NE/M]. Hypotheses
that get easier with $\Delta_g$: H17, $\delta k(0)$, $c_1$ ($\propto\Lambda^{-2}$), $H_{\rm LJ}$,
$H_{\rm edge}$, $H_R$, $H_{\rm OO}$, $H_{\rm RS}$, the de-shift. Harder: A2 ($c_T\gg\Delta_g^2$),
the $K_2$ coverage of $H_M$, and possibly $H_{\rm EFT}$(a) (toy error grows with $\Delta_h$;
untested at $\Delta_h\ge300$) [I].

\textbf{Below 300.} Nothing is claimed. The positivity threshold was not computed. At
$\Delta_g=100$ positivity fails at model level for near-gap spins $J\gtrsim18$ [M]; the
sign-free large-spin window bound closes only from $\Delta_g\approx275$ (margin $\times1.1$ at
300), so 300 is the lowest gap at which the present method closes.

\section{ABJM details}

Dictionary: $M^2=4/\alpha'$, $L^2/\alpha'=\pi\sqrt{2\lambda}$, so $\Lambda\leftrightarrow
ML=2^{5/4}\pi^{1/2}\lambda^{1/4}\approx4.22\lambda^{1/4}$ (leading order in $1/(ML)$). The
type~IIA $R^4$ term $\zeta(3)\alpha'^3/32$ gives $g_4M^6/(8\pi G)=2\zeta(3)=2.404$, checked by
expanding the Virasoro--Shapiro amplitude numerically. Placement $2.404/72.04=0.0334$ and
$2.404/92.81=0.0259$. $\hat g_3=0$ by ten-dimensional type~IIA supersymmetry (not by ``maximal''
supersymmetry: $\mathcal N=6$ at $k>2$). $c_T$ does not enter. The normalisation
$8\pi G=256/c_T$ follows from $c_T=32L^2/(\pi G)$ (free real scalar $c_T=1$). The identity
$c_T=(64/\pi^2)\partial_m^2F$ holds when the mass couples to a current in the stress-tensor
multiplet~\cite{ACP,BCPW}; in an $\mathcal N=2$ theory $\partial_m^2F$ gives a flavour-current
two-point coefficient~\cite{Fmax}, not $c_T$. ABJM violates A1 (light Kaluza--Klein modes);
only the flat image (F) is used and the bound is log-dominated. This is a consistency check,
not evidence for the theorem.
