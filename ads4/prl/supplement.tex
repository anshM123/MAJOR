\section{Grades, provenance, layers}
\label{sec:grades}

\textbf{Grades.} [P] proved; [certified] computer-assisted proof (every inequality checked in exact
rational or interval arithmetic, on top of written-out lemmas); [D] derived; [D given X] derived from the
inputs X (net grade = weakest of them); [D-sketch] derivation sketched (at least one step asserted from a
standard result without being written out or checked); [DV], [D\,+\,DV], [P\,+\,DV], [D; DV], [P; DV] derived or proved and
checked numerically; [Q] quoted; [NE$\pm$] numerical evidence with an explicit error budget (every model
term carries a $\times3$ safety factor, sign-free, and the conclusion survives it; this is an error
\emph{budget} on model terms, not a bound); [NE] numerical evidence; [NE\,+\,M]
computed inside the near-gap model of Sec.~\ref{sec:pos}; [M] model (an approximation whose accuracy is
itself a hypothesis); [A] assumption; [I] open. \emph{Composites.} ``[X\,+\,Y]'' (e.g.\ [D-sketch\,+\,M],
[Q\,+\,DV]) means X with one ingredient of grade Y, and has net grade the weaker of the two (for ``+\,DV'' the
check adds confidence but does not raise the grade); ``[X; DV]'' is X with a numerical check; ``[D given X]''
is derived from inputs X; ``[D, one step D-sketch]'' is net [D-sketch]. An argument carries the grade of its weakest step, ordered
[P]\,$=$\,[certified]\,$>$\,[DV]\,$>$\,[D]\,$>$\,[D-sketch]\,$>$\,[NE$\pm$]\,$>$\,[NE]\,$>$\,[NE\,+\,M]\,$>$\,[M]\,$>$\,[A]\,$>$\,[I].

\subsection{Normalisations and couplings}
$c_T=1$ for a free real scalar; $c_T=32L^2/(\pi G)$ in Einstein gravity, so $8\pi G=256/c_T$
($L=1$). The gap scale is $\Lambda=\sqrt{(\Delta_g-2)(\Delta_g-1)}$, i.e.\ $298.4996$ /
$998.4999$; under A1$'$ it is the spin-4 gap. Heavy states:
$m^2=(\Delta-J-2)(\Delta+J-1)$, $\cosh\beta=1+(2J+1)^2/(2m^2)$, $b=(2J+1)\Lambda/m$; the gap
state of spin $J$ has $m_{\rm gap}^2=(\Delta_g-2)(\Delta_g-1+2J)$. $t_4$ is the $d=3$
Hofman--Maldacena parameter, $\alpha_T$ the parity-odd coefficient; $\tau^2:=t_4^2+\alpha_T^2=57600|\hat
g_3|^2$ in AdS units; the $t_4$ half, $t_4=-240\,{\rm Re}\,\hat g_3$, is [DV] (two independent literature checks), the
$\alpha_T$ half, $\alpha_T=\mp240\,{\rm Im}\,\hat g_3$, is [D] with its sign convention open [I]; only the modulus
$\tau=240|\hat g_3|$ enters, at grade [D]. $\Lambda$ denotes the gap scale throughout, never a cosmological constant. The flat MHV function is
$f(s,u)=8\pi G/(stu)+\dots+g_4+g_5t+\dots$ in $M(1^+2^-3^-4^+)=\langle23\rangle^4[14]^4f$, with
type~II at $g_4M^6/8\pi G=2\zeta(3)$, $M^2=4/\alpha'$. Under A1$'$, $g_4$ is the $R^4$ coefficient with
light spin-0 and spin-2 fields kept in the amplitude, in the minimal light-exchange completion of
Ref.~\cite{CHLPS} (App.~B there); integrating out a light scalar would shift it by $|g_0|^2/m^2$. The
all-plus eight-derivative coefficient $\hat g_4$ (complex) is not bounded. Unit of beyond-Regge
corrections: $c_2:=m^2\times$(relative correction).

\subsection{Cutoff}
\label{sec:cutoff}
$\theta(\nu)=\tfrac12[{\rm erf}((\nu+\nu_c)/a)-{\rm erf}((\nu-\nu_c)/a)]$, even and entire,
bounded on horizontal strips, super-Gaussian decay. Every de-shift weight $\chi$ is replaced by
$\chi\theta$, with $(\nu_c,a)=(\Lambda+100,10)$ ($a$ is the cutoff width here only) for both $k=2$ pieces ($\Omega^{EE}$ and the smeared
$k=2$ piece of $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$, whose weight extends to $\nu\approx\Lambda$) and $(150,6)$ for the
$k=4,5$ pieces of $\Omega^Q$, the $k=2$ weights being the continuum weights (next subsection). On every
\emph{computed} number the cutoff's relative effect is $\lesssim e^{-180}$ ($k=4,5$) and $\lesssim10^{-41}$ ($k=2$) [D\,+\,NE]: these
are evaluated on $\nu\le\Lambda$ (or, for leading-Regge images, against kernels bounded in $\nu$), where $\theta=1$ to within its
leakage. Without the cutoff the interchange cannot be proved under $J_0\le1$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:HK}).
\emph{Beyond $\Lambda$} [DV, scalar proxy; M for $EE$]. Above a heavy state's mass $m\,(\ge\Lambda)$ its exact per-$\nu$ image grows
exponentially ($\ln\Gamma\approx m\,G(\nu/m)$, $G'\approx0.13$--$0.19$ at $\nu=m$, rising to $\pi/2$ only as $\nu/m\to\infty$; for the
$J=0$ gap state at $\Delta_g=1000$ it is $1.6\times10^{16}$ times its $\nu\to0$ value at $\nu=\Lambda+100$). The continuum $k=2$ weights
decay roughly like $e^{-(\pi/2)(\nu-\Lambda)}$ beyond $\Lambda$, with one local bump of about $\times100$ at $\Lambda+30$--$33$ (the
last Gaussians of the basis, centred near $\Lambda+30$; at $\Delta_g=1000$, ${\rm Re}\,\chi$ falls to $2\times10^{-22}$ at $\Lambda+28$ and rises to
$2.4\times10^{-20}$ at $\Lambda+32$), and super-Gaussianly beyond it. The bump is harmless and is included in the tail bound: the
tail beyond a state's mass is
$\le2.3\times10^{-5}$ of $\int|\psi|\,d\nu$ on every state tested ($\le4.4\times10^{-7}$ ($\Omega^{EE}$) / $1.6\times10^{-4}$
($\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$) of the state's own proxy action). The published $\Delta_g=1000$ \emph{grid} files, by contrast, run to
$\Lambda+312$ and carry a non-decaying double-precision floor ($|X|\approx10^{-16}$--$10^{-14}$) beyond $\approx\Lambda+10$; read
literally with $\theta(\Lambda+100,10)$, that floor would add to near-gap states with $m-\Lambda\lesssim60$--$100$ a tail of the size
of their action or larger, with a sign set by round-off. This is why the grids are read as zero beyond $\Lambda+10$ (next
subsection). The $\lesssim10^{-41}$ statement above holds for the computed values and for the continuum-defined functional, not for
the literal grid object; equivalently, the cutoff $(\Lambda+60,6)$, with the same leakage $\tfrac12{\rm erfc}(10)$ at $\Lambda$,
would remove the floor.

\subsection{The light side as one functional, and the collinear set}
\label{sec:arc}
\emph{Definition.} For celestial angles $a\neq b$, the single-ordering swapped form is
$E^+[G](a,b)=\oint_C\oint_C t_1^wt_3^wf_kG$, and the block action is
$\omega_k[\mathcal O](a,b)=E^+[G^s_{\mathcal O}](a,b)+(-1)^kE^+[G^s_{\mathcal O}](b,a)$. The
functional is $\Omega_k[G]=\sum_{\mathcal O\in(12)}p_{\mathcal O}\,\omega_k[\mathcal O]$, the
de-shifted piece $\hat C^{ds}=\int d\nu\,\chi_\theta\int d\varphi_1d\varphi_3\,C_\nu\Omega_k/N_{\rm com}$.
This is CLS-D's and CHMRS's sum-rule object~\cite{CLSD,CHMRS}; heavy blocks enter it one at a time, and the light
side is $\sum_{\rm light}p_{\mathcal O}\omega_k[\mathcal O]$, by linearity the sum over tree terms
$X$ of the term-wise $s$-channel value $\Omega_k[X]$.

\emph{Mellin form.} With
$\prod X_{ij}^{-\delta_{ij}}=(X_{14}X_{23})^{-2}(U/V)^{-\delta_{12}}V^{\delta_{13}}$ and every

poles). Cauchy at fixed $\delta_{13}$:
\begin{equation}
\text{line}-\text{term-wise}=-\oint_{\rm arc}\Gamma(\delta_{12})^2\Gamma(\delta_{14})^2M\,\mathcal K .
\end{equation}
After $1\leftrightarrow3$ (anti)symmetrisation the $D_{\rm LR}$ value of any microcausal term
vanishes as an integrand identity, so $\Omega_k[X]=\mathcal A_k[X]$, the arc. Along the arc $M$
grows like the Regge growth of $X$ and $\mathcal K$ like the corner behaviour of $H$: for Regge

\subsection{The interchange \texorpdfstring{$H_K$}{HK} by Tonelli with cut-off weights}

\emph{Corner count.} If $|K|\lesssim r^a\eta^c$ on $B_c$, R1$'$ gives absolute convergence of the

\begin{equation}
\text{exact edge piece}=(\text{leading-Regge edge piece})\times{}_2F_1(\tfrac12,h;h+\tfrac12;\rho^2),
\qquad h=\tfrac{1/2+i\nu}{2},

to all orders in $\rho$, with poles only at $\nu=i(\tfrac32+2p)$, where the leading coefficient

\emph{Step 2: the first moment has the helicity pattern of $\mathcal E$ [D\,+\,DV].} Translations give

\subsection{Energy-flux corollary}
\label{sec:flux}
In $d=3$ the energy measured along $y$ after a $T_{ij}$ insertion is, in the polarisation basis
$(\varepsilon_+,\varepsilon_\times)=((xx-yy)/\sqrt2,(xy+yx)/\sqrt2)$, the matrix
$(E/4\pi)\,[[1-t_4/4,\ \alpha_T/4],[\alpha_T/4,\ 1+t_4/4]]$ \cite[Eq.~(4.39)]{CDPrakash} [Q]; positivity of its eigenvalues gives
$\tau\le4$. Rotating the detector by $\theta$ rotates a spin-2 polarisation by $2\theta$, so the traceless part rotates by $4\theta$,
and for any normalised polarisation
$\langle\mathcal E(\theta)\rangle=(E/2\pi)[1+A_\varepsilon\cos(4\theta-\varphi_\varepsilon)]$ with
$A_\varepsilon=(\tau/4)\,|\varepsilon_+^2+\varepsilon_\times^2|/(|\varepsilon_+|^2+|\varepsilon_\times|^2)\le\tau/4$ [D; the identity
$(|\varepsilon_1|^2-|\varepsilon_2|^2)^2+4({\rm Re}\,\varepsilon_1^*\varepsilon_2)^2=|\varepsilon_1^2+\varepsilon_2^2|^2$ checked
algebraically]. Equality holds for linear polarisation; helicity eigenstates $\varepsilon\propto(1,\pm i)$ are isotropic. The
parity-even part reproduces $\langle\mathcal E(n)\rangle=(E/2\pi)[1+t_4(|\varepsilon_{ij}n_in_j|^2/\varepsilon^*\!\cdot\varepsilon-\tfrac14)]$
\cite{HofmanMaldacena,Buchel}; Ref.~\cite{Buchel}, Eq.~(3.8), prints the $d=3$ constant as $\tfrac18$, whereas zero net flux (the angular
average of $|\varepsilon_{ij}n_in_j|^2/\varepsilon^*\!\cdot\varepsilon$ is $\tfrac14=2/(d^2-1)$, their Eq.~(3.6)) requires $\tfrac14$,
consistent with $|t_4|\le4$. The cubic bound $\tau\le2.89126\times10^{-8}$ at $\Delta_g=1000$ gives $A\le7.23\times10^{-9}$ and a
maximum-to-minimum flux ratio $\le1+1.45\times10^{-8}$. With $t_4=-4(n_f-n_s)/(n_f+n_s)$, $\alpha_T=8\pi^4p_T/[3(n_f+n_s)]$, where
$n_s$, $n_f$ count the free real scalar and free Majorana fermion structures in $\langle TTT\rangle$ and $p_T$ multiplies the
parity-odd one, and $c_T=n_s+n_f$ (a Majorana fermion has the $c_T$ of a real scalar in $d=3$) \cite{CDPrakash}, this reads
$|n_s-n_f|\le7.23\times10^{-9}c_T$ and $|p_T|\le1.11\times10^{-10}c_T$. The corollary uses only the cubic functional (no $\kappa$, no
scalar condition), holds at leading order in $1/c_T$ under the same assumptions, and has the status of the cubic bound: the step
from the bound is [D] with quoted kinematics [Q] checked [DV].

\subsection{The de-shift identity in shifted form and the published weights}
\label{sec:deshift}
For a weight $u$ (C4: $\chi$; C5: $c/\hat N_5$),
$W[u](t)=\tfrac12\sum_j\int_{\mathbb R}uR_j\cos((\nu-ij)t)\,d\nu$. Shifting each $j$-term to
$\mu=\nu-ij$, assuming $uR_j$ analytic in the crossed strip apart from singularities cancelled by
zeros of $R_j$, a pole of $u$ at $\pm ij_{\max}$, and uniform decay, gives for every real $t$
\begin{equation}
W[u](t)=\sum_{j=\pm j_{\max}}\tfrac{i\pi s_j}{2}\big(a_1^{(j)}-a_3^{(j)}\tfrac{t^2}{2}\big)+\tfrac12\int_{\mathbb R}E(\mu)\cos\mu t\,d\mu .
\end{equation}
Imposing ``residue part $=\Phi$'' exactly gives $|W-\Phi|\le\varepsilon_W:=\int_0^\infty|E_{\rm even}|$ for
all $t\ge0$, hence $|{\rm image}-{\rm target}|\le\varepsilon_WP_{-1/2}(\cosh\beta)$ for all $\beta$
(Mehler--Dirichlet), and light-side moments exact to $\int|E|\mu^{2n}$. Results: $\varepsilon_W\le2.673\times10^{-13}$
(C4 value) and $\le9.058\times10^{-11}$ (C4 derivative), \emph{certified} by ball arithmetic (384 bits; Taylor
panels with Cauchy and aliasing remainders; residue part exact to $10^{-35}$); $\varepsilon_W=2.4\times10^{-7}$ (C5,
a 384-bit quadrature, not certified; it is not load-bearing for $H_K$). For $k=2$ the shifted form has no residue terms (the target is a function):
$W(t)=\tfrac12\int E(\mu)\cos\mu t\,d\mu$, $E(\mu)=2{\rm Re}[\chi(\mu-i)R_-(\mu-i)]$, and the continuum weights give
$\varepsilon_W=3.3\times10^{-6}$ / $1.1\times10^{-6}$ ($\Omega^{EE}$, i.e.\ $1.1\times10^{-9}$ / $1.1\times10^{-10}$ of
$\int|\psi|$) and $1.9\times10^{-7}$ / $7.4\times10^{-8}$ ($\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$) [DV]; the grid weights hold the identity
only to $t\approx22$. Every bound moves by $\le6\times10^{-11}$ on the per-state margins and $\le10^{-12}$
(C4) / $<10^{-6}$ (C5) on the light side. The upper end is exactly independent of the C5 weight [D].
Re-solution with separate code (Sec.~\ref{sec:repro}): $\Omega^{EE}$'s Abel weights to $\le1.5\times10^{-6}$ ($\nu<2$) of $\max\psi_2$, $X^Q$
to $\le8.3\times10^{-7}$; Q's C4 weights by a different method to $10^{-16}$ [DV].

\section{Per-state positivity: evidence}
\label{sec:pos}

\emph{Status.} For $\Omega^{EE}$ at $\Delta_g=1000$ this section and Secs.~\ref{sec:largespin}--\ref{sec:cor} are superseded by Theorem FP
(Sec.~\ref{sec:fullpos}), which proves positivity on every heavy state. They remain the evidence for $\Omega^{EE}$ at other gaps and for
$\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$, and an independent cross-check of Theorem FP where they overlap.

\textbf{How it is computed.} The action of each functional on a block is its flat part plus the leading-Regge
AdS curvature plus beyond-leading-Regge corrections. The flat part is certified (below). The curvature is an
explicit special-function integral, bounded where needed by Lemma C [P]: for real $\nu$, $\beta$,
$|P_{-1/2+i\nu}(\cosh\beta)-J_0(\nu B)|\le(1-{\rm sech}(\beta/2))+\min(2,\nu(B-\beta))$, $B=2\sinh(\beta/2)$, from the
Mehler--Dirichlet formula (worst ratio $0.73$ on a 30-digit check [DV]); since $\nu B=bp$ exactly, it compares the
two functions of the hybrid curvature directly. The corrections beyond leading Regge are evaluated node by node (glossary,
Sec.~\ref{sec:grades}): on $K_2$ rows, i.e.\ by the saddle-point (Laplace) series of the light-ray integrals around their bulk point, checked against
direct quasi-Monte-Carlo nodes, and at small $\nu$ by an analytic $1/\Delta$ expansion of the same integrals. Per spin, the rows are
interpolated in mass near the gap (the chain); elsewhere the hybrid model is used. For $J=7$ and $9$--$32$ at $\Delta_g=1000$, direct rows
at the state's own mass replace the Laplace series, so no truncation term enters there; for $J=21$, $23$, $25$, $27$, $29$, $31$ the
light-cone edges are integrated by Gauss--Kronrod quadrature of direct nodes, with no interpolation. Values so obtained are [NE\,+\,M].
Where the conclusion survives an error budget (every model term multiplied by $3$ and counted sign-free), they are [NE$\pm$]. An
error budget is not a bound: only the flat floor and the Lemma-C curvature term are bounds; the beyond-Regge
part of the budget is an estimate with a safety factor.

\textbf{Remarks.} (i) \emph{Choice of the functionals.} The structure ($q^2\to EE$) and the Abel gauge
(canonical $\to$ minimal norm) were chosen after heavy-state failures were seen; $\Omega^{EE}$ was
fixed before the states below were examined, except $J=18$, $20$, $24$, $28$ at $\Delta_g=300$, which are
in-sample (seen before it was fixed). In the canonical gauge the $J=24$ state at $300$ fails ($n=1.55$ by spline; $-22$ to $-40$
from direct data). The statement concerns the minimal-norm functional. (ii) \emph{Finite sets.} The
heavy spectrum is infinite in $J$ and $m$; Table~\ref{tab:cover} lists what covers the rest. There is no mass
$m_*$ above which all spins are covered. (iii) \emph{Flat certificates} (the flat floors). NF02: $\mathcal F\ge\tfrac15N$ on
every flat heavy state, both channels, all $J$ and $m\ge M$ [certified]; the corner ($+-$,
$J\ge400$, $M\le m<1.633M$) is covered by a backward-endpoint lemma for the Jacobi operator; the constant is sharp
(ratio $0.2000000103$ at $+-$, $J=5$, $m=M$). Q3$^*$: $\mathcal F\ge\tfrac{9}{50}N$ [certified] on all $J<400$, on $J\ge400$
with $b\le120$ (which contains the global minimum $0.1835$), on $++$, $J\ge400$, $m\le2M$, and on $J\ge400$, $b\ge120$,
$2M\le m\le10M$; not certified on $+-$, $J\ge400$, $m\le2M$ and on $J\ge400$, $b\ge120$, $m\ge10M$, where the
floating-point minima are $0.997$ and $0.304$ [NE]. The bulk-point limit of $\Omega^{EE}$ is NF02 by $EE\equiv q^2$ on
CLS-D's polarisation plane [P]; in the full contraction $EE/q^2\to1$ only as $m\to\infty$ [NE].

\textbf{$H_{\rm RS}$: from the definition to the expansion.} The $1/\Delta$ expansion behind $s_d$ is an expansion of
the light-ray integral over the real section cut at the light cones of the shadow point $x_5$ ($D'$). (i)
\emph{Definition [P\,+\,DV].} CLS-D define the functional as an absolutely convergent integral over real
Lorentzian points in the causal order $4>5>3>0>1>2$ (Lorentzian shadow representation of the
block)~\cite{CLSD}. In the gauge $x_3=-x_1=y$, $x_4=-x_2=x/(-x^2)$, $x_5=e$ the conditions $5>3$ and $4>5$ become
$(1-u_y)(1-v_y)>y_\perp^2$, $u_y<1$ and the same for $x$: exactly $D'$ (a separate Minkowski-space
implementation finds 0 mismatches in $2\times10^5$ configurations). On $D'$ the integrand equals a
point-independent constant times ours, and the constant cancels in the state/reference ratio [D\,+\,DV]. (ii)
\emph{Expansion [D].} The expansion equals $\int_{D'}$ up to $O(\Delta^Ne^{-0.336\Delta})$, from the far-region bound
$c=2\,{\rm artanh}(r/2)$ on $D'$; one sub-step (the convergence radius of the $x_0$-dependent slow factors) is
[D-sketch]. (iii) \emph{Checks [DV].} The direct seven-dimensional quadrature over $D'$ equals the expansion at

$\Delta$ with the cut, equals the exact Mellin skeleton to $2\times10^{-8}$ ($\Delta=20$), $6\times10^{-9}$ ($30$) and
$10^{-9}$ ($50$, to $\nu=10$), where $82\%$, $47\%$, $3\%$ of $\int|\Phi|$ lies outside the convergence region of the
$1/\Delta$ series, which tests these steps non-perturbatively. Truncation: $|\Delta s_d|\le0.015$ / $0.0015$, a factor
$\ge85$ inside the margin. (iv) The steepest-descent (saddle-point) cycle is only a numerical method; its
identity with $D'$ is [P] in the Regge-scaled system, open at finite $\Delta$, and a direct test is consistent
but weak. Critical points do lie in the far region (there are many,
at raw radius $0.3$--$1.5$); this does not affect $D'$.

\section{Large spin: two conditional theorems}
\label{sec:largespin}

This section states what is proved, and under which premises, about $\Omega^{EE}$ at $\Delta_g=1000$ on heavy
states beyond the computed sets. Its two conditional theorems are Theorem W and Theorem L$'$; at $\Delta_g=1000$ they are now a cross-check of Theorem FP (Sec.~\ref{sec:fullpos}). Both rest on one structural
assumption, the uniform decay rate (AG$_U$) of Sec.~\ref{sec:largespin}.D, beyond all orders [A], and on magnitude premises graded
[M]; Theorem W also on one real-axis edge integral of the graviton, (EDGE-R) [M], and on the leading-Regge floor, certified on every heavy state by T0 (Sec.~\ref{sec:T0}). (The stronger pointwise premise (AG$'$) is false near the gap edge and is not used, Sec.~\ref{sec:largespin}.D.)
Notation: $\beta$ is the transverse (H$_2$) impact parameter in AdS units, $\sinh(\beta/2)=(J+\tfrac12)/m$,
$b=2\Lambda\sinh(\beta/2)=(2J+1)\Lambda/m$, $\eta=\cosh\beta$, $u=\Lambda^2/m^2$, $x=\nu/m$, $P=P_{-1/2+i\nu}(\eta)$,
$\Omega_k(\beta)=\int_0^1\psi_k(p)P_{-1/2+i\Lambda p}(\cosh\beta)\,dp$, and
$F_{\rm LR}(\beta,u,\sigma)=2\Omega_2(\beta)+u\sigma\Omega_3(\beta)$ ($\sigma=\pm1$ for $++$/$+-$) is the leading-Regge
per-state action. A separate run with its own code (Sec.~\ref{sec:repro}) reproduced the
identity of Sec.~\ref{sec:largespin}.A, the constant $D$, Lemma LR$_\infty$ and the algebra of Theorem W.

\subsection{An exact de-shift identity for the $EE$ kernel}
The $EE$ kernel is an exact finite sum of shifted scalar continuous-spin blocks (Table~\ref{tab:inputs}, LRC$^*$),
$K^{EE}_\nu=N(\nu)r^2\sum_bC_b(x;{\rm pols})v^{b/2}\hat G^1_b(x;\nu)$, with $\nu$-independent $C_b$ that carry all the
polarisation dependence ($r$ the radial variable). \emph{Identity.} For every shift $b$ and all complex $\nu$,
\begin{equation}
2\eta(1+r^2)\,\hat G^1_b(\nu)=\Big(1+\frac{i}{2\nu}\Big)\hat G^0_b(\nu+i)+\Big(1-\frac{i}{2\nu}\Big)\hat G^0_b(\nu-i),
\label{eq:magic}
\end{equation}
with the \emph{same} coefficients for every $b$ (those of CHMRS's magic-block identity at $b=0$~\cite{CHMRS};
$\hat G^{0}_b$ are the corresponding blocks one unit lower in the exchanged dimension). \emph{Grade:} [P] to $O(r^6)$ with
$b$ and the spin both symbolic, so it is an identity in $b$ at each of these orders; [P] to $O(r^8)$ for every integer
$b=-4,\dots,4$ (every residual coefficient is the zero rational function); [DV] to $O(r^{40})$ in 50-digit arithmetic
(relative residual $\le3.7\times10^{-48}$ for $b=-4,\dots,4$, $0.37$, $7$, $10$ and complex $\nu$; the original computation
gives $\le7.9\times10^{-48}$ for $b=0,\dots,4$); [D] at all orders. \emph{Consequence} [P\,+\,DV, given the
representation above]: for every polarisation set and to all orders in $r$, the raw image of \emph{any} input (plane
wave in any gauge, any polynomial or polarisation insertion, any heavy block) is exactly a scalar de-shift,
${\rm raw}(\nu)=R^{\rm sc}_-(\nu)F(\nu+i)+R^{\rm sc}_+(\nu)F(\nu-i)=:\mathcal A_g[F](\nu)$, with $F$ even and analytic for
$|{\rm Im}\,\mu|\le3/2$; end to end against the direct light-ray quadrature: $2\times10^{-7}$--$1.1\times10^{-6}$
with refined quadrature (quadrature-limited; the default quadrature gives up to $7.6\times10^{-3}$ at $\nu=6$). Our weights are built for $\mathcal A=\mathcal A_g\circ c$ with
$c(\mu)=32(4\mu^2+1)(4\mu^2+9)/((4\mu^2+25)(4\mu^2+81))$, and $\mathcal A_g[F]=\mathcal A[F/c]$ is of that form iff
$F(\pm i/2)=0$, a Regge-spin-0 (``monopole'') condition. It holds at leading Regge order, but it is not protected
[P]: radial-weight shifts that $O(1/m^2)$ terms produce give $c_s(i/2)=0.914$ ($|y|^2$) and $0.500$ ($r=|x||y|$).
\emph{Exact cost.} The $k=2$ action of $\mathcal A_g[F]$ is $\int\psi_2F/c+D\,F(i/2)$, with
$D=-9.98332\times10^{-4}$ ($\Delta_g=1000$; $-1.09754\times10^{-2}$ at 300; $\approx-990/\Lambda^2$). The form and the
impact-parameter independence of the monopole term are derived [D]: $1/c=\tfrac1{32}+\tfrac{15/2}{4\mu^2+1}-\tfrac{9/2}{4\mu^2+9}$,
the first and last pieces and $(F-F(i/2))/(4\mu^2+1)$ are analytic in the de-shift strip, so the defect of $F/c$ is
$\tfrac{15}2F(i/2)\,\delta_1$ with $\delta_1$ the defect of $1/(4\nu^2+1)$; only the value of $D=\tfrac{15}{2}\delta_1$ is
numerical [DV] (reproduced to 8 digits by separate code, Sec.~\ref{sec:repro}). The equivalent cost kernel is bounded by $1.0\times10^{-3}$ for
$t\ge0.03$. So the only part of a correction not of the form the weights were built for is one monopole term per state,
with an exact, impact-parameter-independent cost. \emph{For $\langle TTTT\rangle$ at $O(1/m^2)$ the monopole cancels}
(Sec.~\ref{sec:largespin}.C).

\emph{Two lemmas used below.} \emph{Lemma I} [P]: if a de-shifted image is $\int P_{-1/2+i\nu}(\cosh t)\,\sigma(dt)$,
its $k=2$ action is $\int\Omega_2(t)\,\sigma(dt)$; every correction structure therefore has an exact cost. \emph{Congruence}
[P]: if $M(m)=\mathfrak n\,U^\dagger gU$ with $\mathfrak n>0$ and $U$ invertible and $\nu$-independent, then $M\succeq0\Leftrightarrow g\succeq0$:
$\nu$-independent normalisations and coupling-basis maps cannot change positivity, at any size. In the symmetric
frame of Ref.~\cite{CHMRS} the spinning integrand is a sum of $(\Delta,J)$-independent weight-shifting operators
acting on scalar seeds~\cite{CPPR} [Q\,+\,D]. A Weyl reflection,

\subsection{Higher orders: window lemmas and the assumption (AG\texorpdfstring{$_U$}{\_U})}
\emph{Why a window.} Costing the truncated fixed-$\beta$ $1/m$ series over the whole range $0\le\nu\le\Lambda\approx m$ fails
beyond $J\approx80$, but the failure is an artefact: the series is asymptotic and worsens past $x\approx0.3$--$0.4$. On exact
scalar heavy moments (Arb, $J=40,48,64,100$ at the gap) the true remainder beyond the hybrid and beyond $O(1/m^2)$ costs
$\le1.5\times10^{-3}$ of the budget (at $J=100$, $22$ times less than the series' estimate) [DV]. Inside the Regge window
$\nu<\nu_w=3/\beta$ the series converges (successive orders fall by $\le6.5\times10^{-3}$ at all 47 states) and matches the
exact cost to $0.3\%$ [DV].

\emph{Lemma P4W} [P] (Cauchy). Fix a state and an entry $e$; let $G$ be the beyond-hybrid correction (exact minus hybrid,
de-shifted, per unit leading image), $p_w=\nu_w/\Lambda$, and $F_2^{\rm bh}$ the computed $O(1/m^2)$ correction minus the
hybrid's own flat $O(y)$ tower. Then $|{\rm cost}_2(G)|$ is at most the window cost of $F_2^{\rm bh}/m^2+S_4/m^4$ plus
$2\varepsilon_w\int_0^{p_w}|\psi_2|$, plus $4[V(p_w)M_w+V(1)(M_1+A)+V'(1)A+{\rm Top}]$, plus the exact monopole term, where
$V(p_0)=\int_0^T|\psi_2(p_0+iy)|e^{-\kappa y}dy$ and $A$ is the known edge amplitude of the hybrid's curvature waves;
the $k=3$ piece is the same with $u\psi_3$. The $1/m$ series is never used outside the window, and no analyticity is used
inside it. Premises:
(W) on $\nu<\nu_w$, real axis only, $|G-F_2^{\rm bh}/m^2-S_4/m^4|\le\varepsilon_w$, where $S_4$ is the $O(1/m^4)$ beyond-hybrid term
(scalar seed computed; spinning part $=K_{4w}\times$ the window costs of $\{\eta^2\nu^4P,\ \eta\sinh\beta\,\nu^4\partial_\beta P\}$);
a decay premise on the complement $[p_w,1]$: the uniform rate (AG$_U$) below (the pointwise (AG$'$) is false);
(EDGE) the amplitude of exact $-$ flat at $p=1$ ($\nu=\Lambda$, $x_1=\Lambda/m$) is at most $M_1$; on the top side at most
$M_{\rm top}$ (suppressed by $e^{-25}$).
The boundary amplitude $M_w$ is taken as twice the real-axis sup near $\nu_w$ [D-sketch]. On the exact scalar the lemma's
bound is $4.0$--$7.9\%$ of the budget against a true cost $\lesssim10^{-3}$ [DV].

\emph{The pointwise premise (AG$'$) and why it is not used} [P\,+\,DV; NE]. The natural premise (AG$'$) would be: $g_e:=$ exact$_e-$flat$_e$ (de-shifted,
per unit leading image) is, on $Q_\pm=\{p_w\le{\rm Re}\,p\le1,\ 0\le\pm{\rm Im}\,p\le T\}$, a sum $g_++g_-$ of analytic functions with
$|g_\pm(p\pm iy)|\le M(p)e^{-\kappa(p)y}$, with the \emph{local} rate $\kappa(p)=0.97\Lambda\beta/\sqrt{1-x^2}$ for $y\le15/\kappa(p)$. In this
pointwise form it is false near the gap edge: the flat partial wave itself violates the local-rate majorant for $x_1\ge0.95$, by up to
a factor $4.1$ at $(J,m)=(26,1.026\Lambda)$, because the branch point of $\theta(\nu)=2\arcsin(\nu/m)$ at $\nu=m$ bends its decay
profile; only the integrated consequence holds (the edge integral with the true flat profile is $0.72$--$0.82$ of (AG$'$)'s at all 67
sampled states) [P\,+\,DV]. The exact scalar correction, continued to complex $\nu$ (Arb, entire Laplace--Barnes series), has an
effective edge rate of $0.64$--$0.86$ of the local rate at $x_1=0.94$--$0.96$, and $0.98$--$1.06$ of it in the bulk ($x\le0.86$) [NE].

\emph{Lemma F} [P]. The flat partial wave is an exact pair of analytic travelling waves, ${\rm flat}_\pm=E_2W_\pm(\theta(\nu))/2$ with
$W_+(\theta)=\tfrac{2}{\pi i}C_Je^{i(J+1)\theta}{}_2F_1(\tfrac12,J+1;J+\tfrac32;e^{2i\theta})$ and
$|W_+(\theta)|\le\tfrac2\pi C_Je^{-(J+1){\rm Im}\,\theta}\min\{2/|\sin{\rm Re}\,\theta|,(1-e^{-2{\rm Im}\,\theta})^{-1/2}\}$ (DLMF 14.13.1 and
Abel summation; checked to $3\times10^{-29}$). It satisfies the uniform rate below on the whole rectangle (ratio $\ge1.013$ at every
sampled state); so do the curvature waves of the hybrid (Harish-Chandra and Hankel forms) [P].

\emph{Assumption (AG$_U$)} (the decay assumption of Theorems W and L$'$). For each entry $e$, $G_e:=$ exact$_e-$hybrid$_e$ (de-shifted,
per unit leading image) is, on $Q_\pm=[p_w,1]\times[0,\pm T]$, $T\le T_0=25/(0.97\Lambda\beta)$, a sum $G_{e,+}+G_{e,-}$ of functions
analytic in $Q_\pm$ and continuous on their closures, with $|G_{e,\pm}(p\pm iy)|\le M(p)\,e^{-0.97\Lambda\beta\,y}$ on the three sides:
the \emph{uniform} rate only, with no local rate and no threshold. Amplitudes: $M(p_w)$ from the window series' waves (computed),
$M(1)\le\Lambda_{\rm AG}e_{\rm sc}(1)+A$, $M\le M_{\rm top}$ on the top. \emph{Status:} every explicit piece satisfies it [P] (Lemma F,
curvature waves); for the exact correction it holds to all orders of the saddle-point expansion (decay $\ge1.002\beta$) [D]; beyond
all orders it is \textbf{assumed} [A]. Near the edge it asks only that the split exist at the uniform rate; the exact scalar's
correction decays at $\ge2.3$ times that rate on the edge line $p=1$ and $\ge1.11$ times on every line tested [NE]. The plane-wave
(spin-Gamma) representation of heavy blocks cannot supply it near the gap at $J\le50$: $14$--$35\%$ of its moment-weighted measure lies
beyond its own twist edge, so the state's edge behaviour comes from cancellations between plane waves [P\,+\,DV]; it is harmless for
$J\ge100$.

\emph{Lemma P4W-$\chi$} [P] (Cauchy, and $|{\rm erfc}(a+ib)|\le e^{b^2}{\rm erfc}(a)$ for real $a$, $b$). With the entire cutoff
$\chi(p)=\tfrac12{\rm erfc}((p_e-p)/\sigma)$, $p_w<p_e<1$, split $\int_{p_w}^1\psi_2G=E_\chi[G]+\int_{p_w}^1\psi_2(1-\chi)G$. The second
term is moved by Cauchy to the sides of $Q_\pm$ and needs only (AG$_U$); the edge amplitude enters it suppressed by
$\tfrac12{\rm erfc}((1-p_e)/\sigma)$. The first is the premise
(EDGE-R) $|\int_{p_w}^1\psi_2\chi G_e\,dp|\le\varepsilon_{E,2}$ and $|\int_{p_w}^1\psi_3\chi G_e\,dp|\le\varepsilon_{E,3}$: one real-axis
integral per entry, with no continuation and no rate.
Then $|{\rm cost}(G)|$ is at most the window terms of Lemma P4W, plus $2\varepsilon_{E,2}+u\varepsilon_{E,3}$, plus the Cauchy terms of
the cutoff complement, plus the exact monopole term. Since (AG$'$)\,$\wedge$\,(EDGE) imply (AG$_U$)\,$\wedge$\,(EDGE-R), the new pair is
strictly weaker; (EDGE-R) keeps the cancellations of the edge region that an amplitude bound discards. The cutoff $(p_e,\sigma,T)$ is
fixed per state (grid $p_e\in[0.2,0.75]$, $\kappa(p_e)\sigma\ge10$).

\section{Positivity at every gap \texorpdfstring{$\Delta_g\ge1000$}{Delta g >= 1000}}
\label{sec:allgap}

This section states which parts of the $\Delta_g=1000$ evidence for $\Omega^{EE}$ (Secs.~\ref{sec:pos},
\ref{sec:largespin}) hold at every $\Delta_g\ge1000$, with which grade and under which premises. A state at gap $\Delta_g$ is
compared with a state at $1000$ in one of two ways (glossary, Sec.~\ref{sec:grades}): \emph{chart A} keeps $(J,m/\Lambda,h)$ fixed,
\emph{chart B} keeps $(\beta,m/\Lambda)$ fixed, i.e.\ $J+\tfrac12\propto\Lambda$. $\Gamma$ is the beyond-hybrid correction in $c_2$ units
($m^2\times$ the correction relative to the flat envelope), $x=\nu/m$, $\xi=\nu/\Delta$. 

\textbf{Summary.} (1) The certified layers hold at every $\Delta_g\ge1000$ [P; certified] (Sec.~\ref{sec:allgap}.B).
(2) At $\Delta_g=2000$ every near-gap spin $J\le21$ except $18$, $J=23$, $27$, $29$ and the parity-odd OO states $J=4$--$10$
were computed on own rows, for $J=12$, $16$, $20$, $21$, $23$, $27$, $29$ with direct (Monte Carlo) evaluations of the light-ray
integral, including the seam (two spins also at $5000$); nothing is negative; $J\le13$, $J=16$, $20$, $21$, $23$, $27$ and the OO
states close [NE$\pm$] sign-free at every computed mass, $J=14$, $15$, $17$, $19$ on the $\lambda$-level budget only, and $J=29$ from
$1.03\Lambda$ on ([NE\,+\,M] at its $2000$ twist edge and hybrid minimum, $L=0.71$, $0.77$); the direct values differ from the $K_2$ values on the
same rows by at most $0.026$, $\le3\%$ of the $K_2$ truncation charge; for $J\le16$ every budget term scales like $\Lambda^{-2}$ or
faster; $J=18$, $22$, $24$--$26$, $28$, $30$--$32$ are not computed at $2000$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:allgap}.C).
(3) Between and beyond the computed gaps the
transfer rests on a no-growth premise: for $J\le7$ the implication is proved, [P given B$_F$ + windows] with B$_F$ [NE]; for the other
near-gap states it is [D-sketch given $H_{\rm mono\text{-}A}$], and near-gap large spin is taken in chart B under $H_{\rm mono\text{-}B}$ [M]
(Sec.~\ref{sec:allgap}.D). (4) Above $\Delta_1^*(J)\approx0.9\times10^5$--$4.2\times10^5$ positivity of the near-gap states with
$J\le8$ is [P given TAIL, $\Pi\le3$, bounded $K_2$ part, $\xi$-continuity], with the graviton amplitude certified at $J=0,2$ only
(Sec.~\ref{sec:allgap}.E). (5) The large-spin theorems keep their premises, and their margins grow with the gap [DV]
(Sec.~\ref{sec:allgap}.F). (6) One near-gap band, $33\le J<J_W$ above the seam ($J_W\approx26.5\,\Lambda/\Lambda_0$, the chart-B image of $J=26$), is reached by neither
chart (Sec.~\ref{sec:allgap}.D, Table~\ref{tab:coverGap}). \emph{Net:} positivity of $\Omega^{EE}$ at a gap $\Delta_g>1000$ has at best
the grade it has at $1000$; on $1000<\Delta_g<\Delta_1^*$ it rests in addition on the no-growth premise, at large spin on premise
constants carried in AdS units [M], and the band of (6) is not covered.  Nothing
in this section concerns $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$. \emph{Literature.} We know of no explicit uniform-in-gap bound: the flat-to-AdS uplifts
of CHMRS (``sufficiently large'' $M$)~\cite{CHMRS} and of Ref.~\cite{CHLPSpw} (an unquantified $O(1/\Delta_{\rm gap}^4)$) are not
effective; the uniformity of Knop and Maz\'a\v{c} in AdS$_2$ comes from pointwise kernel positivity~\cite{KnopMazac}, which
$\Omega^{EE}$ does not have; van Rees and Zhao note that uniformity is not immediate~\cite{vanReesZhao}.

\subsection{Monotonicity in the gap, and the constants}
\emph{Remark} [D]. A theory that satisfies A1$'$ and A2 with gap $\Delta_g'$ satisfies them with every $\Delta_g\le\Delta_g'$:
its single traces of spin $\ge4$ have twist $\ge\Delta_g'\ge\Delta_g$, and the states of twist $<\Delta_g$ are among those of
twist $<\Delta_g'$, whose data are tree level. So, given (Pos) at $\Delta_g=1000$, the headline numbers bound every theory with
$\Delta_g'\ge1000$ (with $\Lambda=\Lambda(1000)$). Positivity at $\Delta_g'$ itself gives the sharper bound at $\Delta_g'$,

\begin{equation}
|\hat g_3|\Lambda^4\le\sqrt{16L_A}=54.98\sqrt{\ln(\Lambda/8.7415)+6.384/\Lambda+\dots}
=119.75,\ 128.17,\ 138.54,\ 145.90,\ 168.06
\label{eq:g3gap}
\end{equation}
at the same gaps [DV] (printed by the reproduction code, \texttt{reproduce.py} and the scripts it calls). So the $\Delta_g=1000$ numbers are the weakest of the family in
absolute terms, not in gap units. The quartic upper end $2L_{\rm AdS}=92.81$, $104.77$, $120.57$ (Table~\ref{tab:dg}) remains a

\section{An argument without positivity: the angular route}
\label{sec:angular}
\emph{Sum rule.} For a stress-tensor state at rest, the detector $E(n)$ and its first retarded-time moment
$E^{(1)}(n)=\int du\,u\,T(u,n)$ commute at separated directions. Projecting the commutator on the angular weights
$q_0=-3$, $q_{\pm1}=0$, $q_m=6/((m-1)m(m+1))$ ($|m|\ge2$), whose generating function is
$Q(\theta)=(1-\cos\theta)[12\log(2|\sin\frac\theta2|)-9]$ with $Q(0)=Q'(0)=0$, gives the endpoint rule
$\sum_{\mathcal O\ne T,b}\big[\frac{T_\Delta-b(b-1)}3+\frac32\big]|a_{\mathcal O,b,0}|^2=3+\mathcal D$, $T_\Delta=(\Delta-1)(\Delta-2)$,
where $a_{\mathcal O,b,k}$ are the normalised rest-frame detector amplitudes on the two helicity ladders $b=0,4$ and
$\mathcal D\ge0$ is an exactly defined spectral defect (the failure of the angular/spectral exchange).

\emph{Exact ingredients} [P given the two operator identities below; checked against the detector pipeline for $J\le40$, both
parities, exact rational arithmetic]. (a) The special conformal Ward identity $[\mathsf K_n,E]=-6E^{(1)}$ gives the first-moment
matrix $M=\frac i6AH$ with $A$ tridiagonal, diagonal $T_\Delta+J(J+1)-2m^2$, off-diagonal $-\sqrt{(J(J+1)-m(m+1))(T_\Delta-m(m+1))}$,
and $A\succeq(\sqrt{T_\Delta}-\sqrt{J(J+1)})^2$. (b) Conservation of the light-transformed stress tensor gives, for every $J$,
$a_{k+1}/a_k=\frac{k+4}{k+1}\sqrt{(J(J+1)-s(s+1))/(T_\Delta-s(s+1))}$, $s=b+k$, using the all-$J$ momentum-space helicity norms.
(c) Telescoping: on every ladder $\sum_kq_{k+2}{\rm Re}[\bar a_k(Aa)_k]=[T_\Delta-b(b-1)]|a_0|^2$ exactly. (d) The fourth-harmonic
ANEC rule $\sum_{\mathcal O,J\ge6}|a_{\mathcal O,4,2}|^2\ge(t_4^2+\alpha_T^2)/16$ follows from $[E_4,E_{-4}]=0$ (commuting angular POVM),
with $|a_2|^2/|a_0|^2=100\frac{(C-20)(C-30)}{(T_\Delta-20)(T_\Delta-30)}$, $C=J(J+1)$.

\emph{Result.} With $K(g)=\frac{729}{4800}(g-2)^2\big(1-\frac{49}{(2g+9)^2}\big)$, a uniform lower bound on the endpoint weight per unit
fourth-harmonic weight for twist $\ge g$, $J\ge6$ [P],
\[
t_4^2+\alpha_T^2\le\frac{16(3+\mathcal D)}{K(\Delta_g)}+16\mathcal L_g ,
\]
with $\mathcal L_g$ the fourth-harmonic weight of states below the gap. Hence $\tau\le0.0178$ at $\Delta_g=1000$ when
$\mathcal D=\mathcal L_g=0$, falling as $(160/9)/\Delta_g$.

\emph{The two corrections.} $\mathcal D=0$ is equivalent to the absence of a critical $L^{-2}$ tail of the spin-weighted endpoint
measure in the band $\sqrt J\lesssim\Delta-J\ll J$; bounded-, slowly-growing- and comparable-twist sectors provably contribute nothing.
The band is closed by crossing at each finite $c_T$ (Sec.~\ref{sec:angcross}): all-$J$ collinear identities for the conserved
$\langle TTO\rangle$ structures, a reflection-positive two-scale crossing bound in the mirror ordering, and explicit kernel envelopes yield
hence $\mu((L,\infty))=O(L^{-3})$ ($O(L^{-4})$ in the band), $B_N=O(1/N)$ and $\mathcal D=0$. The constants may depend on the CFT;
the order of limits is finite $c_T$ first, as for the main result (Sec.~\ref{sec:HK}), so the planar Regge intercept $J_0=2$ never
enters. The mixed commutator needs $J_0<2$ in the Regge corner~\cite{KKSDZ} and light-cone convergence (Ref.~\cite{KKSDZ},
Eq.~(4.66)). A separate run re-derived this argument (Sec.~\ref{sec:repro}, run (f)); the all-$J$ proofs are in Sec.~\ref{sec:angcross}. $\mathcal L_g\to0$ follows from
planar factorisation: transitions from $T$ to multi-trace states are $O(c_T^{-1/2})$, and $\Delta_T$ is protected, so degenerate
multi-trace clusters have no $O(c_T^{-1/2})$ splitting [D]. The contact term of the mixed commutator is $c_0\delta$ if no scalar with
$\Delta\le1$ appears in $T\times T$, and $Q(0)=0$ removes it.

\emph{Free-field check.} In the free scalar and Dirac fermion the null-plane algebra holds with no extra contact terms, and the
endpoint rule holds exactly, $\frac32$ (from the spin-4 current) $+\frac32$ (from $\phi^2$ or $\bar\psi\psi$) $=3$, with $\mathcal D=0$
(the bound itself does not apply there: there is no gap).

\emph{Inputs.} (1) the first-moment and ladder identities as physical operator identities (algebra [P], pipeline-checked); (2) the
contact coefficient (finite-frequency light-ray algebra, $\Delta_\phi>1$); (3) fixed-window planar regularity; (4) light-cone
convergence and existence of the mixed commutator. The exact checks are part of the reproduction code (\texttt{reproduce.py} and the scripts it calls).

\subsection{The finite-$c_T$ spectral estimate: collinear identities and two-scale crossing}
\label{sec:angcross}
\emph{Collinear identities} [P, all $J$]. Place $x_1=(0,-1,0)$, $x_4=0$, $x_3=(0,1,0)$ on a line of the plane $t=0$,
polarise $\mathcal O$ by $e_+=(0,1,i)$ (a 2d quasi-primary of weights $(h,s)=(\frac{\Delta-J}2,\frac{\Delta+J}2)$) and let
$F_{m_1m_3}$ be the coefficient of the 2d three-point structure in the planar-spin components $T_{m_1}$, $T_{m_3}$.
There $V_4=-\frac12$, $H_{14}\propto a_1^2$, $H_{34}\propto a_3^2$, $\epsilon_{14}\propto a_1b_1$, so only the $V_4^J$
monomials reach $m_1,m_3\le-1$. The canonical structures are explicit polynomial vectors in $(s,J)$, conserved and Bose
symmetric for symbolic $J$ (checked as rational-function identities at one conformal frame, which suffices by
covariance), and their extreme detector amplitudes $M_{\mp J}$ follow in closed form by expanding $V_4^{J-k}$
binomially and summing with Chu--Vandermonde; $S^{++}$, $S^{\rm odd}$ have $M_{+J}=0$ and $S^{+-}$, $S^{\rm odd}_J$ have
$M_{-J}=0$. Hence, for all $J$ and in the canonical normalisation of the structures,
$16F_{-2,-2}=p(h)(h-4)(\lambda_{+-}-2\lambda_{++})$, $16F_{-1,-1}=-16p(h)[2(s-1)\lambda_{++}+s\lambda_{+-}]$,
$\pm16iF_{-2,-1}=-2(2h-7)(2h-5)(2h-3)\lambda_{\rm odd}$, $p(h)=(h-1)(h-2)(h-3)$. With the ladder ratios, the absolute
kernels are, e.g., $|K^{++}_0|^2=864\pi^{3/2}\sin^2(\pi h)\,4^\Delta\frac{2^J}{\binom{2J}J}\frac{\Gamma(s+\frac12)}{\Gamma(s)}
\frac{\Gamma(h)}{\Gamma(h-\frac12)}\frac{\Gamma(\Delta-1)\Gamma(\Delta-\frac12)}{\Gamma(\Delta+3)^2}$, and Wendel's inequality gives,
for all $J\ge4$, $h\ge5$, $|K_{\rm even}|^2\le c\,4^\Delta2^{-J}\sqrt{J+1}\sqrt{sh}\,\Delta^{-15/2}$ and
$|K_{\rm odd}|^2\le c\,4^\Delta2^{-J}\sqrt{J+1}\,\Delta^{-15/2}/\sqrt{sh}$ with explicit $c$ ($c_{++}<10^4$).

\emph{Two-scale crossing} [P given unitarity, OPE convergence with $s$--$t$ crossing, a unique $T$ with finite $c_T$, and
the count of conserved structures]. Let $A=T_{m_1}$, $B=T_{m_2}$ and take the mirror ordering
$G(z,\bar z)=\langle A^\dagger(\infty)B^\dagger(1)B(z,\bar z)A(0)\rangle$. Decomposing the 3d Hilbert space into
$sl(2)\times sl(2)$ representations of the plane, $G=z^{-h_A-h_B}\bar z^{-\bar h_A-\bar h_B}\sum_p\frac{|C_{ABp}|^2}{N_p}
k^{(\delta)}_{2h_p}(z)k^{(\bar\delta)}_{2\bar h_p}(\bar z)$ with $k^{(\delta)}_{2h}=z^h{}_2F_1(h+\delta,h+\delta;2h;z)$,
$\delta=h_B-h_A$: every term is $\ge0$ at independent real $z,\bar z\in(0,1)$ (the ordering $\langle ABA^\dagger B^\dagger\rangle$
would give $(h+\delta,h-\delta)$ and indefinite signs). In the crossed channel the identity gives
$N_AN_B(1-z)^{-2h_B}(1-\bar z)^{-2\bar h_B}$, and Cauchy--Schwarz bounds the rest by the identity-subtracted mirror
correlators $\langle BB^\dagger BB^\dagger\rangle$, $\langle A^\dagger AA^\dagger A\rangle$, monotone in each variable
(all non-vacuum weights are $\ge\frac14$) and finite at the Euclidean point $\frac14$. Euler's integral gives
$k^{(\delta)}_{2h}(1-S^{-2})\ge c(\delta)4^hS^{1/2+2\delta}$ for $h\in[S,2S]$, $S\ge4$, $|\delta|\le1$, with explicit
$c(\delta)$. Taking $z=1-U^{-2}$, $\bar z=1-S^{-2}$ and $|C_{AB\,\mathcal O(e_+)}|^2/N=\kappa_02^{-J}|F_{m_1m_2}|^2$:
\[
\sum_{s\in[S,2S],\,h\in[U,2U]}4^\Delta2^{-J}|F_{m_1m_2}|^2\le\mathcal K\,S^{11/2+M}U^{11/2-M},\qquad M=m_1+m_2 ,
\]
with $\mathcal K$ depending on the CFT only through $T$-component norms and two Euclidean four-point values.

\emph{Consequences} [P given the above and the ladder]. Inverting the identities ($p(h)(h-4)\ge0.038h^4$ for $h\ge5$)
gives $\sum4^\Delta2^{-J}(|\lambda_{++}|^2+|\lambda_{+-}|^2)\lesssim S^{3/2}U^{3/2}$ and
$\sum4^\Delta2^{-J}|\lambda_{\rm odd}|^2\lesssim S^{5/2}U^{5/2}$ per shell, hence $\sum_{\rm shell}\sum_b|a_{\mathcal O,b,0}|^2
\lesssim S^{-5}U^2$, $\sum_{\tau\ge10}\Delta^2|a_0|^2<\infty$, $\mu((L,\infty))=O(L^{-3})$ ($O(L^{-4})$ in the band),
$B_N=O(1/N)$ (sharp for this input) and $\mathcal D=0$. The sectors $J=0,2$ close because $F$ grows like $\Delta^0,\Delta,
\Delta^3,\Delta^2$ (even/odd $J=0$, even/odd $J=2$), giving $\Delta_0^{-3/2}$ per dyadic shell. The ladder ratios rest on
light-transform conservation and the all-$m$ helicity norms of Ref.~\cite{GilliozSpinors} (Eqs.~(2.37)--(2.38), derived there from the
special conformal Ward identity); everything else is exact for symbolic
$J$. The symbolic checks are part of the reproduction code (\texttt{reproduce.py} and the scripts it calls).

\section{ABJM consistency check}
\label{sec:abjm}
Dictionary: $M^2=4/\alpha'$, $L^2/\alpha'=\pi\sqrt{2\lambda}$, so
$\Lambda\leftrightarrow ML=2^{5/4}\pi^{1/2}\lambda^{1/4}\approx4.2156\,\lambda^{1/4}$, at leading order in $1/(ML)$.
\emph{Higher-spin gap.} In the type-IIA 't~Hooft regime ($\lambda\gg1$, $g_s\to0$) the single traces of spin
$J\ge4$ are string states; the lightest is at the first massive level, whose highest spin is 4, with twist
$\approx ML-4$; supergravity Kaluza--Klein modes on $\mathbb{CP}^3$ have spin $\le2$ [D-sketch]. So $\Lambda$ is the
spin-4 gap of Refs.~\cite{CHLPS,CHMRSswamp}, and A1$'$ holds on its own: it constrains only spins $\ge4$, and allows
light spin-0, 2 single traces. \emph{No light single traces in $T\times T$} (a separate statement, needed only
for the scalar condition of item (2a) and to make the rows LS of Table~\ref{tab:inputs} vacuous). At tree level only the
stress-tensor multiplet is exchanged in the stress-tensor-multiplet four-point function: ``the only bulk fields
that contribute a meromorphic piece in the exchange diagrams are those in the stress tensor multiplet''
(Ref.~\cite{CPY}, $\mathcal N=8$), and the IIA tree-level amplitude of Ref.~\cite{BCP} has poles only for that
multiplet, through the $R^4$ order [Q]. Kaluza--Klein modes couple to two zero-mode gravitons at two derivatives
only through $\int\sqrt g\,\chi(y)=0$~\cite{CHLPS}, and the only multiplet member in $T\times T$ is $T$ (the others are
$R$-charged or have spin 1) [D-sketch]. For $\mathcal N=6$ the four-point function of the multiplet's bottom component
fixes only two of five superconformal structures, so the $\langle TTTT\rangle$ statement rests on the bulk
three-point argument [D-sketch]. Hence, at leading order in $1/\lambda$, ABJM-IIA satisfies A1$'$ and A2 (by the spin count alone) and the
condition of item (2a) (by this exchange argument, [D-sketch]).
\emph{What is bounded.} At tree level no Kaluza--Klein state is exchanged between zero modes, so the flat limit of
$\langle TTTT\rangle$ is the ten-dimensional IIA amplitude at zero internal momentum and
$g_4M^6/8\pi G_4=2\zeta(3)=2.404$, $\lambda$-independent~\cite[Sec.~3.8, Eq.~(3.43)]{CHMRSswamp}; Binder, Chester and Pufu~\cite{BCP}
derive this coefficient within ABJ(M) from localisation and the stress-tensor-multiplet four-point function, and
Chester, Pufu and Yin~\cite{CPY} in the M-theory regime (not covered here: no string scale); for the IIA regime
Ref.~\cite{CPY} is cited only for the exchange content, alongside Ref.~\cite{BCP}. $\hat g_3=0$ by
ten-dimensional supersymmetry. \emph{Placement.} $2.404/72.04=3.34\%$, $2.404/92.81=2.59\%$ ($\lambda=2.51\times10^7$ / $3.15\times10^9$ for $\Lambda=ML$); with twist$(J=4)\approx ML-4$, $(\Lambda/ML)^6$ shifts these by $\approx-11\%$ / $-3\%$ relative, so they are
meaningful to one digit. Against the ten-dimensional window $[0,3.000]$ (Ref.~\cite{CHMRSswamp}, Sec.~3.8, Eq.~(3.42)), $2\zeta(3)$ is at $80\%$; the small
fraction reflects the four-dimensional infrared logarithm, which is real for this sum rule. \emph{Status.} A
consistency check of a theory inside the assumptions [D-sketch for ``inside''], not evidence for positivity: (Pos)
is not shown for string states in AdS, and at $3\%$ of the upper end the check could fail only grossly. Loops
(finite $g_s$) are outside A2.
