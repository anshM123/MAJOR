\section{Grades, provenance, layers}

\textbf{Grades.} [P] proved; [certified] computer-assisted proof (interval arithmetic); [D]
derived; [D given X] derived from the inputs X (net grade = weakest of them); [D-sketch]
derivation sketched (at least one step asserted from a standard result without being written
out or checked); [DV], [D\,+\,DV], [P\,+\,DV] derived or proved and checked numerically;
[Q] quoted; [NE] numerical evidence; [NE\,+\,M] computed inside the near-gap model of
Sec.~\ref{sec:pos}; [NE\,+\,D-sketch] numerical evidence whose interpretation uses a sketched
step; [M] model (an approximation whose accuracy is itself a hypothesis); [A] assumption; [I]
open. A chain carries the grade of its weakest link, ordered
[P]\,$>$\,[DV]\,$>$\,[D]\,$>$\,[D-sketch]\,$>$\,[NE]\,$>$\,[M]\,$>$\,[A]\,$>$\,[I].

\textbf{Three layers.} (i) the setting A1, A2; (ii) the \emph{inputs} of the implication
(Table~\ref{tab:inputs}); (iii) per-state positivity, which the theorem assumes and for which
Sec.~\ref{sec:pos} gives the \emph{evidence}. Evidence is never used as an input.

\textbf{Provenance.} Every statement is quoted from the project's consolidated statement and
the analyses it cites; none of these is externally refereed. Two analyses are independent
reproductions with fresh code (Sec.~\ref{sec:repro}). One step, the reduction of the spin-2
(EE) per-$\nu$ kernel to scalar partial waves (Sec.~\ref{sec:HK}), is being checked at the
time of writing; it is marked ``EE step''. % EESTEP placeholder.

\subsection{The light side as one functional}
\label{sec:arc}
\emph{Definition.} For celestial angles $a\neq b$, the single-ordering swapped form is
$E^+[G](a,b)=\oint_C\oint_C t_1^wt_3^wf_kG$, and the block action is
$\omega_k[\mathcal O](a,b)=E^+[G^s_{\mathcal O}](a,b)+(-1)^kE^+[G^s_{\mathcal O}](b,a)$. The
functional is $\Omega_k[G]=\sum_{\mathcal O\in(12)}p_{\mathcal O}\,\omega_k[\mathcal O]$, the
de-shifted piece $\hat C^{ds}=\int d\nu\,\chi_\theta\int d\varphi_1d\varphi_3\,C_\nu\Omega_k/N_{\rm com}$.
This is CLS-D's and CHMRS's sum-rule object~\cite{CLSD,CHMRS}; heavy blocks enter it one at a time, and the light
side is $\sum_{\rm light}p_{\mathcal O}\omega_k[\mathcal O]$, by linearity the sum over tree terms
$X$ of the term-wise $s$-channel value $\Omega_k[X]$.

\emph{Mellin form.} With
$\prod X_{ij}^{-\delta_{ij}}=(X_{14}X_{23})^{-2}(U/V)^{-\delta_{12}}V^{\delta_{13}}$ and every
$X_{ij}$ on its principal branch on $C$ (Lemma C$'$), the kernel
$\mathcal K(\delta_{12},\delta_{13})=\oint_C\oint_C H\,(U/V)^{-\delta_{12}}V^{\delta_{13}}$ is entire
in $\delta_{12}$ because $|U/V|\le1$ on $C$. Three evaluations of one object: the fixed-angle
integral $D_{\rm LR}$ ($t$-integrals outside), the straight Mellin line, and the term-wise
$s$-channel sum (close $\delta_{12}$ to the left: double poles at $\delta_{12}=-n$, single-trace
poles). Cauchy at fixed $\delta_{13}$:
\begin{equation}
\text{line}-\text{term-wise}=-\oint_{\rm arc}\Gamma(\delta_{12})^2\Gamma(\delta_{14})^2M\,\mathcal K .
\end{equation}
After $1\leftrightarrow3$ (anti)symmetrisation the $D_{\rm LR}$ value of any microcausal term
vanishes as an integrand identity, so $\Omega_k[X]=\mathcal A_k[X]$, the arc. Along the arc $M$
grows like the Regge growth of $X$ and $\mathcal K$ like the corner behaviour of $H$: for Regge
spin $n_X<k$ the integrand is $o(|\delta|^{-1})$ and $\mathcal A_k=0$; at $n_X=k$ it is
$\sim c/\delta_{12}$ with $c\propto$ the top Regge coefficient. This is the position-space form
of CHMRS's light-side formula. [D given C$'$ and Mellin convergence.]

\emph{Checks [DV]} on a spinning family $T'=\sigma\otimes T_{\rm free}$, $G_\sigma$ an exact sum of
AdS$_4$ Witten diagrams: term-wise $=$ whole-function values to $10^{-11}$--$3\times10^{-5}$; a
borderline contact gives a finite light value ($-8.7642\times10^{-3}$ at one configuration)
equal to its top-Regge term to $\le7\times10^{-7}$; a borderline exchange (the analogue of the
graviton pole of $\Omega^{EE}$) matches its top-Regge term to $8\times10^{-8}$. At the borderline
the fixed-angle integral itself diverges (joint-corner exponent $-3$), so the earlier reading that
the $R^4$ value ``sits at the collinear set or in the $\nu$ integral'' is withdrawn.

\emph{Collinear set.} $|C_\nu(\varphi_1,\varphi_3)|\le A_{13}^{5/4}\bar K(\Delta\varphi)$,
$\bar K=2\log(1/\Delta\varphi)+8.69+o(1)$, uniformly in $\nu$; a light function growing like
$\Delta\varphi^{-p}$ pairs absolutely iff $p<\tfrac72$ [D]. For $J_0<4$ terms the light function
vanishes identically off $a=b$ [D]. For the $R^4$ arc the joint-corner collinear limit is the
light-cone edge of the Regge box, $\eta\ge1/\sin(\Delta\varphi/2)$ [P]; its integrability is
input L6$'$ [I].

\subsection{Lemma C\texorpdfstring{$'$}{'}}
\label{sec:Cprime}
Frame: $x_2=-e$, $x_4=e$, $x_3=t_3z(b)$, $x_1^+=-t_1z(a)$, $y=2\sin^2(\Delta\varphi/2)\in(0,2]$;
$X_{12}=2t_1-1$, $X_{34}=2t_3-1$, $X_{13}=2yt_1t_3$, $X_{14}=2t_1+1$, $X_{23}=2t_3+1$,
$X_{24}=4$. With $s_i=1/t_i$: $U=(2-s_1)(2-s_3)/(8y)$, $V=(2+s_1)(2+s_3)/(8y)$.

\emph{Lemma W [P].} For ${\rm Re}\,s_1,{\rm Re}\,s_3\ge0$ and $y\in(0,2]$, no root of
$\lambda^2-(1+U-V)\lambda+U$ lies in $[1,\infty)$. Proof: the exact identity
\begin{equation}
8y[\lambda^2-(1+U-V)\lambda+U]=(s_1+4\lambda-2)(s_3+4\lambda-2)-8(2-y)\lambda(\lambda-1)
\end{equation}
and $|s_i+4\lambda-2|\ge4\lambda-2$ give modulus of the product $\ge16\lambda(\lambda-1)+4>8(2-y)\lambda(\lambda-1)$
for $\lambda\ge1$. $\square$

\emph{Lemma C$'$ [D].} On
$\Omega=\{{\rm Re}\,t_1,{\rm Re}\,t_3>0;\ t_i\notin(0,\tfrac12]\}$ the swapped integrand
$t_1^wt_3^wf_kG$ is one single-valued analytic function, whose one-sided boundary values on
$t_i\in(0,\tfrac12)\pm i0$ are the four Wightman orderings of $\langle[T_4,T_3][T_1,T_2]\rangle$.
By Lemma W, $|\rho(z)|,|\rho(\bar z)|<1$ on $\Omega$, uniformly on compacts; the $s$-channel OPE
converges absolutely there for independent complex $\rho,\bar\rho$ [Q]; the only multivaluedness,
through $\log U$, is fixed by $L=\mathrm{Log}(2-s_1)+\mathrm{Log}(2-s_3)-\log8y$, analytic on
$\Omega$; Weierstrass gives analyticity. Tree-level terms: Witten diagrams are analytic on the
$s$-channel sheet away from $z,\bar z\in\{0,1,\infty\}$, the bulk-point singularity lies on the
sheet reached through $[1,\infty)$, which $\Omega$ never reaches. A closed-form consequence is
checked to $\le2\times10^{-9}$ [DV]. Lemma A (pointwise vanishing of $D_{\rm LR}$ on $J_0<4$ tree
terms) uses microcausality, endpoint convergence ($k>J_0$) and C$'$ only, so it holds for the
Einstein term as a derivation; its mechanism is checked at $O(1/c_T)$ on the free scalar
($\le8\times10^{-15}$), the free fermion ($\le3.8\times10^{-15}$) and the Witten family above
($10^{-14}$--$2\times10^{-12}$; controls fail at $O(1)$) [DV].

\subsection{The \texorpdfstring{$R^4$}{R4} normalisation by calibration, and (P)}
\label{sec:F}
Add a heavy scalar $\chi$ of mass $m$ with $\mathcal L\supset c\,\chi(W^+)^2+\bar c\,\chi(W^-)^2$.
(i) Its exchange $W_\chi$ has flat image $f_\chi\propto|c|^2/(m^2-t)$ (MHV) and all-plus part
$\propto c^2\sum_xx^4/(m^2-x)$: Regge spin $3<4$ [D]. (ii) Lemma A, term-wise:
$p_\chi\hat C_4^{ds}[G_\chi]+\hat C_4^{ds}[{\rm DT}(W_\chi)]=0$. (iii) Heavy dictionary: for
$J=0$, $P_{-1/2}(1)=1$ and $p_\chi\hat C_4^{ds}[G_\chi]=2u^2|c^{++}_\chi|^2(1+O(m^{-2}))=2g_4^\chi(1+O(m^{-2}))$
(CLS-D's crystal dictionary with the heavy normalisation of $\Omega^Q$; flat $B_4^{(1)}$ sum rule).

\subsection{The interchange \texorpdfstring{$H_K$}{HK} by Tonelli with cut-off weights}
\label{sec:HK}
\emph{Uniform Regge bound (R1$'$).} In the frame $x_4=(\rho,\bar\rho)=-x_3$, $x_1=(1,1)=-x_2$,
$\rho_b:=1/\bar\rho$, $r=\sqrt{\rho\rho_b}$, $\eta=(\rho+\rho_b)/2r$. Rindler positivity and
Cauchy--Schwarz bound the Regge-sheet correlator by first-sheet comparison correlators at the
same cross-ratios; their OPE converges uniformly on the closed box $\bar B_c$ including its edges,
and the identity pieces with aligned polarisations scale as the generic normalisation, so
$|{\rm dDisc}\,\mathcal G|\le2B(c)$ with edge exponent $0$ [D]. KKSDZ's caveat sits at
$\bar\rho=1$, outside $B_c$ [P]. Uniformity holds only in this Rindler ($r^0$) form, not with a
true intercept $J_0<1$ [DV].

\emph{Corner count.} If $|K|\lesssim r^a\eta^c$ on $B_c$, R1$'$ gives absolute convergence of the
corner integral iff $c<a+1-J_0$, and R3 transfers it to $\sum_{\mathcal O}$. $a=0$ for $k=2,3$,
$a=2$ for $k=4,5$.

\emph{No-go for grid weights [P].} A weight of compact $\nu$-support has an entire Fourier transform
of exponential type, so its leading-Regge image grows at the raw rate $e^{\beta/2}$ ($k=2$) or
$e^{5\beta/2}$ ($k=4$): $c=\tfrac12$, $\tfrac52$, requiring $J_0<\tfrac12$. The stored grid weights
follow this pattern beyond $\beta\approx25$ (D) and $42$ (Q) [NE].

\emph{Strip analyticity from the de-shift identity [D].} For $k=2$ write $f=\chi h_-$ on $\nu>0$,
$f(-\nu)=\chi h_+$, $E(t)=\tfrac12[e^t\hat f(t)+e^{-t}\hat f(-t)]$. $E=\Phi$ on $t\ge0$ gives
$g(t):=e^t\hat f(t)=2\Phi(t)-e^{-t}\hat f(-t)$, bounded on $t\ge0$, $\le e^t\|f\|_1$ on $t<0$,
$O(t^{-2})$ at $+\infty$; hence $f$ continues to $-1<{\rm Im}\,\nu<0$ with uniformly $L^1$ lines.
For $k=4$, $G(t)=e^{3t}F_{-3}+e^tF_{-1}$ with $W=\tfrac12[G(t)+G(-t)]$ gives analyticity of
$\chi R_{-3}$ in $-2<{\rm Im}\,\nu<0$. \emph{Cutoff residual} (shift $\mathbb R\to\mathbb R-i$):
$|I_\theta-I|\le\varepsilon_\theta P_{-1/2}(\cosh\beta)$ for $k=2$ [D]. For the published $k=4,5$
weights the identity holds to $\varepsilon_W$ for all $t$, so $|I-{\rm target}|\le\varepsilon_WP_{-1/2}$
directly [DV].

(2,4) factor is the three-point structure of an operator of dimension 1 and spin $l=-\tfrac12+i\nu$,

$N(\nu)r^2\hat G_{1,l}(\rho,\bar\rho)$, a continuous-spin Casimir eigenfunction normalised to

\subsection{The de-shift identity in shifted form and the published weights}

zeros of $R_j$, a pole of $u$ at $\pm ij_{\max}$, and uniform decay, gives for every real $t$

\textbf{$H_{\rm RS}$ (preliminary).} (i) \emph{Contiguity [D].} To every order in $1/\Delta$ the
integrand of the $1/\Delta$ expansion is a product of one-variable twisted local systems (four radial,
$s$, $\varphi$, $\alpha$) times a Laurent polynomial; integration by parts reduces every monomial to a
rational, cycle-independent multiple of the base integral ($s$- and $\varphi$-odd forms are exact; the
$\alpha$-odd class is not populated). So the integral over any closed cycle of this local system is
$c_\Gamma(\nu)\times$(expansion) order by order, and $c_\Gamma$ cancels in the state/reference ratio.
The hypotheses (same local system for scalar and EE, no interior poles, no integer exponents, no
boundary layers) are checked to $a^4$--$a^5$ [DV]. (ii) \emph{Far region.} The EE integrand is
$a\,Q\times$(scalar integrand) with $Q$ single-valued rational and every pole of $Q$ on a singular
divisor of the scalar [P\,+\,DV]. On the real section cut at the zeros of $B$ ($D'$) the far region is
$O(\Delta^Ne^{-c\Delta})$, $c=2\,{\rm artanh}(r/2)\ge0.336$ [D]; integrated fits agree with this within
$3\%$ [DV]. On the saddle-point thimbles the far EE weight at raw radius $r\ge0.3$ is $\le e^{-66}$ (300)
and $\le e^{-277}$ (1000) pointwise; after the thimble volume factor ($\lesssim e^{40}$ at 300) this is
$\lesssim e^{-26}$, against failure thresholds $e^{-8.5}$ / $e^{-10.8}$ [NE\,+\,D-sketch]. (The $r\ge0.2$
figure $e^{-37}$ does not survive the volume factor and is not used.) (iii) \emph{What remains:}
$P_{\rm cyc}$. Truncation: $|\Delta s_d|\le0.015$ / $0.0015$, a factor $\ge85$ inside the margin. A side
finding: at $\nu\lesssim2$ the untruncated scalar thimble has an angular end on which the EE integrand
does not decay, which plausibly explains an earlier loss of signal in direct checks below
$\nu\approx2$; $D'$ is admissible. Coverage: $++$ $J=0,2$ and OO $J=0$; the other measured channels use

\section{The \texorpdfstring{$\Delta_g\ge300$}{Delta g >= 300} corollary}
\label{sec:cor}

\textbf{Statement} [D-sketch\,+\,M]. Assume (i) A1, A2 and the inputs of Table~\ref{tab:inputs} at
every $\Delta_g\ge300$; (ii) (Pos) at $\Delta_g=300$ for the functional in question, which at 300
includes the open window of $H_{\rm LJ}(300)$ [I]; (iii) $H_{\rm mono}$: at fixed $(J,m/\Lambda)$ the
beyond-leading-Regge correction (in $c_2$) grows at most like $m$, and in the Regge window it is
bounded uniformly in $\Delta_g$ [M]; (iv) $H_{\rm cont}$ for states between the 300 and $\Delta_g$
twist edges [NE]; (v) $H_{\rm LJ}(\Delta_g)$ [D-sketch\,+\,M]. Then (Pos) holds at every
$\Delta_g\ge300$, and Table~\ref{tab:dg} gives bounds there.

\textbf{Support} (proxies reusing the computed weights and corrections, validated on the
100/300/1000 verdicts). A correction fixed in $x=\nu/m$ costs $\propto\Lambda^{-2}$, one fixed in $\nu$
at small $\nu$ $\propto\Lambda^{-4}$ [D for the weights]; empirically $p=2.83$ (range $1.4$--$3.2$) between
300 and 1000 [NE]. The binding baseline decreases monotonically to the flat $0.200$. The large-spin
window tolerance grows like $\Lambda^{1.0\text{--}1.2}$. $\Omega^Q$'s binding state is $\Delta_g$-stable:
$s_d\approx0.5076+147/\Delta^2$. The sign-free window bound closes from $\Delta_g\approx275$ only with the
[D-sketch] refinement (margin $\times1.1$ at 300) and from $\approx440$ without it; ``300 is the lowest gap
where the method closes'' holds only with the refinement. It is not a proof. Nothing is claimed below
300; at $\Delta_g=100$ positivity fails at model level for near-gap $J\gtrsim18$.

\section{Normalisation check (ABJM)}
Dictionary: $M^2=4/\alpha'$, $L^2/\alpha'=\pi\sqrt{2\lambda}$, so
$\Lambda\leftrightarrow ML=2^{5/4}\pi^{1/2}\lambda^{1/4}\approx4.2156\,\lambda^{1/4}$, at leading
order in $1/(ML)$ (the lowest string states have spin up to 4 and twist $\approx ML-4$). The type~IIA
$R^4$ term $\zeta(3)\alpha'^3/32$ gives $g_4M^6/8\pi G=2\zeta(3)=2.404$, $\lambda$-independent, the
normalisation of Ref.~\cite{CHMRSswamp}; Binder, Chester and Pufu~\cite{BCPW} derive this coefficient within
ABJ(M) from localisation and the stress-tensor-multiplet four-point function in the IIA regime, and
Chester, Pufu and Yin~\cite{CPY} in the M-theory regime (where $\Lambda\leftrightarrow ML$ is undefined). Our $\tilde
g_4$ is a different CFT object; identifying the two needs (F), the restriction of the 10d amplitude to 4d
graviton kinematics, and supersymmetry. Placement: $2.404/72.04=3.34\%$, $2.404/92.81=2.59\%$, mostly
reflecting the 4d infrared logarithm; against the 10d window $[0,3.000]$, $2\zeta(3)$ is at $80\%$.
$\hat g_3=0$ by 10d type~IIA supersymmetry. ABJM violates A1 (light KK and R-singlet single traces), so
this is a check of sign and normalisation, not evidence.
