\begin{abstract}
For holographic CFT$_3$s with large $c_T$ and single-trace twist gap $\Delta_g$,
light-ray sum rules on $\langle TTTT\rangle$ give a conditional theorem: given listed
analytic inputs, if three fixed functionals act positively on heavy states, then
$\sqrt{t_4^2+\alpha_T^2}\le \CubicHead$ and
$-O(\Lambda^{-2})\le \tilde g_4\Lambda^6/8\pi G\le \QuartHead$ at $\Delta_g=\DgHead$, up to one
uncomputed light-side term, with explicit dependence on $\Lambda\simeq\Delta_g-\tfrac32$.
Positivity is computed on finite sets of states and assumed elsewhere through named
hypotheses. The ABJM value $2\zeta(3)$ lies at $\ABJMHead\%$ of the upper end.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph*{Introduction.}
A conformal field theory with a large central charge and a large gap in its spectrum of
single-trace operators is expected to have a local, weakly coupled gravitational dual whose
low-energy action is Einstein gravity plus higher-derivative corrections suppressed by the
gap~\cite{HPPS}. For the stress tensor itself this expectation has been made parametric:
causality forces the non-Einstein $\langle TTT\rangle$ structures to vanish as the gap
grows~\cite{CEMZ,AHKT,MeltzerPerlmutter,CHP}, in three dimensions as
$t_4\lesssim \ln\Delta_g/\Delta_g^4$ with an unknown constant~\cite{AKT}. For scalar
correlators, Caron-Huot, Mazáč, Rastelli and Simmons-Duffin (CHMRS) turned flat-space
dispersive bounds into two-sided $\log\Delta_g$ bounds on AdS$_4$ contact couplings with
explicit constants~\cite{CHMRS}. For stress tensors, Chang, Landau and Simmons-Duffin (CLS-D)
constructed spinning light-ray sum rules on $\langle TTTT\rangle$ and their dictionary to
flat-space graviton sum rules in the bulk-point limit, and left ``the remaining steps for
uplifting flat space bounds to AdS'' as future work~\cite{CLSD}. Flat four-dimensional
graviton bounds with an infrared logarithm are given in Ref.~\cite{CHLPS}.

This Letter reports an explicit-constant, \emph{conditional} execution of that uplift for the
cubic ($R^3$) and quartic ($R^4$) graviton couplings in AdS$_4$/CFT$_3$. The method is CLS-D's
and CHMRS's; our ingredients are a dual tensor structure that removes a curvature-induced
helicity mixing, de-shifted higher-$k$ functionals, a pointwise annihilation lemma for
low-spin tree terms, and computations of the heavy-state action near the gap.

We state at the outset what is and is not established. The implication ``positivity of three
fixed functionals on heavy states $\Rightarrow$ bounds'' is derived, given a list of analytic
inputs that are themselves derived, sketched, quoted or assumed (Table~\ref{tab:hyp}). Its
constants are computed and have been reproduced independently. Per-state positivity is
\emph{not} established in general: it is computed on finite sets of heavy states inside
controlled models and assumed elsewhere through named hypotheses. The result is a conditional
theorem, not an unconditional bound. Throughout, grades are [P] proved, [certified]
computer-assisted, [D] derived, [D-sketch] derivation sketched, [DV] derived and checked
numerically, [Q] quoted, [NE] numerical evidence, [M] model, [A] assumption, [I] open; a
chain carries the grade of its weakest link. \HLJstatus

\paragraph*{Setting.}
Let $\mathcal T$ be a unitary CFT$_3$, parity-even or parity-violating, with a unique stress
tensor $T$. We normalise $c_T$ so that a free real scalar has $c_T=1$~\cite{OsbornPetkou};
Einstein gravity then has $c_T=32L^2/(\pi G)$, and we set $8\pi G:=256/c_T$ (AdS units,
$L=1$). The two standing assumptions are
\begin{itemize}
\item[A1] (gap) every single-trace operator other than $T$ has twist $\Delta-J\ge\Delta_g$, for
  all $J$ including $J=0,1,2$;
\item[A2] (tree level) we work at $O(1/c_T)$ with $c_T\gg\Delta_g^2$; loops are dropped.
\end{itemize}
Heavy states $(\Delta,J)$ carry the AdS mass $m^2=(\Delta-J-2)(\Delta+J-1)$, and the gap
scale is
\begin{equation}
\Lambda:=\sqrt{(\Delta_g-2)(\Delta_g-1)}=\Delta_g-\tfrac32-\tfrac{1}{8\Delta_g}+O(\Delta_g^{-2}),
\label{eq:Lambda}
\end{equation}
i.e.\ $\Lambda=298.50$ ($\Delta_g=300$) and $998.50$ ($\Delta_g=1000$).

We write $\langle TTT\rangle$ in the basis $(c_T,t_4,\alpha_T)$~\cite{HofmanMaldacena,Buchel};
in the bulk $\sqrt{t_4^2+\alpha_T^2}=240|\hat g_3|$, with $\hat g_3$ the $R^3$ coupling in
the normalisation of Ref.~\cite{CHLPS}. The quartic coupling $g_4$ is the flat $R^4$
coefficient normalised so that the type~II Virasoro--Shapiro amplitude has
$g_4/(8\pi G)=2\zeta(3)/M^6$, $M^2=4/\alpha'$. Its CFT counterpart $\tilde g_4$ is
\emph{defined} by the light side of the de-shifted $k=4$ functional below; $\tilde g_4=g_4$
holds given the flat-limit hypothesis (F)+(P).

\paragraph*{Method in brief.}
\emph{Light-ray sum rules.} CLS-D's functional $\Psi_{k,\nu,j'}$ integrates the commutator of
two light-transformed stress tensors against a dual three-point structure, at Regge spin $k$
and celestial frequency $\nu$~\cite{CLSD}. For $k$ above the Regge intercept the
non-perturbative correlator is annihilated, which we take from the Regge bound of
Kologlu, Kravchuk, Simmons-Duffin and Zhiboedov (KKSDZ)~\cite{KKSDZ} with its stated loophole
(input K1). Splitting the OPE into light (identity, $T$, double traces) and heavy
($\Delta-J\ge\Delta_g$) parts gives, for every admissible functional $\Omega$,
\begin{equation}
\sum_{\rm heavy}\Omega[\mathcal O_{\Delta,J}]=-\,\Omega[\text{light}] .
\label{eq:sumrule}
\end{equation}
If $\Omega$ is non-negative on every heavy block, the light side is bounded. The interchange of
the heavy sum with the corner and $\nu$ integrations, $H_K$, is sketched [D-sketch].

\emph{The EE functional (cubic).} At $k=2,3$ we use the dual $(2,4)$ structure
$EE=\big[V_{0,42}^2(H_{24}-2V_{2,04}V_{4,02})/(X_{20}X_{40})\big]^2$. Its leading-Regge heavy
action is helicity-diagonal: the curvature-induced helicity off-diagonal of the na\"ive
structure vanishes identically [P]. On CLS-D's physical polarisation plane at the bulk-point
saddle, $EE$ coincides with the flat-space structure [P], so the bulk-point limit of the
functional is a flat functional whose positivity is certified (minimum $0.200$ in units of the
per-state normalisation [certified]; one corner float-checked only); the identification is
[P given the polarisation map] + [NE]. The functional is
$\Omega^{EE}=\int_0^1\!dp\,\psi_2\Lambda^4\hat C'_{EE,\Lambda p}
+\int_0^1\!dp\,\psi_3\tfrac12\Lambda^6\hat C_{3,\Lambda p}$, with fixed polynomial weights
$\psi_{2,3}$ normalised to $k(0)=1$. The light side consists of the identity and the single
$T$ block: unsubtracted $k=2,3$ functionals put every $[TT]$ double trace at a double zero and
drop contacts (L0, [D]), and the graviton pole is exact,
$-\hat C'_{EE,\nu}|_{E}=16\pi G/(\nu^2+9/4)$ (H7, [D]).

\emph{De-shift.} The heavy image of $\Psi_{k,\nu}$ is, at leading Regge order, a known kernel
times a Regge harmonic. We invert that kernel (e.g.\
$\hat C'_{EE}:=c(\nu)^{-1}\mathcal A_g^{-1}[\Psi_{EE}]$, via a generalised Abel equation in a
minimal-norm gauge) so that the bulk-point limit of each AdS functional is a prescribed flat
functional. Existence of the de-shift is [D] for $k=2$ and [NE] for the $k=4,5$ continuum
solutions.

\emph{The Q functionals (quartic).} The upper functional $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$ realises a fixed
flat functional of margin $\delta=0.2$ (log coefficient $17.241$) by de-shifted
$\hat C_4^{ds},\partial\hat C_4^{ds},\hat C_5^{ds}$ plus smeared $k=2,3$ parts; the lower
functional $\Omega^Q_{\rm low}$ is the de-shifted $k=4$ value piece, whose leading-Regge
heavy image $P_{-1/2}(\cosh\beta)$ is positive. At $k=4,5$ the low-spin tree terms must drop
out of the light side. \emph{Lemma A} [D]: every tree-level term with Regge
intercept $J_0<4$ (Einstein, $E\times W^3$, mean-field theory) is annihilated pointwise by
the swapped-form $\Psi_4,\Psi_5$ at non-collinear angles $\varphi_1\ne\varphi_3$.
\emph{D$_{\rm LR}$} [D] fixes the integration domain to CLS-D's $x_{13}^2>0$ with the
cone-preserving prescription, on which the collinear set carries nothing. The light side is
\emph{defined} as D$_{\rm LR}$ on the $J_0<4$ terms plus the term-wise flat-limit value
(F)+(P) on the $J_0\ge4$ terms ($R^4$ contact, heavy-exchange double traces); it is not
D$_{\rm LR}$ applied to the full correlator, which would assign the $R^4$ term the value zero
[D-sketch]. This is how $\tilde g_4$ is defined: $-\hat C_4^{ds}|_{\rm light}=2\tilde g_4$.
The equality of term-wise and actual double-trace values is $H_{\rm EFT}$.

\emph{Per-state positivity.} For each twist-allowed heavy primary the functional acts as a
small Hermitian matrix in helicity and parity; positivity means this matrix is positive
semidefinite. Away from the flat limit the action receives curvature and beyond-leading-Regge
corrections, which we compute from the leading-Regge action plus saddle-point
(``crystal-cycle'') evaluations of the light-ray integrals, organised in near-gap chains and
a hybrid model.

\paragraph*{Result.}
\textbf{Theorem (conditional).} \emph{Let $\mathcal T$ satisfy A1 and A2 and the analytic
inputs of Table~\ref{tab:hyp}. Assume that $\Omega^{EE}$, $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$ and
$\Omega^Q_{\rm low}$ act positively on every twist-allowed heavy block. Let $c_1$ be the
(uncomputed, free) coefficient of a possible $\nu$-independent $E\times W^3$ light term
$16\pi G\,c_1t_4$. Then:}

\emph{(1) Cubic.} With $L_A=\int_0^1\psi_2(p)\,dp/(p^2+9/4\Lambda^2)$,
\begin{equation}
\sqrt{t_4^2+\alpha_T^2}\le\frac{240}{\Lambda^4}\sqrt{\frac{16L_A}{k(0)(1+\delta_3)}}\,
\big(1+O(c_1\Lambda^{-2})\big),
\label{eq:cubic}
\end{equation}
\emph{where $\delta_3=O(\Lambda^{-2})$ collects the curvature correction to the $|\hat g_3|^2$
light coefficient and its H17 remainder. Numerically, in AdS units,
$\sqrt{t_4^2+\alpha_T^2}\le3.13\times10^{-6}$ at $\Delta_g=300$ and $\le2.89\times10^{-8}$ at
$\Delta_g=1000$, shifted by $\mp0.22\%$ / $\mp0.017\%$ per unit $c_1$ and by $\pm0.24\%$ /
$\pm0.021\%$ from H17. Equivalently $|\hat g_3|^2\Lambda^8\le1.07\times10^4$ /
$1.43\times10^4$.}

\emph{(2) Quartic. If in addition either $t_4=\alpha_T=0$, or $\kappa\ge0$ (upper end) and
$c_L>0$ (lower end), then}
\begin{equation}
-O(\Lambda^{-2})\le\frac{\tilde g_4\Lambda^6}{8\pi G}\le 17.241\ln\frac{2\Lambda}{3}
+B_{\rm eff}+\varepsilon_{c_1},
\label{eq:quartic}
\end{equation}
\emph{with $B_{\rm eff}=-19.220$ ($\Delta_g=300$) and $-19.265$ ($\Delta_g=1000$), i.e.\ an
upper end $72.04$ / $92.81$, and $|\varepsilon_{c_1}|=2.03\,|c_1t_4|\Lambda^2$. Using (1) to
bound $t_4$ gives $|\varepsilon_{c_1}|\le0.57|c_1|$ / $0.059|c_1|$. For $\hat g_3\ne0$ and
$\kappa\ge0$ the additional term $-\kappa|\hat g_3|^2\Lambda^8$ ($\kappa=0.110$) only
tightens the upper end.}

\emph{Grade.} The implication is [D] given the inputs; the constants are [DV]. Item (1) uses
only $\Omega^{EE}$ and needs neither Lemma A, (F)+(P), $H_{\rm EFT}$ nor anything forward.
Item (2) needs item (1) only through $\varepsilon_{c_1}$; if (1) is used for that purpose,
(2) inherits the positivity hypotheses of (1) at that $\Delta_g$ (at $300$ including
$H_{\rm LJ}(300)$), which carrying $c_1t_4$ explicitly avoids.
$\kappa\ge0$ and $c_L>0$ are [D-sketch + M]; the measured lower slope is positive
($0.255$--$0.49$). Item (2) gives no cubic closure (no bound on $|\hat g_3|^2$ and none on the
ratio $|\hat g_3|^2/g_4$), and none is claimed.

\paragraph*{Dependence on the gap.}
With the functionals held fixed, the light side is an exact function of $\Lambda$, because
the graviton pole $1/(p^2+9/4\Lambda^2)$ integrates in closed form against polynomial
weights [DV]:
\begin{align}
\sqrt{t_4^2+\alpha_T^2}&\le\frac{1.3194\times10^4}{\Lambda^4}
\nonumber\\&\quad\times\sqrt{\ln\frac{\Lambda}{8.7415}+\frac{6.384}{\Lambda}+O\Big(\frac{\ln\Lambda}{\Lambda^2}\Big)},
\label{eq:cubicL}\\
\frac{\tilde g_4\Lambda^6}{8\pi G}&\le17.2407\ln\frac{2\Lambda}{3}-19.2782+\frac{10.314}{\Lambda}
\nonumber\\&\quad+\frac{860.3\ln(2\Lambda/3)-2508.6}{\Lambda^2}+O\Big(\frac{\ln\Lambda}{\Lambda^{3}}\Big)
\nonumber\\&\quad+\varepsilon_{c_1}.
\label{eq:quarticL}
\end{align}
The exact closed forms reproduce every quoted digit at $\Delta_g=300$ and $1000$; the
truncated forms shown have relative error $\lesssim5\times10^{-5}$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:cubicL})
and absolute error $\le10^{-3}$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:quarticL}) there. Eq.~\eqref{eq:cubicL} is to be multiplied by the $c_1$ and H17
factors of Eq.~\eqref{eq:cubic}. The drift of $B_{\rm eff}$ from $-19.220$ to $-19.265$ is the
finite-$\Lambda$ tail of the AdS pole; it converges to the flat constant $-19.2782$. The
error terms are also explicit: $\mp19941/(\Lambda^2\sqrt{16L_A})$ per unit $c_1$ on the
cubic bound, $\pm212.5/\Lambda^2$ from H17 [M], and
$|\varepsilon_{c_1}|\le487.7\sqrt{16L_A}\,|c_1|/\Lambda^2$. Equation~\eqref{eq:cubicL} scales
as $\sqrt{\ln\Lambda}/\Lambda^4$; replacing $\Lambda$ by $\Delta_g$ is not innocuous at
$\Delta_g=300$. Equations~\eqref{eq:cubicL}--\eqref{eq:quarticL} are values of the
\emph{implication}; they are bounds at a given $\Delta_g$ only if positivity holds there.

\paragraph*{Status of per-state positivity.}
\emph{Result (1).} The $\Omega^{EE}$ action is computed [NE + M] at $\Delta_g=1000$ on even
$J\le24$, $28$, $32$, $36$, $40$, $48$ and odd $J\le31$, and at $\Delta_g=300$ on even
$J\le24$, $28$, $32$ and odd $J\le15$, for masses from the twist edge to $5\Lambda$. The
minimum is always the parity-odd $J=5$ state at the twist edge: $+0.2038$ at $1.0166\Lambda$
($\Delta_g=300$) and $+0.2009$ at $1.00499\Lambda$ ($\Delta_g=1000$), against the flat
$0.200$. The most fragile computed state at $300$ is $J=32$ ($+0.783$). Outside the computed
sets positivity rests on the hypotheses of Table~\ref{tab:hyp}, chiefly the near-gap
large-spin hypothesis $H_{\rm LJ}(\Delta_g)$.

\emph{Result (2).} The binding pair is $++$ and parity-odd $J=0$ at the gap. Its margin is
controlled by one number, the derivative-piece coefficient $s_d$ of the beyond-leading-Regge
correction: $s_d=+0.5096$ ($300$) and $+0.5077$ ($1000$), against a failure point
$s_{\rm fail}=1.78$ (margin $\approx3.5$). The binding action is $+0.1427$ / $+0.1428$ in
units of the normalisation (full-set minimum $+0.1426\pm0.0017$). The measured channels are
$++$ at $J=0$--$8$ (even), parity-odd $J=0,2$ and $+-$ at $J=4$--$9,11$; every unmeasured
channel needs $|s_d|\ge17$--$30\times$ the measured value to fail. The decisive
input was $H_{\rm RS}$: that the analytic $1/\Delta$ expansion (``engine'') used for $s_d$
equals the physical crystal-cycle integral at small $\nu$. Two recent analyses, not yet
reviewed, reduce it to a single cycle-class premise. First, at every order in $1/\Delta$ the
integrand is one product of one-variable twisted local systems times a polynomial;
integration by parts makes each order cycle-independent up to one constant that cancels in
the state/reference ratio [D], with all hypotheses checked on the engine's pieces [DV].
Second, the far region carries weight $O(\Delta^Ne^{-c\Delta})$ with $c=2\,{\rm
artanh}(r/2)\ge0.336$ on the cut real cycle [D]; on the actual thimbles it is $\le e^{-37}$
($300$) and $\le e^{-171}$ ($1000$) against failure thresholds $e^{-8.5}$ / $e^{-10.8}$
[NE + D-sketch]. Since the EE integrand is the scalar one times a rational function with no
new divisor [P + DV], what remains is the premise $P_{\rm cyc}$ that the physical cycle lies in the
class of the real cycle; it is shared with the scalar integrand, where it is checked through
its periods [DV]. Hence $H_{\rm RS}$ is [D given $P_{\rm cyc}$], with truncation error
$|\Delta s_d|\le0.015$ / $0.0015$, a factor $\ge85$ inside the margin.

\emph{Independent reproduction.} Fresh code reproduces, within stated precision, the cubic
bounds ($3.13206\times10^{-6}$ / $2.89126\times10^{-8}$), $2L_{\rm AdS}=72.0406$ / $92.8135$,
the $\Lambda$-forms, all error terms, the ABJM placement, D's binding states at the level of
the hybrid model, and Q's binding value given the engine ratio. A separate direct
seven-dimensional quadrature of the exact integrand at finite $\Delta$ reproduces the engine ratio to $\le3.4\times10^{-7}$ / $3\times10^{-8}$
and $s_d=+0.50967$ / $+0.50776$; failure would need a worst-sign shape error of the ratio
about $600\times$ the measured difference. Not reproduced independently: the Abel de-shift
weights, the beyond-Regge correction to D's binding state, the flat certificate (grid check
only) and the full $\Omega^Q$ state scan (details in the Supplemental Material).

\emph{Weakest links.} \HLJweakest. The other open or weakest inputs are the unevaluated
Einstein instance of Lemma A [I]; $H_{\rm EFT}$(b) [A]; $H_K$ and (F)+(P) [D-sketch]; the
KKSDZ loophole; and the model-level items. Lemma A is structural and linear. Its mechanism is
[DV] at $O(1/c_T)$ on the exact free-scalar and free-fermion $\langle TTTT\rangle$
(residuals $\le8\times10^{-15}$) and on interacting spinning correlators built from exact
AdS$_4$ Witten diagrams, including a massive spin-2 exchange with $J_0=2$ in all channels
(residuals $10^{-14}$--$2\times10^{-12}$; controls fail at $O(1)$). Not exercised are the
graviton's own twist $\tau=1$, its tensor structures, and the exact Einstein coefficients; an
explicit tree-level $\langle TTTT\rangle$ exists only in momentum space~\cite{Raju,AlbayrakKharel},
and its light-ray evaluation, e.g.\ in the helicity basis of Ref.~\cite{CaronHuotLi}, is open.

\paragraph*{Gaps above 300.}
Only $\Delta_g=300$ and $1000$ have been computed. A proxy analysis that reuses the computed
weights and corrections finds every correction-driven margin improving monotonically with
$\Delta_g$; the binding baseline decreases monotonically to the certified flat $0.200$; and
the large-spin window tolerance grows like $\Lambda^{1.0\text{--}1.2}$ [D for the weights,
M for the corrections]. This motivates a \textbf{corollary (conditional):} \emph{assume in
addition $H_{\rm mono}$ (at fixed $(J,m/\Lambda)$ the beyond-leading-Regge correction grows
at most like $m$, and in the Regge window it is bounded uniformly in $\Delta_g$) and
$H_{\rm cont}$ (continuity in $m/\Lambda$ for near-edge states without a $\Delta_g=300$
counterpart). Then positivity, and hence Eqs.~\eqref{eq:cubicL}--\eqref{eq:quarticL} with
their error terms, hold for every $\Delta_g\ge300$} [D-sketch given $H_{\rm mono}$,
$H_{\rm cont}$ and $H_{\rm LJ}(\Delta_g)$; $H_{\rm mono}$ is M]. The corollary inherits
\HLJcorollary. It is not a proof: the corrections are measured only where direct data exist.
Below $300$ nothing is claimed. Three hundred is the lowest gap at which the present method
closes, and only marginally; at $\Delta_g=100$ positivity already fails at model level for
near-gap large spins.

\paragraph*{ABJM consistency check.}
In ABJM theory~\cite{ABJM} at strong coupling, $M^2=4/\alpha'$ and $L^2/\alpha'=\pi\sqrt{2\lambda}$
give $\Lambda\leftrightarrow ML=2^{5/4}\pi^{1/2}\lambda^{1/4}\approx4.22\,\lambda^{1/4}$, valid at
leading order in $1/(ML)$. The type~IIA $R^4$ term $\zeta(3)\alpha'^3/32$ gives
$g_4M^6/(8\pi G)=2\zeta(3)=2.404$, independent of $\lambda$. This sits at $3.3\%$
($\Delta_g=300$) and $2.6\%$ ($\Delta_g=1000$) of the upper end of Eq.~\eqref{eq:quartic},
and is consistent with the lower end. The cubic coupling vanishes, $\hat g_3=0$, because ten-dimensional
type~IIA supersymmetry forbids an $R^3$ term; this does not follow from ``maximal''
supersymmetry, since ABJM at Chern--Simons level $k>2$ has $\mathcal N=6$. The placement
does not involve $c_T$: $2\zeta(3)$ is $\lambda$-independent and $\Lambda$ depends only on
$\lambda$. (Mass derivatives of the sphere free energy give $c_T$ only when the mass couples
to a current in the stress-tensor multiplet~\cite{ACP,BCPW}; in an $\mathcal N=2$ theory they
give a flavour-current coefficient, which must not be confused with $c_T$~\cite{Fmax}.)
ABJM violates A1 through its light Kaluza--Klein modes, and the check uses only the flat
image (F). It is a consistency check, not evidence for the theorem.

\paragraph*{Discussion.}
Under the stated hypotheses, the stress-tensor four-point function of a large-gap holographic
CFT$_3$ forces the bulk effective action to agree with Einstein gravity through quartic order
in derivatives, with explicit constants: the $\langle TTT\rangle$ deviations are bounded by
$1.32\times10^4\sqrt{\ln(\Lambda/8.74)}/\Lambda^4$, and the $R^4$ coefficient lies in a
window between $-O(\Lambda^{-2})$ and $17.24\ln(\Lambda/4.59)+O(1/\Lambda)$ in units of
$8\pi G/\Lambda^6$. The parametric form
of Eq.~\eqref{eq:cubicL} differs from the $\ln\Delta_g/\Delta_g^4$ of Ref.~\cite{AKT}, and the
constants are not comparable. Rewritten as $|\hat g_3|^2\Lambda^8\le3022.34\ln(2\Lambda/3)-5327.21+O(1/\Lambda)$,
item (1) is about ten times weaker than the transplanted flat optimum of Ref.~\cite{CHLPS};
that optimum would need forward and helicity-flip sum rules in AdS, which are not available.
Literature searches (abstract level, plus one extended search) found no published bound on
$R^3$ or $R^4$ couplings from
$\langle TTTT\rangle$ sum rules, so novelty is asserted only at search level; the relation to
gap-scaling arguments for higher-derivative couplings~\cite{Kundu} and to the $\mathcal N=6$
numerical bootstrap~\cite{BCJP} remains to be discussed in detail. % TODO: expand if space allows.

What is \emph{not} claimed: an unconditional bound; any statement below $\Delta_g=300$, at
loop level, or for theories that violate A1; a cubic closure $|\hat g_3|^2\Lambda^8\le\dots$
or a ratio $|\hat g_3|^2/g_4$ from item (2); and the evaluation of Lemma A on the actual
Einstein correlator. The most valuable next steps are graviton window data at $\Delta_g=300$
for $J\ge34$ together with a validated below-fold method, one swapped-form $\Psi_4$ on the
Einstein $\langle TTTT\rangle$, a proof of $P_{\rm cyc}$, a spinning $O(r)$ Regge kernel near
the gap, and the Regge residue that fixes $c_1$.
