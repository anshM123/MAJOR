\begin{abstract}
Must a three-dimensional CFT with large central charge and a large higher-spin gap
$\Delta_g$ be dual to Einstein gravity? From light-ray sum rules on $\langle TTTT\rangle$
we derive that, at leading order in $1/c_T$, given stated analytic inputs and positivity of
fixed functionals on heavy (and light spin-0, 2) states (in AdS no computed state is negative),
the $\langle TTT\rangle$ deviations obey $\sqrt{t_4^2+\alpha_T^2}\le\CubicHead$ at
$\Delta_g=\DgHead$, and an $R^4$ coefficient defined by the CFT obeys
$\tilde g_4\Lambda^6/8\pi G\le\QuartHead$ if no scalar lighter than $0.46\Lambda$ couples to
$TT$, up to one uncomputed universal constant.
\end{abstract}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{Introduction.}
A conformal field theory (CFT) with a large central charge $c_T$ and a large gap in its
spectrum of single-trace operators is expected to be dual to weakly coupled Einstein
gravity, with higher-derivative corrections suppressed by the gap~\cite{HPPS}. Turning
this expectation into inequalities with numbers tests holography from the CFT side and
gives sharp ``swampland'' constraints on gravitational effective field theories in anti-de
Sitter space. For the graviton cubic couplings the expectation has been made parametric:
causality of high-energy scattering~\cite{CEMZ} and of CFT correlators in the Regge
limit~\cite{AHKT,CHP,MeltzerPerlmutter,AKT} forces the non-Einstein three-point
couplings of the stress tensor to vanish as powers of $1/\Delta_g$, without explicit
constants. Averaged null energy is positive~\cite{HofmanMaldacena,HKT}, and the light-ray
operators that measure it commute at spacelike separation~\cite{KKSDZ,KKSDZlightray}; this
gives superconvergence sum rules on $\langle TTTT\rangle$ that force those couplings to
vanish at infinite gap~\cite{KKSDZ,BHM}.

Explicit constants exist in two neighbouring settings. In flat space, dispersive
sum rules bound graviton couplings sharply in terms of the mass $M$ of the lightest
higher-spin state~\cite{CaronHuotVanDuong,CHMRSswamp,BKZ,CHLPS,CHLPSpw}; Caron-Huot, Li,
Parra-Martinez and Simmons-Duffin (CHLPS) remark that their four-dimensional bound
translates to holographic CFT$_3$ (without a derivation)~\cite{CHLPS}, and record
explicit bounds on holographic central charges in higher dimensions~\cite{CHLPSpw}. In
AdS, dispersive CFT sum rules~\cite{CHMRSdisp} gave Caron-Huot, Mazáč, Rastelli and
Simmons-Duffin (CHMRS) explicit-constant bounds on scalar contact couplings, with an
infrared $\log\Delta_g$~\cite{CHMRS} (see also Ref.~\cite{Kundu}). Chang, Landau and
Simmons-Duffin (CLS-D) built the spinning sum rules on $\langle TTTT\rangle$ and listed
the steps still missing before graviton bounds can be derived in AdS~\cite{CLSD}.

We carry out those steps for AdS$_4$/CFT$_3$, \emph{conditionally} on positivity of the
functionals on heavy states. Our main result (item 1) bounds the parity-even and
parity-odd $\langle TTT\rangle$ deviations $t_4$, $\alpha_T$ with an explicit constant:
$\sqrt{t_4^2+\alpha_T^2}\le\CubicHead$ at $\Delta_g=\DgHead$. The secondary result
(item 2) is an upper bound on one $R^4$ graviton coefficient in AdS. To our knowledge
neither has been derived before from $\langle TTTT\rangle$. Both are implications, not
unconditional theorems. Positivity is certified in the flat limit for the functional of
item (1); in AdS it is supported by error-budgeted numerics on low-lying heavy states, and on
parts of the large-spin region it is reduced to conditional statements whose premises are
measured on one state or modelled (Fig.~\ref{fig:coverage}). Grades: [P] proved; [certified]
computer-assisted proof; [D] derived (written out, not independently checked);
[D-sketch] sketched; [DV] derived and checked numerically; [NE$\pm$] numerical with an
explicit error budget ($\times3$ safety factors on all model terms; not a bound);
[NE] numerical; [NE\,+\,M] numerical inside a model; [M] model; [A] assumption; [I] open.
``[X\,+\,Y]'' is X with one ingredient of grade Y, ``[D given X]'' is derived from inputs X,
and a chain carries its weakest grade (Supplemental Material~\cite{SM}, Sec.~S1).

\paragraph*{Why it matters.}
If positivity and the stated inputs hold, then at leading order in $1/c_T$ and up to the
constant $c_1$, every holographic CFT$_3$ with a higher-spin gap $\Delta_g=\DgHead$ has
stress-tensor three-point couplings within $\tau=\sqrt{t_4^2+\alpha_T^2}\le\CubicHead$ of their
Einstein values. Conformal-collider bounds allow $\tau\le4$ in every unitary
CFT$_3$~\cite{HofmanMaldacena,CDPrakash}; most of the factor $\sim10^8$ between the two is the
$\Lambda^{-4}$ scaling already implied by causality~\cite{CEMZ,AKT}. What is new is the
coefficient: in units of the gap $|\hat g_3|\Lambda^4\le\GthreeHead$, a number rather than a
scaling law. We do not bound the $1/c_T$ corrections, so at finite $c_T$ this does not yet
constrain $t_4$ at the $10^{-8}$ level. The method also reaches quartic order, where string
theory produces the first correction: the type~IIA value $2\zeta(3)$ for ABJM
theory~\cite{ABJM,BCP} lies below our upper end on the $R^4$ coefficient, a consistency check
against an independent string-theory computation (not a test of positivity).

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
\paragraph*{Setting.}
The CFT is unitary, parity-invariant or not, with a unique stress tensor $T$ and
$c_T=1$ for a free real scalar~\cite{OsbornPetkou}, so that $8\pi G=256/c_T$ in units of
the AdS radius. We assume
\begin{itemize}
\item[A1$'$] (higher-spin gap) every single-trace operator of spin $J\ge4$ in the
  $T\times T$ operator product expansion (OPE) has twist $\Delta-J\ge\Delta_g$; single
  traces of spin $0$, $2$ may be light;
\item[A2] (weak coupling below the gap) $c_T\to\infty$ at fixed $\Delta_g$: the OPE data of
  states with $\Delta-J<\Delta_g$ (other than light single traces) are those of a tree-level
  bulk effective theory up to $o(1/c_T)$, uniformly in spin. The sum rules are used at finite
  $c_T$; heavy states are never expanded in $1/c_T$.
\end{itemize}
Spins 1 and 3 never appear: identical conserved $T$'s in $d=3$ have no
$\langle TT\mathcal O_J\rangle$ structure for $J=1,3$~\cite{DKKPS}, and odd $J\ge5$ couple
only through a parity-odd structure. \emph{Heavy} states are all states with
$\Delta-J\ge\Delta_g$, single- or multi-trace. The gap scale is
$\Lambda:=\sqrt{(\Delta_g-2)(\Delta_g-1)}\simeq\Delta_g-\tfrac32$ (not a cosmological
constant), and a heavy state $(\Delta,J)$ has bulk mass $m$,
$m^2=(\Delta-J-2)(\Delta+J-1)$.

$\langle TTT\rangle$ is fixed by $c_T$ and two numbers~\cite{HofmanMaldacena,Buchel,CDPrakash}:
$t_4$, and $\alpha_T$, which multiplies the parity-odd structure that exists only in
$d=3$~\cite{GPY}. Positivity of energy flux confines them to the disk
$t_4^2+\alpha_T^2\le16$~\cite{HofmanMaldacena,CDPrakash}. In the bulk,
$\tau:=\sqrt{t_4^2+\alpha_T^2}=240|\hat g_3|$, with $\hat g_3$ the complex $R^3$ coupling
of Ref.~\cite{CHLPS} ([DV] for the $t_4$ part; [D] for the $\alpha_T$ part, whose sign
convention is not fixed; only the modulus enters). The quartic coupling $g_4$ is defined
by the flat-space amplitude $M(1^+2^-3^-4^+)=\langle23\rangle^4[14]^4f$,
$f=8\pi G/(stu)+\dots+g_4+g_5t+\dots$~\cite{CHLPS}, normalised so that type~II strings
have $g_4M^6/8\pi G=2\zeta(3)$, $M^2=4/\alpha'$~\cite{CHMRSswamp}. We bound only $g_4$
among the eight-derivative couplings~\cite{CGGHJM}.

\paragraph*{Mechanism.}
Light-transformed stress tensors (energy detectors at null infinity) commute when
spacelike separated. Integrating the vacuum expectation value of their double commutator
against a weight gives a sum rule~\cite{KKSDZ,CLSD}: if the weight decays fast enough in
the Regge limit (high energy at fixed momentum transfer; its Regge spin $k$ must exceed the
Regge intercept $J_0$ of the exact correlator, and $J_0\le1$ in every unitary
CFT~\cite{KKSDZ,CaronHuotInversion}), the total vanishes. We use it at finite $c_T$, as in
Refs.~\cite{CHMRS,CLSD}: only the light part is expanded in $1/c_T$, and the heavy part enters
only through its sign; the planar intercept ($J_0=2$ for graviton
exchange~\cite{KKSDZ}) never enters. Expanding in the $T\times T$ OPE splits the sum rule into
a light part (identity, $T$ and its multi-traces below the gap) and heavy states,
\begin{equation}
\sum_{\rm heavy}{\rm Tr}\big[P_{\mathcal O}\,M_\Omega(\mathcal O)\big]=-\,\Omega[\text{light}],
\label{eq:sumrule}
\end{equation}
where $P_{\mathcal O}\succeq0$ collects the squared OPE coefficients and
$M_\Omega(\mathcal O)$ is the functional $\Omega$ acting on the blocks of $\mathcal O$.
At leading order the light side is fixed by the bulk couplings: graviton exchange,
$|\hat g_3|^2$ and, at $k=4$, $g_4$. If $\Omega$ is \emph{positive} on every heavy state
(Pos), then $\Omega[\text{light}]\le0$ at every $c_T$, and its leading term in $1/c_T$ is the
bound. In the flat limit $\Lambda\to\infty$ the heavy action becomes the partial-wave
expansion of graviton scattering, and Pos becomes the positivity used in flat
space~\cite{CHLPS}. The difficulty in AdS is that curvature shifts every heavy state's action
away from its flat value, by amounts that are not small near the gap and at large impact
parameter.

Three ingredients make this work. (i) A dual tensor structure, $EE$, for which AdS
curvature produces no helicity mixing at leading Regge order [P]; with the standard
structure that mixing makes the functional negative at large impact parameter [M]. (ii)
\emph{De-shifting}: the weights are chosen so that, at leading Regge order, every heavy
state's action equals its flat value exactly (a generalised Abel equation); what is left
are beyond-leading-Regge corrections, which are what Pos must survive. (iii) The light
side at $k=4$, where the $R^4$ contact, which grows like the fourth power of the energy, sits
exactly at the convergence border of the $k=4$ sum rule: its
contribution is a finite Regge ``arc'', normalised by calibrating against a heavy scalar
that generates an $R^4$ contact when integrated out, $-\mathcal A_4[R^4]=2g_4$ [D].

\paragraph*{Functionals.}
$\Omega^{EE}$ (item 1) combines CLS-D's functional at Regge spins $k=2,3$ with
polynomial weights fixed by a linear program, the $EE$ structure and the de-shift;
$\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$ (item 2) adds de-shifted $k=4,5$ pieces. All weights are published as
explicit continuum functions that satisfy the de-shift identity for every heavy state, to
$\le9.1\times10^{-11}$ for the load-bearing $k=4$ pieces [certified] (Supplemental
Material~\cite{SM}). The interchange of the OPE sum and the integrals is absolutely
convergent for $J_0<\tfrac32$ ($k=2,3,5$) and $J_0<\tfrac52$ ($k=4$) [D], hence at every
finite $c_T$, with margins $\tfrac12$ and $\tfrac32$. The bounds are the $c_T\to\infty$ limit
of the resulting inequality: its heavy side is non-negative at every $c_T$ by (Pos), and its
light side is $O(1/c_T)$ (A2). Applied term by term to the $O(1/c_T)$ correlator, the same
count would need a planar intercept below $\tfrac32$, which fails; we never do this.

The light side of $\Omega^{EE}$ is the graviton pole, $16\pi G/(\nu^2+9/4)$ at celestial
frequency $\nu$ [D], a $|\hat g_3|^2$ term with coefficient $K=1+O(\Lambda^{-2})$ (the
shift is [DV] up to a $\pm0.02\%$ band [D-sketch\,+\,M]), and
a term $16\pi Gc_1t_4$. Here $c_1$ is one universal number fixed by $\langle TT\rangle$
and $\langle TTT\rangle$ alone [D-sketch]; it vanishes given one premise [A] and is
otherwise uncomputed. For $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$ the $R^4$ arc is integrable at coincident
celestial angles [D]: the light function grows like $\Delta\varphi^{-2}\log\Delta\varphi$,
below the threshold $\Delta\varphi^{-7/2}$. This rests on an analytic counting lemma; the
direct numerical scan is still pre-asymptotic (local slopes 2.5--2.8 against the
predicted 2). We identify $\tilde g_4:=-\frac12\mathcal A_4[\text{light}]$ with $g_4$ up to
$O(\Lambda^{-2})$ assuming, as CHMRS and CLS-D do~\cite{CHMRS,CLSD}, that light
double-trace data are those of a tree-level bulk effective theory [A].

Light spin-0, 2 single traces, allowed by A1$'$, enter at $k=2,3$ like heavy states
[D-sketch]. $\Omega^{EE}$ is positive on them at every mass in the flat limit [P], so
item (1) is unchanged and bounds their couplings too~\cite{CHLPS}. $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$ is
negative on scalars lighter than $0.4602\Lambda$ [P] (such scalars couple to $TT$ through
$\phi W^2$; cf.\ Ref.~\cite{CMT}); this is the origin of the condition in item (2a).

\paragraph*{Result} (conditional; implication derived at sketch level, [D-sketch]).
\emph{Assume A1$'$, A2, the inputs listed in the Supplemental Material~\cite{SM}, and
(Pos) for the functional used, on heavy states and on light spin-$0,2$ states. Then, at
leading order in $1/c_T$ and for every value of $c_1$:}

\emph{(1) Cubic.} With $L_A=\int_0^1\psi_2(p)\,dp/(p^2+\tfrac{9}{4\Lambda^2})$ ($\psi_2$
the published $k=2$ weight),
\begin{equation}
\tau\le\tau_0\big(x+\sqrt{1+x^2}\big),\qquad \tau_0=\frac{240}{\Lambda^4}\sqrt{\frac{16L_A}{K}},
\label{eq:cubic}
\end{equation}
\emph{with $x=19941|c_1|/(\Lambda^2\sqrt{16KL_A})$. At $K=1$, $\tau_0=2.89\times10^{-8}$
($|\hat g_3|\Lambda^4\le120$) and $x=1.67\times10^{-4}|c_1|$ at $\Delta_g=1000$;
$\tau_0=3.13\times10^{-6}$ at $\Delta_g=300$.}

\emph{(2a) Quartic. If $\tau=0$, or if a light-side coefficient $\kappa$ is positive
[D-sketch\,+\,M; flat value $0.1099$, corrections $O(\Lambda^{-2})$], and
if no scalar single trace with $m<0.4602\Lambda$ appears in $T\times T$,}
\begin{equation}
\frac{\tilde g_4\Lambda^6}{8\pi G}\le17.2407\ln\frac{2\Lambda}{3}+B_{\rm eff}(\Lambda)
+\frac{5.946\times10^4c_1^2}{\kappa\Lambda^4},
\label{eq:quartic}
\end{equation}
\emph{with $B_{\rm eff}=-19.265$ at $\Delta_g=1000$ and $-19.220$ at $300$ (it tends to the
flat $-19.278$ as $\Lambda\to\infty$), i.e.\ $92.81+5.4\times10^{-7}c_1^2$ at $1000$ and
$72.04+6.8\times10^{-5}c_1^2$ at $300$ (flat $\kappa$). A scalar below $0.4602\Lambda$ with
$\phi W^2$ coupling $g_0$ does not invalidate (2a); it adds
$(0.4304-2.0321\,m^2/\Lambda^2)|g_0|^2\Lambda^4/8\pi G$ to the right side.}

\emph{(2b) Without the scalar condition, using $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}+0.040\,\Omega^{EE}$ and
(Pos) also for $\Omega^{EE}$, the upper end is $164.51+1.03\times10^{-6}c_1^2$ at
$\Delta_g=1000$ ($125.71$ at 300).}

Both items are exact in $c_1$; at $\Delta_g=1000$ they move by less than $1\%$ unless
$|c_1|\gtrsim60$ (item 1), $1306$ (2a) or $1262$ (2b). Item (2a) uses only $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$ and
is independent of positivity of $\Omega^{EE}$; item (2b) is not. The weakest links of the
implication are the $\nu$-independence of the $c_1$ term and the action of light single
traces [D-sketch] (item 1); for item (2), the treatment of the $g_5$ light term, the
light single traces, the $0.040$ repair (2b) [D-sketch] and, if $\tau\ne0$, the sign of
$\kappa$ [D-sketch\,+\,M]. The constants are reproduced by independent code [DV], and so are,
from the written statements alone, the exact $EE$-kernel reduction behind the interchange and
the counting lemma behind the $R^4$ arc. A lower
bound on $\tilde g_4$ is not claimed: the functional that would give it has no positivity
evidence beyond leading Regge order. With the functionals fixed, the constants are
explicit functions of $\Lambda$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:bounds}); since positivity was examined
only at $\Delta_g=300$ and $1000$, other gaps are not claimed.

\paragraph*{ABJM consistency check.}
In the type-IIA 't~Hooft regime of ABJM theory~\cite{ABJM} the higher-spin gap is the
string scale, $\Lambda\leftrightarrow ML$ at leading order in $1/(ML)$: supergravity
fields have spin $\le2$, and the lightest single trace of spin $\ge4$ is a
first-massive-level string state. So A1$'$ and A2 hold at leading order [D-sketch].
Separately, through the orders computed (supergravity and $R^4$) only the stress-tensor
multiplet is exchanged in $T\times T$ at tree level~\cite{CPY,BCP}, so the scalar condition
of (2a) holds there [D-sketch], and the flat limit
gives $g_4M^6/8\pi G=2\zeta(3)$~\cite{CHMRSswamp,BCP}, $2.6\%$ of the upper end at
$\Delta_g=1000$. The upper end is dominated by the four-dimensional infrared logarithm
(the ten-dimensional window is $[0,3.000]$~\cite{CHMRSswamp}), and the identification
$\Lambda\leftrightarrow ML$ shifts the placement by $\approx3\%$, so this is a consistency
check at the one-digit level, not evidence for positivity.

\paragraph*{Discussion.}
Conditionally on positivity, a holographic CFT$_3$ with a large higher-spin gap has
$|\hat g_3|\Lambda^4\le\GthreeHead$ at $\Delta_g=\DgHead$, and, absent light scalars below
$0.46\Lambda$ in $T\times T$, $\tilde g_4\le\QuartHead\times8\pi G/\Lambda^6$, at leading
order in $1/c_T$: in units of the gap both couplings are at most $O(10^2)$, as expected when
higher-derivative terms are set by the gap. The bounds are not near-optimal. Item (1) is about
$10\times$ weaker in $|\hat g_3|$ ($\approx10^2\times$ in $|\hat g_3|^2$) than the transplanted
flat optimum~\cite{CHLPS} (Fig.~\ref{fig:bounds}), which uses forward and helicity-flip sum
rules not available here. The upper end on $\tilde g_4$ is dominated by the infrared
logarithm $17.24\ln(2\Lambda/3)$.
Compared with Refs.~\cite{KKSDZ,BHM,AKT}, which give vanishing at infinite gap or
parametric scaling, and with the flat-space and higher-dimensional
precedents~\cite{CHLPS,CHLPSpw}, the new elements are explicit constants derived from
$\langle TTTT\rangle$ in $d=3$, the parity-odd $\alpha_T$, and an AdS bound on a graviton
$R^4$ coefficient; supersymmetric localisation instead determines that coefficient in
ABJ(M) theory~\cite{BCP,BCJP}. Not claimed: an unconditional bound, unconditional positivity
at large spin, other gaps, the size of $1/c_T$ corrections, a lower end, bounds on $g_5$ or the
all-plus $R^4$ coefficient. The decisive next steps are the spinning $O(1/m^2)$ coefficients of
the heavy blocks (the main undetermined premise of both large-spin theorems), error budgets
for the remaining near-gap spins (including $345\le J<857$), positivity over a range of gaps,
the value of $c_1$, and a quartic functional built with light-scalar positivity. Whether the
flat-space core of the argument has a de Sitter analogue is open; it would need a positivity
principle there, which is not established.
