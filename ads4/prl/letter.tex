\begin{abstract}
How far does consistency of a three-dimensional CFT with large central charge and a
large higher-spin gap $\Delta_g$ force its dual to be Einstein gravity? From light-ray
sum rules on $\langle TTTT\rangle$ we derive, given stated analytic inputs and positivity
of fixed functionals on heavy states (tested numerically), $\sqrt{t_4^2+\alpha_T^2}\le
\CubicHead$ and $-O(\Lambda^{-2})\le g_4\Lambda^6/8\pi G\le \QuartHead$ for one $R^4$
coupling at $\Delta_g=\DgHead$ if no light scalar couples to $TT$ ($\QuartRep$
otherwise), up to one uncomputed universal constant, with explicit dependence on
$\Lambda\simeq\Delta_g-\tfrac32$.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph*{Introduction.}
A conformal field theory with large central charge $c_T$ and a large gap in its
single-trace spectrum is expected to have a weakly coupled gravitational dual whose
action is Einstein gravity plus higher-derivative terms suppressed by the
gap~\cite{HPPS}. We ask how far CFT consistency \emph{alone} forces this, quantitatively
and at finite gap, through quartic order in curvature.

For the stress tensor the expectation has been made parametric. Causality of
high-energy scattering~\cite{CEMZ} and Regge-limit causality of correlators of $T$
with probe operators~\cite{AHKT,CHP,MeltzerPerlmutter} force the non-Einstein
$\langle TTT\rangle$ structures to vanish as powers of $1/\Delta_g$; in three
dimensions $|t_4|,|\alpha_T|\lesssim\ln\Delta_g/\Delta_g^4$ with unspecified
constants~\cite{AKT,AHKTshock}. On $\langle TTTT\rangle$ itself, commutativity of
ANEC operators gives superconvergence sum rules that force these couplings to vanish
when $T$ is the only light single trace~\cite{KKSDZ,BHM}, with heavy contributions
argued to be suppressed by powers of $1/\Delta_g$~\cite{KKSDZ}. Dispersive CFT sum
rules~\cite{CHMRSdisp} turn flat-space bounds~\cite{CaronHuotVanDuong,CHMRSswamp}
into AdS bounds: for scalar correlators with graviton exchange, Caron-Huot, Mazáč,
Rastelli and Simmons-Duffin (CHMRS) obtained in AdS$_4$ a lower bound on the leading contact coupling and two-sided
bounds on the next, with explicit constants and an infrared $\log\Delta_g$~\cite{CHMRS}, and
Kundu derived positivity and log-convexity of scalar contacts $\phi^2\Box^k\phi^2$
from the Regge limit~\cite{Kundu}. Flat four-dimensional graviton bounds with an
infrared logarithm are in Refs.~\cite{CHLPS,CHLPSpw}; Bern, Kosmopoulos and
Zhiboedov bound the $R^3$ by the $R^4$ coefficient~\cite{BKZ}. Chang, Landau and
Simmons-Duffin (CLS-D) constructed spinning dispersive sum rules on
$\langle TTTT\rangle$ with their flat-space dictionary, and listed what remains
before graviton bounds can be lifted to AdS~\cite{CLSD}.

This Letter carries out those steps \emph{conditionally} in AdS$_4$/CFT$_3$ for the
cubic couplings $t_4$, $\alpha_T$ and one quartic ($R^4$) coupling. Relative to
Refs.~\cite{KKSDZ,BHM,AKT}, which give vanishing at infinite gap or parametric
scaling, the new elements are explicit constants at finite $\Delta_g$, the inclusion
of the parity-odd $\alpha_T$, and a bound on a graviton $R^4$ coefficient in AdS
rather than in flat space~\cite{CHMRSswamp,CHLPS,BKZ} or for scalar
contacts~\cite{CHMRS,Kundu}. The method is CLS-D's and CHMRS's. Our ingredients are a
dual tensor structure that removes a curvature-induced helicity mixing, explicit
de-shifted functionals, an analysis of the light side as a single functional, and
computations of the heavy-state action near the gap.

\emph{What is established.} The implication ``positivity of fixed functionals on heavy
states $\Rightarrow$ bounds'' is derived from a list of precisely stated inputs, graded
individually; its constants are reproduced independently. Positivity itself is
\emph{not} established: it is proved in the flat limit, error-bounded numerically on
some states, and elsewhere computed on finite sets inside a model; the functionals were
chosen with knowledge of failing states. Grades: [P] proved, [certified]
computer-assisted proof, [D] derived, [D-sketch] sketched, [DV] derived and checked
numerically, [Q] quoted, [NE$\pm$] numerical evidence with an explicit error budget,
[NE] numerical evidence, [M] model, [A] assumption, [I] open; ``[D given X]'' is derived
from inputs X, and [NE\,+\,M] is numerical evidence obtained inside a model. A chain
carries the grade of its weakest link.

\paragraph*{Setting.}
$\mathcal T$ is a unitary CFT$_3$, parity-even or not, with a unique stress tensor $T$;
$c_T=1$ for a free real scalar~\cite{OsbornPetkou}, and $8\pi G:=256/c_T$ in AdS units.
The standing assumptions are
\begin{itemize}
\item[A1$'$] (higher-spin gap) every single-trace primary of spin $J\ge4$ in the
  $T\times T$ OPE has twist $\Delta-J\ge\Delta_g$~\cite{CEMZ,CHLPS}; single traces of
  spin $0$ and $2$ may have any dimension, and spins $1$, $3$ do not occur in $T\times T$
  (at tree level only $T\times T$ enters $\langle TTTT\rangle$);
\item[A2] tree level: $O(1/c_T)$ with $c_T\gg\Delta_g^2$.
\end{itemize}
Bounds refer to $c_T\to\infty$ at fixed $\Delta_g$. Heavy states $(\Delta,J)$ have
$m^2=(\Delta-J-2)(\Delta+J-1)$, and the gap scale is
$\Lambda:=\sqrt{(\Delta_g-2)(\Delta_g-1)}=\Delta_g-\tfrac32+O(\Delta_g^{-1})$
($298.50$, $998.50$ at $\Delta_g=300$, $1000$).

$\langle TTT\rangle$ is parametrised by $(c_T,t_4,\alpha_T)$~\cite{HofmanMaldacena,Buchel,CDPrakash},
$\alpha_T$ multiplying the parity-odd structure that exists only in
$d=3$~\cite{GPY}; $t_4$, $\alpha_T$ are dimensionless, and in the bulk
$R:=\sqrt{t_4^2+\alpha_T^2}=240|\hat g_3|$ with $\hat g_3$ the $R^3$ coupling of
Ref.~\cite{CHLPS}. The quartic coupling $g_4$ is defined by the flat amplitude
$M(1^+2^-3^-4^+)=\langle23\rangle^4[14]^4f(s,u)$,
$f=8\pi G/(stu)+\dots+g_4+g_5t+\dots$~\cite{CHLPS}, with light spin-$0,2$ fields kept
in the amplitude, normalised so that type~II strings give $g_4M^6/8\pi G=2\zeta(3)$,
$M^2=4/\alpha'$~\cite{CHMRSswamp}. Of the eight-derivative four-graviton couplings
(classified in Ref.~\cite{CGGHJM}) we bound only $g_4$: the complex all-plus
coefficient $\hat g_4$ is invisible to our functional [D].

\paragraph*{Sum rules and functionals.}
CLS-D's functional $\Psi_{k,\nu}$ integrates the double commutator of two
light-transformed stress tensors against a dual three-point structure, at Regge spin
$k$ and celestial frequency $\nu$, over real Lorentzian points~\cite{CLSD}. For $k$
above the Regge intercept $J_0\le1$~\cite{KKSDZ,CaronHuotInversion} it annihilates the
correlator. Splitting the $s$-channel OPE into light (identity, $T$, $[TT]$ double
traces) and heavy ($\Delta-J\ge\Delta_g$) operators (light spin-$0,2$ single traces are
treated below),
\begin{equation}
\sum_{\rm heavy}{\rm Tr}\big[P_{\mathcal O}\,M_\Omega(\mathcal O)\big]=-\,\Omega[\text{light}],
\label{eq:sumrule}
\end{equation}
where $P_{\mathcal O}\succeq0$ is the matrix of OPE coefficients and
$M_\Omega(\mathcal O)$ the Hermitian action of $\Omega$ on the blocks of
$\mathcal O$ in the helicity--parity basis. \textbf{Positivity} (Pos$_\Omega$) is the
statement $M_\Omega(\mathcal O)\succeq0$ for every heavy primary; we quote its smallest
eigenvalue in units of the state's flat normalisation.

\emph{Cubic functional $\Omega^{EE}$.} At $k=2,3$ we use the dual structure
$EE=[V_{0,42}^2(H_{24}-2V_{2,04}V_{4,02})/(X_{20}X_{40})]^2$, whose leading-Regge
heavy action is helicity-diagonal [P]. We \emph{de-shift}: the heavy image of
$\Psi_{2,\nu}$ is a known kernel times a Regge harmonic, and we invert that kernel (a
generalised Abel equation, minimal-norm solution) so that the leading-Regge image of
every heavy state equals its flat-space value (the \emph{de-shift identity}) and the bulk-point
limit of
$\Omega^{EE}=\int_0^1\!dp\,[\psi_2\Lambda^4\hat C'_{EE,\Lambda p}+\psi_3\tfrac12\Lambda^6\hat C_{3,\Lambda p}]$
is a fixed flat functional with polynomial weights $\psi_{2,3}$.

\emph{Quartic functionals.} $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$ realises a fixed flat functional (nominal
margin $\delta=0.2$, log coefficient $17.2407$) by de-shifted $k=4,5$ pieces plus $k=2,3$
parts; $\Omega^Q_{\rm low}$ is the de-shifted $k=4$ value piece, whose leading-Regge
heavy image $P_{-1/2}(\cosh\beta)$ is positive.

\emph{Cutoff and data.} Every de-shift weight $\chi$ is multiplied by an entire
erf plateau $\theta(\nu)$. This changes no computed number (relative effect
$<10^{-40}$) but is necessary: a weight of compact $\nu$-support, such as any grid
weight, regrows at the raw Regge rate by Paley--Wiener [P]. All de-shift weights are
published as explicit continuum functions that satisfy the de-shift identity for
\emph{all} heavy states: to $\le2.7\times10^{-13}$ and $\le9.1\times10^{-11}$ for the
$k=4$ pieces [certified], $2.4\times10^{-7}$ for $k=5$, and $\le1.1\times10^{-9}$
relative for $k=2$ [DV], together with the flat weights and kernels (Supplemental
Material).

\paragraph*{Light side.}
For $\Omega^{EE}$ every $[TT]$ double trace sits at a double zero and contacts drop
out [D]; the light side is the identity plus the $T$ block, with the exact graviton
pole $-\hat C'_{EE,\nu}|_E=16\pi G/(\nu^2+9/4)$ [D].

For $k=4$ the light side is the value of the \emph{same} term-wise $s$-channel
functional that acts on the heavy blocks~\cite{CLSD}. In Mellin space a contour
deformation splits it, on every tree term $X$, into the fixed-angle integral and a
$u$-channel Regge arc, $\Omega_4[X]=D_{\rm LR}[X]+\mathcal A_4[X]$ [D]. The arc
vanishes when $X$ has Regge spin below $4$ and otherwise depends only on $X$'s top
Regge coefficient [D; DV]. The fixed-angle part vanishes for every tree term with
intercept $J_0<4$ (Einstein, $E\times W^3$, mean field theory; Lemma A), because the
swapped integrand is one single-valued analytic function on the region swept by the
contour deformation (Lemma C$'$, [D]; there the $s$-channel OPE converges). Lemma A is
[D given C$'$]; for the Einstein term it is derived but not evaluated numerically.

The $R^4$ contact sits exactly at the borderline. Its normalisation follows from the
sum rule itself: a heavy scalar coupled to $W^2$ has Regge spin $3$, so its exchange
has zero total value; hence the arc of the $R^4$ contact it generates equals minus the
heavy block's value, which is flat-normalised. This gives
$-\mathcal A_4[R^4]=2g_4$, and zero for $\hat g_4$ [D given Lemma A, $H_{\rm EFT}$(a)
and the heavy dictionary]. Its light function grows only like
$\Delta\varphi^{-2}\log\Delta\varphi$ at coincident celestial angles, because the
collinear variable enters every term of a spinning contact
diagram~\cite{Paulos,CGPspin,PenedonesMellin} only through one cross-distance; this
pairs absolutely with the celestial kernel (growth below $\Delta\varphi^{-7/2}$
suffices) [D]. Defining $\tilde g_4:=-\tfrac12\mathcal A_4[\text{light}]$,
$\tilde g_4=g_4+O(\Lambda^{-2})$ given the standard assumption $H_{\rm EFT}$(b), made
also by CHMRS and CLS-D~\cite{CHMRS,CLSD}, that light double-trace data are those of a
tree-level bulk EFT.

\emph{Light single traces} (A1$'$). In the helicity-preserving sector the functionals
see, a light scalar exchange has Regge spin $3$ and a light spin-2 exchange spin $4$; the
crossed-channel spin-2 pole is helicity-flipping and annihilated by $EE$. So light
scalars drop out of the $k=4,5$ sum rules and light spin-2 states out of $k=5$, with
their own double traces [D; DV]; at $k=2,3$ they act like heavy states [D-sketch]. In the
flat limit $\Omega^{EE}$ is positive on all of them at every mass [P], so item (1) below
holds unchanged and in addition bounds their couplings to two gravitons, as in flat
space~\cite{CHLPS}. $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$ is positive on light spin-2 states but negative on
scalars lighter than $0.46\Lambda$ [P]; for those we use $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}+0.04\,\Omega^{EE}$
[D-sketch].

The $O(t_4)$ light term of $\Omega^{EE}$ (from one Einstein and one $W^3$ vertex) is
$16\pi Gc_1t_4$ with $c_1$ one universal number fixed by $\langle TT\rangle$ and
$\langle TTT\rangle$ data alone [D]: its shock-wave ($u$-channel) part vanishes
identically in $d=3$ [P], its $k=3$ part vanishes [D], and the rest is an
energy-channel $T$ exchange (checked against Ref.~\cite{JJMNS}), $\nu$-independent
[D given $J_0(E\times W^3)=2$, D-sketch]. It vanishes [D-sketch] given one premise [A]:
that the $k=2$ functional sees this exchange only through light-ray one-point matrices,
as is derived for the $k=3$ one. Otherwise it is uncomputed. The same $c_1$ enters $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$.

\paragraph*{Result.}
\textbf{Theorem (conditional).} \emph{Assume A1$'$, A2, inputs I1--I6 below, and
(Pos$_\Omega$) for $\Omega^{EE}$ [item (1)] or for $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$,
$\Omega^Q_{\rm low}$ [item (2)], on heavy states and on light spin-$0,2$ states at the
$k$ where they enter. Then, for every value of $c_1$:}

\emph{(1) Cubic.} With $L_A=\int_0^1\psi_2(p)\,dp/(p^2+\tfrac{9}{4\Lambda^2})$,
\begin{gather}
R\le R_0\big(x+\sqrt{1+x^2}\big),\label{eq:cubic}\\
R_0=\frac{240}{\Lambda^4}\sqrt{\frac{16L_A}{K}},\qquad
x=\frac{19941\,|c_1|}{\Lambda^2\sqrt{16KL_A}},\nonumber
\end{gather}
\emph{where $K=1+O(\Lambda^{-2})$ is the $|\hat g_3|^2$ light coefficient. At $K=1$,
$R_0=3.13\times10^{-6}$ ($\Delta_g=300$) and $2.89\times10^{-8}$ ($1000$), and
$x=2.16\times10^{-3}|c_1|$ / $1.67\times10^{-4}|c_1|$.}

\emph{(2) Quartic. If $t_4=\alpha_T=0$, or if $\kappa>0$ (upper end) and $c_L>0$
(lower end), and if no scalar lighter than $0.46\Lambda$ couples to $TT$, then}
\begin{align}
-O(\Lambda^{-2})\le\frac{\tilde g_4\Lambda^6}{8\pi G}&\le
17.2407\ln\frac{2\Lambda}{3}+B_{\rm eff}\nonumber\\
&\quad+\frac{5.946\times10^4\,c_1^2}{\kappa\,\Lambda^4},
\label{eq:quartic}
\end{align}
\emph{with $B_{\rm eff}=-19.220$ ($300$), $-19.265$ ($1000$), i.e.\ upper end
$72.04$ / $92.81$ at $c_1=0$. With the flat value $\kappa=0.1099$ the last term is
$6.8\times10^{-5}c_1^2$ / $5.4\times10^{-7}c_1^2$; it is absent if $t_4=\alpha_T=0$.
Without the condition on light scalars, and with (Pos) also for $\Omega^{EE}$, the
upper end is $125.71+1.29\times10^{-4}c_1^2$ / $164.51+1.03\times10^{-6}c_1^2$. The
lower end has no computed constant.}

The $c_1$ terms are exact: (1) is the root of a quadratic inequality in $(t_4,\alpha_T)$,
and (2) is the supremum over unknown $(t_4,\alpha_T)$ of
$2L_{\rm AdS}-\kappa R^2\Lambda^8/57600+2.032\,c_1t_4\Lambda^2$, so item (2) needs
nothing from item (1) unless light scalars couple. The bounds move by less than $1\%$ for
$|c_1|<4.6$ / $60$ (item 1) and $|c_1|<99$ / $1262$ (item 2, either form), against a natural size
$10^{-3}$--$10^{-1}$ [M]. A possible $O(\Lambda^{-2})$ difference between the $t_4^2$
and $\alpha_T^2$ light coefficients (H17, modelled as a $\pm0.24\%$ / $\pm0.021\%$ band
[M]) would make (1) a bound on a weighted norm.

\emph{Net grades.} Implication (1): [D-sketch], through the $\nu$-independence of the
$c_1$ term and, for light single traces, their $k=2,3$ action; constants [DV].
Implication (2): [D-sketch] in the $g_5$ term (I4), the light single traces and, if
light scalars couple, the $0.04\,\Omega^{EE}$ repair; [D-sketch\,+\,M] in the signs (I6)
if $\hat g_3\ne0$; [A] through $H_{\rm EFT}$(b) for $\tilde g_4=g_4$. No input is open.
Positivity: not established.

\paragraph*{Positivity.}
In the flat limit both functionals are positive on every heavy state, by an
interval-arithmetic proof: $\ge N/5$ for $\Omega^{EE}$ (attained to $10^{-8}$), and
$\ge0.18N$ for $\Omega^Q_{\rm up}$ on all but two regions checked in floating point
(Table~\ref{tab:pos}). For $\Omega^{EE}$ the minimum is the parity-odd $J=5$ state at
the lowest allowed mass, $+0.2038$ ($\Delta_g=300$) and $+0.2009$ ($1000$). At 1000 we
bound every AdS correction to it with factor-3 safety margins on all model inputs:
$\lambda_{\min}\ge+0.198$, i.e.\ the margin is $78$ times the error bound [NE$\pm$]
($\ge+0.146$, $3.5$ times, at 300). The same holds near the gap for $J=0$, $2$, $4$,
$6$ (and $J=8$ on a weaker budget), and for masses $m\ge1.3\Lambda$ at impact parameter
$b=(2J+1)\Lambda/m<5$, where an elementary Mehler--Dirichlet bound controls the
curvature [P] and an analytic $1/\Delta$ expansion the remaining corrections; there is
no mass above which all spins are covered. Other near-gap spins\HS, large impact
parameter and large spin are computed on finite sets inside a model and assumed in
between. % HIGHSPIN: near-gap J>=12 grade at 1000
The structure $EE$ and the gauge of the Abel solution were
chosen \emph{after} heavy-state failures had been seen; in the canonical gauge the
$J=24$ state at $\Delta_g=300$ fails, and $J=18$, $20$, $24$, $28$ at $300$ are
in-sample. At $\Delta_g=1000$ large near-gap spins rest on $H_{\rm LJ}(1000)$, whose
sign-free form is violated at $(\Delta,J)=(1100,100)$ and which holds only in a
refined form [D-sketch\,+\,M]\HS. % HIGHSPIN: H_LJ(1000) grade
\HLJstatus

For $\Omega^Q$ the binding pair (the states attaining the minimum) is the $++$ and
parity-odd $J=0$ states at the gap.
Its margin is controlled by $s_d$, the coefficient of the beyond-leading-Regge
correction to the derivative piece: $s_d=+0.5096$ / $+0.5077$ against a failure point
$1.776$; unmeasured channels need $\ge19\times$ the measured $|s_d|$ to fail. $s_d$
comes from an analytic $1/\Delta$ expansion of the integral over the real section cut
at the light cones of the shadow point. This region is exactly the causal domain over
which CLS-D define the functional~\cite{CLSD} [P], so the expansion equals the physical
integral up to $O(\Delta^Ne^{-0.336\Delta})$ [D, one step D-sketch]; for the scalar integrand the
full integral matches the exact Mellin result to $10^{-8}$ even at $\Delta=20$ [DV].

\emph{Reproduction.} Fresh code reproduces the bound constants, the $\Lambda$-forms, the
binding values of both functionals at the level of the model, and, by direct
seven-dimensional quadrature at finite $\Delta$, $s_d=+0.50967$ / $+0.50776$; the
de-shift weights were re-solved with independent code. Not reproduced: the beyond-Regge
correction to $\Omega^{EE}$'s binding state and the full $\Omega^Q$ state scan.

\paragraph*{Dependence on the gap.}
With the functionals fixed, the light side is an exact function of $\Lambda$ [DV]:
\begin{align}
R_0&=\frac{1.3194\times10^4}{\Lambda^4}\nonumber\\
&\quad\times\sqrt{\ln\frac{\Lambda}{8.7415}+\frac{6.384}{\Lambda}+O\Big(\frac{\ln\Lambda}{\Lambda^2}\Big)},
\label{eq:cubicL}\\
2L_{\rm AdS}&=17.2407\ln\tfrac{2\Lambda}{3}-19.2782+\frac{10.314}{\Lambda}\nonumber\\
&\quad+\frac{860.3\ln\frac{2\Lambda}{3}-2508.6}{\Lambda^2}+\dots
\label{eq:quarticL}
\end{align}
accurate to $5\times10^{-5}$ relative and $10^{-3}$ absolute at $\Delta_g=300$, $1000$
(the quartic form truncated at $1/\Lambda$ errs on the unsafe side). $R_0$ scales as
$\sqrt{\ln\Lambda}/\Lambda^4$; Ref.~\cite{AKT} gives $\ln\Delta_g/\Delta_g^4$ without
constants, so no numerical comparison is possible. These are values of the
implication; only $\Delta_g=300$, $1000$ were computed. \emph{Corollary}
[D-sketch\,+\,M]: if (Pos) holds at $\Delta_g=300$ (including the open window above),
and if at fixed $(J,m/\Lambda)$ the beyond-Regge corrections grow at most like $m$ and
are bounded uniformly in $\Delta_g$ in the Regge window [M], with continuity in $m$
[NE] and $H_{\rm LJ}(\Delta_g)$, then Eqs.~\eqref{eq:cubicL}--\eqref{eq:quarticL} are
bounds for every $\Delta_g\ge300$. Nothing is claimed below $300$.

\paragraph*{ABJM consistency check.}
In the type-IIA 't~Hooft regime of ABJM theory~\cite{ABJM} the higher-spin gap is the
string scale, $\Lambda\leftrightarrow ML$ with $M^2=4/\alpha'$ at leading order in
$1/(ML)$, and $T\times T$ contains no light single traces at tree level (only the
stress-tensor multiplet is exchanged~\cite{CPY,BCPW}), so A1$'$ and A2 hold at leading
order in $1/\lambda$ [D-sketch]. The flat limit of $\langle TTTT\rangle$ is the
ten-dimensional amplitude at zero internal momentum, so
$g_4M^6/8\pi G=2\zeta(3)$~\cite{CHMRSswamp,BCPW}. This lies at $2.6\%$ of the upper end
at $\Delta_g=1000$ ($3.3\%$ at 300), meaningful to one digit. It is a consistency check
of a theory inside our assumptions, not evidence for positivity, which is not shown for
string states; the bound is loose there because of the four-dimensional infrared
logarithm (against the ten-dimensional window $0\le g_4M^6/8\pi G\le3.000$ of
Ref.~\cite{CHMRSswamp}, $2\zeta(3)$ sits at $80\%$).

\paragraph*{Discussion.}
Conditionally on positivity and on inputs I1--I6, the cubic couplings and one quartic
coupling of a holographic CFT$_3$ with a large higher-spin gap are bounded with explicit
constants: the $\langle TTT\rangle$ deviations by
$1.32\times10^4\sqrt{\ln(\Lambda/8.74)}/\Lambda^4$, and the $R^4$ coefficient $g_4$ to the
window between $-O(\Lambda^{-2})$ and $17.24\ln(\Lambda/4.59)+O(1/\Lambda)$ in units of
$8\pi G/\Lambda^6$ (absent light scalars). In gap units,
$|\hat g_3|\Lambda^4\le104$ ($\Delta_g=300$) and $120$ ($1000$): both couplings are at
most $O(10^2)$ in units of the gap and grow only logarithmically with it, the scaling
expected when higher-derivative terms are set by the gap. Rewritten as
$|\hat g_3|^2\Lambda^8\le3022.34\ln(2\Lambda/3)-5327.21+O(1/\Lambda)$, item (1) is
about ten times weaker than the transplanted flat optimum of Ref.~\cite{CHLPS}, which
would need forward and helicity-flip sum rules not available in AdS.

To our knowledge no explicit-constant, finite-gap bound on $t_4$, $\alpha_T$ or a
graviton $R^4$ coefficient has previously been derived from $\langle TTTT\rangle$:
the superconvergence sum rules of Refs.~\cite{KKSDZ,BHM} give vanishing at infinite
gap with parametric suppression, the bounds of Ref.~\cite{AKT} are parametric and
come from other correlators, and Kundu's constraints concern scalar
contacts~\cite{Kundu}. In ABJ(M) theory the $R^4$ term is determined rather than
bounded~\cite{CPY,BCPW}, and the $\mathcal N=6$ bootstrap gives rigorous islands for
those theories~\cite{BCJP}; our bounds need no supersymmetry but are conditional.

Not claimed: an unconditional bound; any $\Delta_g$ other than $300$, $1000$; loop
level; bounds on $\hat g_4$, $g_5$ or the ratio $|\hat g_3|^2/g_4$; a lower constant.
The decisive next steps are certified positivity at one $\Delta_g$ (large-spin
asymptotics plus a compact interval computation), the value of $c_1$, and a
quartic functional optimised with light-state positivity imposed.
