David Simmons-Duffin, Simon Caron-Huot, Cyuan-Han Chang, Yakov Landau, Julio Parra-Martinez, Yue-Zhou Li, Dalimil Mazáč,
Leonardo Rastelli (CLS-D 2311.04271, CHLPS 2201.06602 / 2205.01495, CHMRS 2106.10274). CLS-D listed the steps that the Letter
carries out, and a follow-up by that group would be the most direct competitor. An INSPIRE search of the papers citing CLS-D
(15 records, 25 Nov 2026) found **no** follow-up on AdS graviton bounds by them, so none is identifiable as unfinished competing
work. They are not suggested only so that the suggestion list does not consist of the authors whose programme the Letter
completes; the editors are likely to consult some of them anyway, which is appropriate.
